70.2 Complete with the same

Use: age, colour, problem, street, time.

  1. I live in North Street and you live in North Street. We live in the same street.
  2. I arrived at 8.30 and you arrived at 8.30. We arrived at _____.
  3. James is 25 and Sue is 25. James and Sue are _____.
  4. My shirt is dark blue and so is my jacket. My shirt and jacket are _____.
  5. I have no money and you have no money. We have _____.
