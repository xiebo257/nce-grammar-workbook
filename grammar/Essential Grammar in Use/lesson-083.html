Lesson 83: a lot/much/many

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 83. Your answers are saved in this browser.

83.1 Write much or many

  1. Did you buy much food?
  2. There aren't _____ hotels in this town.
  3. We don't have _____ petrol. We need to stop and get some.
  4. Were there _____ people on the train?
  5. Did _____ students fail the exam?
  6. Paula doesn't have _____ money.
  7. I wasn't hungry, so I didn't eat _____.
  8. I don't know where Gary lives these days. I haven't seen him for _____ years.
  9. _____ people are coming to the party?
  10. _____ milk do you want in your coffee?
  11. _____ bread did you buy?
  12. _____ players are there in a football team?

83.2 Complete with much or many

Use: books, countries, luggage, people, time, times.

  1. I don't read very much. I don't have many books.
  2. Hurry up! We don't have _____.
  3. Do you travel a lot? Have you been to _____?
  4. Tina hasn't lived here very long, so she doesn't know _____.
  5. 'Do you have _____?' 'No, only this bag.'
  6. I know Tokyo well. I've been there _____.

83.3 Complete with a lot of

Use: accidents, books, fun, interesting things, traffic.

  1. I like reading. I have a lot of books.
  2. We enjoyed our visit to the museum. We saw _____.
  3. This road is very dangerous. There are _____.
  4. We enjoyed our holiday. We had _____.
  5. It took me a long time to drive here. There was _____.

83.4 Is much natural?

Change the sentence or write OK.

  1. Do you drink much coffee? OK
  2. I drink much tea. a lot of tea
  3. It was a cold winter. We had much snow.
  4. There wasn't much snow last winter.
  5. It costs much money to travel around the world.
  6. We had a cheap holiday. It didn't cost much.
  7. Do you know much about computers?
  8. 'Do you have any luggage?' 'Yes, much.'

83.5 Write sentences using much and a lot

  1. James loves films. (go to the cinema) He goes to the cinema a lot.
  2. Nicola thinks TV is boring. (watch TV) She doesn't watch TV much.
  3. Tina is a good tennis player. (play tennis)
  4. Mark doesn't like driving. (use his car)
  5. Paul spends most of the time at home. (go out)
  6. Sue has been all over the world. (travel)
