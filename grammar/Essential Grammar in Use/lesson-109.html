109.1 Complete the sentences about the people

They are arranged in two rows: Alan - Becky - Carl standing behind Daniela - Emma - Frank, who are sitting.

  1. Carl is standing behind Frank.
  2. Frank is sitting _____ Emma.
  3. Emma is sitting _____ Becky.
  4. Emma is sitting _____ Daniela and Frank.
  5. Daniela is sitting _____ Emma.
  6. Frank is sitting _____ Carl.
  7. Alan is standing _____ Daniela.
  8. Alan is standing _____ left.
  9. Becky is standing _____ middle.

109.2 Complete the sentences from the scene descriptions

  1. (cat beneath table) The cat is under the table.
  2. (tree at the rear of house) There is a big tree _____ the house.
  3. (plane higher than clouds) The plane is flying _____ the clouds.
  4. (woman before piano) She is standing _____ the piano.
  5. (cinema on right-hand side) The cinema is _____ the right.
  6. (woman beside fridge) She's standing _____ the fridge.
  7. (switch lower than window) The switch is _____ the window.
  8. (cupboard higher than sink) The cupboard is _____ the sink.
  9. (shoes beneath bed) There are some shoes _____ the bed.
  10. (plant beside piano) The plant is _____ the piano.
  11. (Paul faces Fiona from the other side) Paul is sitting _____ Fiona.
  12. (British traffic uses left-hand side) In Britain people drive _____ the left.
