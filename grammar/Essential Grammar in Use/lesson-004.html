Lesson 4: are you doing? (present continuous questions)

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 4. Your answers are saved in this browser.

4.1 Write the questions

  1. (you / watch / it?) Are you watching it?
  2. (you / go / now?)
  3. (it / rain?)
  4. (you / enjoy / the film?)
  5. (that clock / work?)
  6. (you / wait / for a bus?)

4.2 Complete the questions

Use: cry, eat, go, laugh, look at, read.

  1. What are you reading?
  2. Where she ?
  3. What they ?
  4. Why he ?
  5. What you at?
  6. Why she ?

4.3 Make questions

  1. (is / working / Ben / today) Is Ben working today?
  2. (what / the children / are / doing) What are the children doing?
  3. (you / are / listening / to me)
  4. (where / your friends / are / going)
  5. (are / watching / your parents / TV)
  6. (what / Jessica / is / cooking)
  7. (why / you / are / looking / at me)
  8. (is / coming / the bus)

4.4 Write short answers

  1. Are you watching TV? No, I'm not.
  2. Are you wearing a watch?
  3. Are you eating something?
  4. Is it raining?
  5. Are you sitting on the floor?
  6. Are you feeling well?
