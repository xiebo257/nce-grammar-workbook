Lesson 74: this/that/these/those

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 74. Your answers are saved in this browser.

74.1 Complete the sentences

Use this/that/these/those + birds, house, plates, postcards, seat, shoes.

  1. (shoes, near) Do you like these shoes?
  2. (house, far) Who lives in _____?
  3. (postcards, near) How much are _____?
  4. (birds, far) Look at _____.
  5. (seat, near) Excuse me, is _____ free?
  6. (plates, near) _____ are dirty.

74.2 Write questions

Use Is this/that your ...? or Are these/those your ...?

  1. (bag, near) Is this your bag?
  2. (umbrella, far)
  3. (book, near)
  4. (books, far)
  5. (bicycle, far)
  6. (keys, near)
  7. (keys, far)
  8. (watch, near)
  9. (glasses, far)
  10. (gloves, near)

74.3 Complete with this is, that's, or that

  1. A: I'm sorry I'm late. B: That's all right.
  2. A: I can't come to the party tomorrow. B: Oh, _____ a pity. Why not?
  3. On the phone - Sue: Hello, Jane. _____ Sue.
  4. A: You're lazy. B: _____ not true!
  5. A: Beth plays the piano very well. B: Does she? I didn't know _____.
  6. Paul: Mark, _____ my sister, Helen. Mark: Hi, Helen.
  7. A: I'm sorry I was angry yesterday. B: _____ OK. Forget it!
  8. A: You're a friend of Tom's, aren't you? B: Yes, _____ right.
