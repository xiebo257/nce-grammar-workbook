16.2 Complete the sentences

Use already + present perfect.

  1. What time is Paul arriving? He's already arrived.
  2. Do your friends want to see the film? No, they _____ it.
  3. Don't forget to phone Tom. I _____.
  4. When is Mark going away? He _____.
  5. Do you want to read the newspaper? I _____.
  6. When does Sarah start her new job? She _____.

16.3 Write with just or a negative with yet

Prompts: (she / go / out), (the bus / go), (the train / leave), (he / open / it), (they / finish / their dinner), (it / stop / raining).

  1. (she / go / out) She hasn't gone out yet.
  2. (the bus / go) The bus _____.
  3. (the train / leave) The train _____.
  4. (he / open / it) He _____.
  5. (they / finish / their dinner) They _____.
  6. (it / stop / raining) It _____.
