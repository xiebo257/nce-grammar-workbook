## Key Takeaway

- Use subject pronouns before the verb: `She knows Daniel.`
- Use object pronouns after a verb or preposition: `Daniel knows her`; `with me`.
- Use possessive adjectives before nouns: `my desk`, `her cat`, `its paws`.
- Use possessive pronouns without a noun: `The desk is mine`; `The suitcase is hers`.
- Use reflexive pronouns when the subject and object are the same person: `We enjoyed ourselves`.
- Use `each other` for a mutual action: `They help each other`.
- Use `by myself` to mean “alone.”
- Remember that `its` means possession, while `it's` means `it is`.
