14.1 Question: 14.1. Complete with an object pronoun: I met Daniel at the station, and he invited _____ to have coffee.
14.1 Answer: me
14.2 Question: 14.2. Complete with its or it's: The library is known for _____ quiet reading room.
14.2 Answer: its
14.3 Question: 14.3. Complete with a possessive pronoun: This scarf belongs to Maya, so it is _____.
14.3 Answer: hers
14.4 Question: 14.4. Correct the sentence: Leo sent we the meeting details yesterday.
14.4 Answer: Leo sent us the meeting details yesterday.
14.5 Question: 14.5. Correct the sentence: The gallery is famous for it's collection of maps.
14.5 Answer: The gallery is famous for its collection of maps.
14.6 Question: 14.6. Correct the sentence: I had dinner with a friend of me after work.
14.6 Answer: I had dinner with a friend of mine after work.
14.7 Question: 14.7. 他们邀请我和我的朋友去他们家。
14.7 Answer: They invited me and friend of mine to their house.
14.8 Question: 14.8. 这是你的雨伞吗？不，是我的。
14.8 Answer: Is this your umbrella? No, it's mine.
14.9 Question: 14.9. 她独自完成了这项工作，但她没有伤到自己。
14.9 Answer: She finished this work by herself, but she didn't hurt herself.
14.10 Question: 14.10. Complete both with him or himself, then explain who the pronoun refers to. A. I saw Ben in the hallway and waved to _____. (I waved to Ben) / B. Ben looked in the mirror and saw _____. (Ben saw Ben)
14.10 Answer: him (I waved to Ben)
himself (Ben saw Ben)
14.11 Question: 14.11. Complete both with each other or themselves, then explain the difference. A. The two teammates congratulated _____. (one congratulated the other) / B. The two teammates photographed _____. (each person photographed that same person)
14.11 Answer: each other. (one congratulated the other)
themselves (each person photographed that same person)
14.12 Question: 14.12. Complete both pairs, then explain why the form changes before and after a noun. A. This is _____ notebook; that one is _____. (I) / B. Those are _____ keys; these are _____. (they)
14.12 Answer: my mine 拥有权; 代词,我的
their theirs 拥有权; 代词,他们的
14.13 Question: 14.13. Write a sentence about someone you spoke with, travelled with, or worked with. Use an object pronoun after with.
14.13 Answer: I spoke with him at school.
14.14 Question: 14.14. Write two sentences about one possession: identify it with a possessive adjective before a noun, then describe the same possession with a possessive pronoun.
14.14 Answer: It's my book.
The book is mine.
14.15 Question: 14.15. Write one sentence about doing something alone or one sentence about two people helping or knowing each other. Use the appropriate reflexive or reciprocal form.
14.15 Answer: I did this work by myself.
