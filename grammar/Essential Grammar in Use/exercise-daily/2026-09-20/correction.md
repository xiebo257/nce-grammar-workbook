## Key Takeaway

1. Use object pronouns after verbs and prepositions: `invite me`, `with him`.
2. Possessive adjectives come before nouns (`my book`); possessive pronouns stand alone (`mine`).
3. Use `a/an` in the double-possessive pattern: `a friend of mine`.
4. Reflexive pronouns refer back to the subject (`Ben saw himself`); reciprocal `each other` describes mutual action.
5. Distinguish possessive `its` from the contraction `it's`.
