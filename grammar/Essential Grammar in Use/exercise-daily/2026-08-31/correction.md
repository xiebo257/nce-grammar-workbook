## Key Takeaway

1. **Past participle after "have/has" (Unit 57)**: "I haven't **made** an appointment" — not "make." After have/has + not, use the past participle (made, done, had).
2. **Catch/get a cold, not "have cold" (Unit 56/58)**: to talk about becoming sick, use **catch a cold** or **get a cold**. "Have a cold" means you are already sick (a state), not that you will become sick.
3. **At the party, not "in the party" (Unit 58/108)**: for events (parties, weddings, concerts, meetings), use the preposition **at** — at the party, at the meeting, at the concert.
4. **Past tense for past events (Unit 58)**: "We had a good time" (past), not "We have a good time" (present). Match the tense to the time reference.
5. **Get off buses, get out of cars (Unit 56)**: for public transport (bus, train, plane) → **get on/off**. For cars → **get in/out of**. Never "get out the bus."
6. **Do vs Make collocations (Unit 57)**: do = perform an action (do homework, do a favour, do an exam, do housework). Make = create/produce (make a mistake, make a phone call, make an appointment, make a cake, make a list). These are fixed — memorize them.
