## Key Takeaway

1. **`hope` vs `expect` for others' actions:** `hope` does not take `object + to-infinitive`. Say `I hope (that) she will come` or `I expect her to come` — not `I hope her to come`.
2. **`on time` vs `in time`:** `on time` = punctual, at the scheduled moment (准时). `in time` = with enough time to spare, not late (来得及).
3. **`wait for` pattern:** `wait for sb/sth to do sth` — the `for` is required before the object. `I wait my wife` is incorrect; `I'm waiting for my wife` is correct.
4. **`like` vs `would like`:** `like` accepts both `-ing` and `to`-infinitive (both mean preference). `would like` accepts only `to`-infinitive (means wish/desire).
5. **Causative verbs:** `make` + object + base verb (no `to`); `tell` + object + `to`-infinitive.
6. **`for` as preposition:** after `for`, use a noun or `-ing` form, not a base verb.
