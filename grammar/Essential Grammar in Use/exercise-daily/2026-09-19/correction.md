## Key Takeaway

1. Use `go to + place`, `go for + noun`, and `go + -ing` according to the structure: `go to bed`, `go for a walk`, `go swimming`.
2. `Get` can mean become (`get tired`) or obtain (`get tickets`).
3. Learn common collocations: `make a phone call`, `make a reservation`, `make a mistake`, `do homework`, `do housework`, and `do someone a favour`.
4. In the present simple, `he/she/it` takes `does`, including fixed expressions such as `She does the shopping`.
