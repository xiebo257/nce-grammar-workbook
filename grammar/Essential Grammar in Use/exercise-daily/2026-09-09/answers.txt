1.1 Question: 1.1. Complete with am, is, or are: _____ you hungry? There's some rice in the kitchen.
1.1 Answer: Are
1.2 Question: 1.2. Complete with the correct present simple form: Water _____ (boil) at 100 degrees Celsius.
1.2 Answer: boil

1.3 Answer: swiming

1.10 Answer: am working (happening now)
work (a habit)

is watching (happening now)
