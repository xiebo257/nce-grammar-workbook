A. The office _____ every day. (a regular habit / general fact)
B. The office _____ right now. (an action in progress at this moment)

A. My bike _____ last week. (past event with a specific time)
B. My bike _____. I can't find it anywhere. (present result of a past event)

A. "When _____ this bridge _____ (build)?" "In 1990." (asking about a completed past event)
B. "_____ this bridge _____ (repair) at the moment?" "Yes, they're working on it." (asking about an action in progress now)
