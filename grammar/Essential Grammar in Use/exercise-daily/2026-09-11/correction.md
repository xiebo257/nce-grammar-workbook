## Key Takeaway

1. **Past passive = was/were + past participle (Unit 21)**: use **was** with singular subjects such as *this house*, *the letter*, and *my phone*; use **were** with *we*. Examples: “The house **was built** 100 years ago.” “We **were invited**.”
2. **Present passive for routines = is/are + past participle (Unit 21)**: “Glass **is made** from sand” and “These windows **are cleaned** every two weeks.” Match **is/are** to the subject.
3. **Present continuous passive = is/are being + past participle (Unit 22)**: an action happening now is “The bridge **is being repaired**.” Do not use the active -ing form (*repairing*) after **being**.
4. **Present perfect passive = has/have been + past participle (Unit 22)**: use **has** with a singular subject and **have** with a plural subject: “The oranges **have been eaten**.”
5. **Past event vs present result**: a finished event with a time expression such as *last week* uses the past passive (**was stolen**); a past event whose result matters now can use the present perfect passive (**has been stolen**).
6. **Spelling reminder**: the past participle of **repair** is **repaired**. The spelling *reapaired* is incorrect.
