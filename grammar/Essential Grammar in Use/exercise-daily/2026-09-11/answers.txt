4.3 Answer: is being repaired

4.10 Question: 4.10. Complete both with the correct passive form of clean, then explain the difference. A. The office _____ every day. (a regular habit / general fact)B. The office _____ right now. (an action in progress at this moment)
4.10 Answer: is cleaned  (a regular habit / general fact)
is being cleaned (an action in progress at this moment)
4.11 Question: 4.11. Complete both with the correct passive form of steal, then explain the difference. A. My bike _____ last week. (past event with a specific time)B. My bike _____. I can't find it anywhere. (present result of a past event)
4.11 Answer: was stolen (past event with a specific time)
has been stolen (present result of a past event)

"_____ this bridge _____ (repair) at the moment?" "Yes, they're working on it." (asking about an action in progress now)
4.12 Answer: was built  (asking about a completed past event)
Is being reapaired  (asking about an action in progress now)
4.13 Question: 4.13. Write one sentence about something that was built or created in your city or town, using the past passive (was/were + past participle).
4.13 Answer: The railway was built in 2020.
4.14 Question: 4.14. Write one sentence about something that is being done right now, using the present continuous passive (is/are being + past participle).
4.14 Answer: The homework is being done.
4.15 Question: 4.15. Write one sentence about something that has been done (completed with a present result), using the present perfect passive (has/have been + past participle).
4.15 Answer: The oranges have been eaten.
