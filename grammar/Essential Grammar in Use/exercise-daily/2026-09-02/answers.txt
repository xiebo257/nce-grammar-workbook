16.1 Question: 16.1. Complete the sentence with this, that, these, or those: (you are holding a phone, near you) Is _____ your phone or mine?
16.1 Answer: this
16.2 Question: 16.2. Complete the sentence with some or any: I wanted to buy _____ bananas, but the shop didn't have _____.
16.2 Answer: some any
16.3 Question: 16.3. Complete the sentence with one or ones: I don't like those shoes. I prefer the black _____.
16.3 Answer: ones
16.4 Question: 16.4. Correct the sentence: We don't have no money.
16.4 Answer: We have no money.
16.5 Question: 16.5. Correct the sentence: I'm looking for my keys. Has anybody seen it?
16.5 Answer: I'm looking for my keys. Has anybody seen them?
16.6 Question: 16.6. Correct the sentence: This cup is dirty. Can I have a clean?
16.6 Answer: This cup is dirty. Can I have a clean one?
16.7 Question: 16.7. 冰箱里没有牛奶了。
16.7 Answer: There's no milk in the fridge.
16.8 Question: 16.8. 你想要一些面包吗？
16.8 Answer: Do you want any bread?
16.9 Question: 16.9. 这个箱子太重了。我想要一个轻的。
16.9 Answer: This case is too heavy. I want a light one.
16.10 Question: 16.10. Explain the difference in meaning between these sentences: There's someone in the garden. / Is there anyone in the garden? / There's no one in the garden.
16.10 Answer: someone use positive
anyone use question+negative
no one use positive
16.11 Question: 16.11. Complete both sentences with no or not ... any, then explain the difference. A. There are _____ shops near here. (write one word)B. There aren't _____ shops near here. (write one word)
16.11 Answer: some positive
any negative
16.12 Question: 16.12. Complete both sentences with this or that, then explain why the choices differ. A. (on the phone) Hello, _____ is David. (identifying yourself)B. (referring to something someone just said) _____'s a great idea!
16.12 Answer: this. it's near to you.
that.  it's far to you.
16.13 Question: 16.13. Write one sentence about something near you and something far from you, using this/these and that/those.
16.13 Answer: This is my car. That's my wife.
16.14 Question: 16.14. Write one sentence about something you have or don't have, using some or any.
16.14 Answer: I have some new jeans.
I don't have any apples.
16.15 Question: 16.15. Write one sentence about something you need to replace or buy, using one or ones (e.g., I need a new one / I like the blue ones).
16.15 Answer: I have a old car, but I want to buy a new one.
