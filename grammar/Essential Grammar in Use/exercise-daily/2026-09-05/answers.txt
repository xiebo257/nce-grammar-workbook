6.1 Question: 6.1. Complete the sentence with the present continuous for a future arrangement: We _____ to the concert on Saturday. We've already bought tickets. (go)
6.1 Answer: are going
6.2 Question: 6.2. Complete the sentence with going to: Look at those dark clouds! It _____ rain. (rain)
6.2 Answer: is going to
6.3 Question: 6.3. Complete the sentence with will or won't: Don't worry about the bus. I _____ drive you home. (will)
6.3 Answer: will
6.4 Question: 6.4. Correct the sentence: Look at the sky. It will rain.
6.4 Answer: Look at the sky. It is going to rain.
6.5 Question: 6.5. Correct the sentence: I think I'm going to carry your bag for you. (you just decided to help)
6.5 Answer: I think I will carry your bag for you. (you just decided to help)
6.6 Question: 6.6. Correct the sentence: The train is leaving at 9.30 tomorrow morning. (this is a timetable)
6.6 Answer: The train leaves at 9.30 tomorrow morning.
6.7 Question: 6.7. 我明天晚上要和朋友去吃饭。（已经安排好了）
