2.1 Question: 2.1. Complete with was or were: At noon yesterday, the library _____ quiet, but the study rooms _____ full.
2.1 Answer: was were
2.2 Question: 2.2. Complete with the correct past simple negative form: Maya _____ (not take) the bus yesterday because she walked to work.
2.2 Answer: didn't take
2.3 Question: 2.3. Complete with the correct past continuous form: At seven yesterday evening, our neighbours _____ (have) dinner.
2.3 Answer: was having
2.4 Question: 2.4. Correct the sentence: They are at home last night.
2.4 Answer: They were at home last night.
2.5 Question: 2.5. Correct the sentence: Did Leo bought the tickets yesterday?
2.5 Answer: Did Leo buy the tickets yesterday?
2.6 Question: 2.6. Correct the sentence: At ten last night, I working in the kitchen.
2.6 Answer: At ten last night, I was working in the kitchen.
2.7 Question: 2.7. 我昨晚给姐姐写了一封信。
2.7 Answer: I wrote a letter for my sister last night.
2.8 Question: 2.8. 昨天下午三点，他们正在等公交车。
2.8 Answer: They were waiting the bus at three at noon yesterday.
2.9 Question: 2.9. 我走进厨房时，爸爸正在做晚饭。
2.9 Answer: When I came in the kitchen, my dad was cooking.
2.10 Question: 2.10. Complete both with the correct past form of leave, then explain how the negative is formed. A. Nina _____ home at 7.30 yesterday morning. (completed action)B. Nina _____ home yesterday because she was ill. (negative completed action)
2.10 Answer: left (completed action)
wasn't (negative completed action)
2.11 Question: 2.11. Complete both with the correct form of cook, then explain the difference. A. Omar _____ dinner yesterday. (the whole completed activity)B. At 6.30 yesterday, Omar _____ dinner. (in progress at that time)
2.11 Answer: cooked (the whole completed activity)
was cooking   (in progress at that time)
2.12 Question: 2.12. Complete both pairs, then explain the difference between simultaneous actions and an interruption. A. While Ava _____ (study), her brother _____ (play) a video game. (two actions in progress together)B. While Ava _____ (study), the lights _____ (go) out. (an action in progress interrupted by an event)
2.12 Answer: studying playing (two actions in progress together)
studying went  (an action in progress interrupted by an event)
2.13 Question: 2.13. Write one sentence saying where you were at a specific time yesterday. Use was or were and include the time.
2.13 Answer: I was at home yesterday morning.
2.14 Question: 2.14. Write one past simple question beginning with Did you ...?, then give your own short answer.
2.14 Answer: Did you have lunch today?
Yes, I did. I ate it at twelve.
2.15 Question: 2.15. Write one sentence about two actions that were in progress at the same time. Use while and the past continuous in both clauses.
2.15 Answer: While I was reading my book, my wife was watching TV.
