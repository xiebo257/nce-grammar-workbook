## Key takeaway

- Direct questions use auxiliary inversion: `Where does she work?` and `Where did they go?`
- Embedded questions use statement order: `Do you know where she works?`
- When `who` or `what` is the subject, do not add `do/does/did`: `Who called?`
- When `who` or `what` is the object, use the auxiliary: `Who did you call?`
- Use `which` for a limited choice and `what` for a general question.
- Use `has finished` when a past action has a present result; use `finished` for a definite past event.
