21.1 Question: 21.1. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb in brackets: It started raining, so we _____ the beach and drove home. (leave)
21.1 Answer: It started raining, so we left the beach and drove home.
21.2 Question: 21.2. Complete the sentence with the correct particle: The door was open, so the children walked _____ the house.
21.2 Answer: The door was open, so the children walked in the house.
21.3 Question: 21.3. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the verb in brackets: I can't hear you. Can you _____ a little? (speak, + up)
21.3 Answer: I can't hear you. Can you speak up a little?
21.4 Question: 21.4. Correct the sentence: I picked up it from the table.
21.4 Answer: I picked up it from the floor.
21.5 Question: 21.5. Correct the sentence: The plane took up from the runway.
21.5 Answer: The plane took off from the runway.
21.6 Question: 21.6. Correct the sentence: I tried to learn the guitar, but I gave after two weeks.
21.6 Answer: I tried to learn the guitar, but I gave up after two weeks.

21.10 Question: 21.10. Explain the difference in meaning between these two sentences: She put on her jacket. / She put her jacket on.
21.10 Answer: She put on her jacket.  V + Prep + O
She put her jacket on. V + O + Prep
21.11 Question: 21.11. Complete both sentences, then explain the difference. A. He took off his shoes. B. He took them off. Fill in the blank: Why is one form possible in A but not in B? A. He took off his shoes. — his shoes is a noun phrase.B. He took _____ off. — them is a pronoun.
21.11 Answer: A. He took off his shoes. — his shoes is a noun phrase. noun can come after verb or preposition.
B. He took them off. — them is a pronoun. pronoun must come after verb
21.12 Question: 21.12. Complete both sentences with the correct particle, then explain why the meanings differ. A. The bus stopped and we got _____ (we were passengers leaving the bus).B. The bus stopped and we got _____ (we were outside, entering the bus).
21.12 Answer: A. The bus stopped and we got out (we were passengers leaving the bus). We came down from the bus.
B. The bus stopped and we got on (we were outside, entering the bus).  We came up to the bus.
