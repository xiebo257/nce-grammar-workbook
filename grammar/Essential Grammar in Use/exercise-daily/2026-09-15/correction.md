## Key Takeaway

1. **Present simple agreement:** singular `he/she/it` subjects take `-s` or `-es` (`rises`, `boils`, `works`, `eats`); negatives use `doesn't + base verb` (`doesn't like`).
2. **Present continuous spelling:** use `be + -ing`; drop silent `e` (`write -> writing`) and double a final consonant in short CVC verbs (`swim -> swimming`, `run -> running`).
3. **Simple vs. continuous:** use present simple for habits and permanent facts (`studies`, `eats`), and present continuous for actions happening now (`is studying`, `are playing`).
4. **Questions:** use `do/does + subject + base verb` for general abilities (`Do you speak English?`) and `am/is/are + -ing` for actions in progress (`Are you speaking to him?`).
5. **Possession:** use `has got` with he/she/it (`She's got a new iPhone 17`).
6. **Translation precision:** `every day` is grammatical and natural in Q1.7, while `every morning` preserves the original Chinese time phrase more exactly.
