## Key Takeaway

1. **Noun phrases with "of" (Unit 69)**: a container noun needs **of** before the substance: "a bottle **of** water," "a cup **of** tea," "a piece **of** cake." The learner dropped **of** in 15.3.
2. **General vs. specific with "the" (Units 70–71)**: "I like coffee" (general, no article) vs. "I don't like **the** coffee in this café" (specific, needs "the"). The learner missed **the** in 15.8, losing the general/specific contrast.
3. **Second mention → "the" (Unit 70)**: "We found **a** cafe. **The** cafe had…" — something already introduced switches from **a** to **the**. The learner handled this correctly in 15.5 and 15.11.
4. **Institutions vs. specific places (Units 71–72)**: "at school" (institution, no article) vs. "the post office / the cinema / the bank" (specific place, with "the"). The learner handled these correctly in 15.7 and 15.14.
5. **Country/river names (Unit 71)**: countries take no article ("China"), but rivers take "the" ("The Chang Jiang"). The learner handled this correctly in 15.15.
6. **Superlative + "in" (Unit 90 overlap)**: "the highest mountain **in** the world," not "of the world." This was the minor slip in 15.15.
7. **Comma splices**: two independent clauses need a period or a conjunction, not just a comma. This appeared in 15.7 and 15.9.

## One-line Summary

Strict score 8/15 (2 errors + 5 notes); the article choices are mostly solid, but two errors remain — 15.3 "a bottle"→"a bottle **of** water" (missing **of**), and 15.8 "coffee"→"**the** coffee in this café" (missing **the** for specific reference) — while five notes are punctuation (comma splices), explanation language (Chinese instead of English), and a preposition ("of"→"in the world").
