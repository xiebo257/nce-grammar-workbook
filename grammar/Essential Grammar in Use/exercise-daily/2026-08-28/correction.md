## Key Takeaway

1. **Time clauses with when/before/after/while**: always use the **present simple** for future meaning — never *will*. (Unit 98)
2. **First conditional (possible future)**: **if + present simple, will + verb**. Remember third-person singular **-s** on the verb. (Unit 99)
3. **Second conditional (unreal/imaginary)**: **if + past simple, would + verb**. (Unit 100)
4. **Relative pronouns**: **who** for people (subject), **which/that** for things; when the pronoun is the **object**, it can be **omitted** and must not have a duplicate pronoun (e.g., ❌ "the recipe that I tried it"). (Units 101–102)
5. **when vs if**: *when* = the event is certain to happen; *if* = the event may or may not happen. (Unit 99.4)
