19.11 Answer: A. If I had a free afternoon today, I would visit the museum. I don't have time today.
B. If I have a free afternoon tomorrow, I will visit the museum. I possibly have time tomorrow.
19.12 Question: 19.12. Complete both sentences with who, that, or no relative pronoun where possible, then explain why the choices differ. A. The teacher _____ helped me was patient.B. The teacher _____ I thanked after class smiled.
19.12 Answer: A. The teacher who helped me was patient. who is subject.
B. The teacher that I thanked after class smiled. that is object.
