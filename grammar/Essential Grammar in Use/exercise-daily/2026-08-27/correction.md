## Key Takeaway

- Use `at` for exact times: `at 9:30`; use `since` for a starting point that continues to now: `since 2022`.
- Use `before + -ing` for an earlier action: `before leaving`.
- Use `on` for surfaces and `in` for enclosed places: `on the table`, `in the classroom`.
- Use `to` for destinations and `by + transport` for travel: `go to Rome`, `by train`.
- Learn adjective-preposition combinations as fixed patterns: `interested in` and `good at`.
- Use verb-preposition combinations such as `wait for` and `talk to`.
- Use `from ... to ...` for a range: `from Monday to Friday`.
- A movement preposition should be paired with a movement verb: `walk across the road`; a location phrase alone does not show movement.
