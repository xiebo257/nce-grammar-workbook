8.1 Question: 8.1. Complete with there is, there are, or it is: _____ a strange smell in the kitchen.
8.1 Answer: There is
8.2 Question: 8.2. Complete with there is, there are, or it is: _____ several mistakes in your essay.
8.2 Answer: There are
8.3 Question: 8.3. Complete with there is, there are, or it is: _____ a long way from here to the station.
8.3 Answer: It's
8.4 Question: 8.4. Correct the sentence: There have a lot of problems with this car.
8.4 Answer: There are a lot of problems with this car.
8.5 Question: 8.5. Correct the sentence: There weren't any milk in the fridge.
8.5 Answer: There wasn't any milk in the fridge.
8.6 Question: 8.6. Correct the sentence: There is cold today.
8.6 Answer: It is cold today.
8.7 Question: 8.7. 桌上有一本书和两支笔。
8.7 Answer: There are a pen and two pens on the table.
8.8 Question: 8.8. 今天很冷。
8.8 Answer: It's cold today.
8.9 Question: 8.9. 从这里到机场有十公里。
8.9 Answer: It's ten kilometres from here to the airport.
8.10 Question: 8.10. Complete both with there or it, then explain the difference. A. _____ is a cat in the garden. (something exists)B. _____ is raining. (weather)
8.10 Answer: There  (something exists)
It  (weather)
8.11 Question: 8.11. Complete both with there or it, then explain the difference. A. _____ is ten o'clock. (time)B. _____ were ten people at the meeting. (counting how many)
8.11 Answer: It (time)
There  (counting how many)
8.12 Question: 8.12. Complete both with there or it, then explain the difference. A. _____ is true that he lied. (referring to a fact)B. _____ is a rumour that he lied. (something exists)
8.12 Answer: It (referring to a fact)
There (something exists)
8.13 Question: 8.13. Write one sentence about what is (or isn't) in your room right now, using there is/are.
8.13 Answer: There is a table in the living room.
8.14 Question: 8.14. Write one sentence about today's weather or the distance from your home to your workplace, using it.
8.14 Answer: It's mild.
8.15 Question: 8.15. Write one sentence about something that existed or happened in the past, using there was/were.
8.15 Answer: There are many people in the office.
