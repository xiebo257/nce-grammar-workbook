## Key Takeaway

1. **-ing spelling rules (Unit 23)**: (a) verbs ending in silent -e drop the -e → write → **writing**, lie → **lying**; (b) short verbs (one consonant + vowel + one consonant) double the final consonant → run → **running**. "runing" and "writeing" are both wrong.
2. **Past participle vs. past simple after have (Unit 24)**: after has/have, use the **past participle**, not the past simple. go → went (past) / **gone** (past participle); "She has **gone**" ✅, "She has went" ✗. Other examples: write → wrote → **written**, break → broke → **broken**.
3. **Present continuous (am/is/are + -ing) vs. present perfect (have/has + past participle)**: "She is **going** to school now" (happening now) vs. "She has **gone** to school" (completed before now). Same verb, two different forms, two different meanings.
4. **Error-correction ≠ rewording**: when a sentence has a single spelling/form error (e.g. "runing"), fix that error and keep the original tense and meaning. Don't rewrite "They are runing fast" as "They run fast" — that changes the grammar, not just the spelling.
5. **Present continuous for "正在" and present perfect for "已经…了"**: 他正在睡觉 → He's sleeping; 她已经完成了作业 → She has (already) finished her homework; 他们在踢足球 → They are playing football.
