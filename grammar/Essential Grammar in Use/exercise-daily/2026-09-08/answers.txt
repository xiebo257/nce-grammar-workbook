3.1 Question: 3.1. Complete the sentence with the present perfect form of the verb in brackets: I _____ my keys. I can't find them anywhere. (lose)
3.1 Answer: have lost
3.2 Question: 3.2. Complete the sentence with just, already, or yet: Don't tell me the ending — I _____ haven't watched that film.
3.2 Answer: yet
3.3 Question: 3.3. Complete the sentence with for or since: She has worked here _____ 2019.
3.3 Answer: since
3.4 Question: 3.4. Correct the sentence: I have seen that film yesterday.
3.4 Answer: I saw that film yesterday.
3.5 Question: 3.5. Correct the sentence: She has gone to Paris three times.
3.5 Answer: She has been to Paris three times.
3.6 Question: 3.6. Correct the sentence: How long do you live here?
3.6 Answer: How long have you lived here?
3.7 Question: 3.7. 我刚吃完午饭。
3.7 Answer: I've just eaten lunch.
3.8 Question: 3.8. 你去过日本吗？
3.8 Answer: Have you been to Japan.
3.9 Question: 3.9. 她学英语已经三年了。
3.9 Answer: She's learnt English for three years.
3.10 Question: 3.10. Explain the difference in meaning: She has gone to London. / She has been to London.
3.10 Answer: 1, she isn't here now.
2, she is here now.
3.11 Question: 3.11. Complete both sentences, then explain the difference. A. I _____ my homework. Can I go out now? (finish — just completed)B. I _____ my homework at 8 o'clock. (finish — specific past time)
3.11 Answer: have just finished,(finish — just completed)
finished,(finish — specific past time)
3.12 Question: 3.12. Complete both sentences with for or since, then explain why the choices differ. A. I've known her _____ five years. (a duration — use for)B. I've known her _____ 2021. (a starting point — use since)
3.12 Answer: for (a duration — use for)
since  (a starting point — use since)
3.13 Question: 3.13. Write one sentence about something you have never done but would like to try. Use I have never ....
3.13 Answer: I have never played parachute.
3.14 Question: 3.14. Write one sentence about how long you have lived in your current home. Use I have lived ... for/since ....
3.14 Answer: I've lived in Shanghai since 2016.
3.15 Question: 3.15. Write one sentence about something you have just done or have already done today.
3.15 Answer: I've just eaten dinner.
