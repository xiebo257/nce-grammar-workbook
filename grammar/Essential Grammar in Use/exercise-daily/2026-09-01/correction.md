## Key Takeaway

1. **Singular countable nouns always need a/an (Unit 67)**: "I never wear **a** hat" — not "I never wear hat." Every singular countable noun (hat, key, knife, umbrella, car) requires a or an. Uncountable nouns (music, milk, water, cheese) do not take a/an.
2. **Jeans, trousers, scissors are always plural (Unit 66)**: you cannot say "a jeans." Say "some jeans," "new jeans," or "a pair of jeans."
3. **Irregular plurals (Unit 66)**: child → children, foot → feet, woman → women, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice, sheep → sheep, fish → fish. Don't add -s to these.
4. **The for unique/specific things (Unit 70)**: the sun, the moon, the sky, the internet, the police, the name of..., the same. Use "the" when there is only one, or when both speakers know which one.
5. **No article for meals (Unit 70)**: have breakfast, have lunch, have dinner — no "the" or "a." But: "We had dinner at a restaurant" (dinner = no article, a restaurant = a specific place).
6. **a vs an is about sound, not spelling (Unit 65)**: "an hour" (silent h, vowel sound), "a university" (/juː/ consonant sound), "an engineer" (vowel sound).
