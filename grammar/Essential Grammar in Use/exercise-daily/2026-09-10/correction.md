## Key Takeaway

1. **Past continuous form = was/were + -ing (Unit 14)**: an action in progress in the past needs the auxiliary **was/were** plus the **-ing** form — "was reading", "was cooking", "was writing". The bare verb ("read") alone is not past continuous; it is past simple. This was the error in 2.3.
2. **Past tense with past time markers (Unit 10)**: "yesterday", "last week", "last night" all signal the past. Use **was/were** or the past simple, not the present tense. "I'm tired yesterday" ✗ → "I **was** tired yesterday." This was the error in 2.7.
3. **Two simultaneous past actions both use past continuous (Unit 14)**: "While I was …-ing, she was …-ing." This is the **parallel** pattern, distinct from the **interruption** pattern ("While I was …-ing, the phone rang" = past continuous + past simple). The learner confused the two in 2.15, writing an interruption where the exercise asked for two parallel ongoing actions.
4. **Past simple negative = didn't + base form (Unit 12)**: "didn't go", not "don't went". The auxiliary **didn't** already carries the past tense; the main verb stays in its base form.
5. **Past simple of go = went (Unit 11, irregular verbs)**: go → went, write → wrote. These irregular forms were all used correctly (2.2, 2.8, 2.11, 2.13).
