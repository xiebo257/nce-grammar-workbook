2.1 Question: 2.1. Complete with was or were: Yesterday _____ Sunday, and we _____ at home all day.
2.1 Answer: was were
2.2 Question: 2.2. Complete with the correct past simple form: She _____ (go) to the cinema last night.
2.2 Answer: went
2.3 Question: 2.3. Complete with the correct past continuous form: I _____ (read) a book when the phone rang.
2.3 Answer: read
2.4 Question: 2.4. Correct the sentence: I were tired yesterday.
2.4 Answer: I was tired yesterday.
2.5 Question: 2.5. Correct the sentence: He don't went to school.
2.5 Answer: He didn't go to school.
2.6 Question: 2.6. Correct the sentence: While I cooked, she called me.
2.6 Answer: While I was cooking, she called me.
2.7 Question: 2.7. 我昨天很累。
2.7 Answer: I'm tired yesterday.
2.8 Question: 2.8. 他上周去了北京。
2.8 Answer: He went to Beijing last week.
2.9 Question: 2.9. 她打电话的时候，我正在做饭。
2.9 Answer: While I was cooking, she telephoned me.
2.10 Question: 2.10. Complete both with the correct form of go, then explain the difference. A. She _____ to the cinema last night. (completed past action)B. She _____ to the cinema when I met her. (in progress at that moment)
2.10 Answer: went (completed past action)
was going (in progress at that moment)
2.11 Question: 2.11. Complete both with the correct form of write, then explain the difference. A. He _____ a letter every day last week. (repeated past action)B. He _____ a letter when the lights went out. (in progress at that moment)
2.11 Answer: wrote  (repeated past action)
was writing  (in progress at that moment)
2.12 Question: 2.12. Complete both questions, then explain the difference in tense. A. "Where _____ you _____ (go) last summer?" "In Japan." (asking about a completed past event)B. "What _____ you _____ (do) when I called?" "I was watching TV." (asking about an action in progress then)
2.12 Answer: did go  (asking about a completed past event)
were doing (asking about an action in progress then)
2.13 Question: 2.13. Write one sentence about something you did yesterday, using the past simple (I …-ed / I went).
2.13 Answer: I went shopping yesterday.
2.14 Question: 2.14. Write one sentence about what you were doing at 8 p.m. yesterday, using the past continuous (I was …-ing).
2.14 Answer: I was brushing my teeth at 8 p.m. yesterday.
2.15 Question: 2.15. Write one sentence about two past actions happening at the same time, using while and the past continuous (While I was …-ing, …).
2.15 Answer: While I was doing my homework,  my phone rang.
