## Key takeaway

- Use the base verb after modal verbs: `might rain`, `should check`, `must go`, and `can eat`.
- Use `to + verb` after verbs such as `want`, `decide`, and `hope`: `want to leave`.
- Use `verb-ing` after verbs such as `enjoy`, `suggest`, and `mind`: `enjoy reading` and `suggest taking`.
- Use `want + object + to + verb`: `I want you to listen.`
- Use `to + verb` to explain purpose: `I went to the pharmacy to buy medicine.`
- Use `for + noun` when naming a thing or occasion: `I went out for some food.`
- Check subject-verb agreement and spelling: `My teacher wants` and `early`.
