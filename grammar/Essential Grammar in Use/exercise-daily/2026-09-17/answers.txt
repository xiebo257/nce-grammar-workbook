21.1 Question: 21.1. Complete with one particle: The train reached our station, so we got _____.
21.1 Answer: on
21.2 Question: 21.2. Complete with one particle: My friend opened the passenger door of her car, and I got _____.
21.2 Answer: in
21.3 Question: 21.3. Complete with take back and the pronoun them: These books belong to the library. I must _____ tomorrow morning.
21.3 Answer: take them back
21.4 Question: 21.4. Correct the sentence: I borrowed the charger and brought back it the next day.
21.4 Answer: I borrowed the charger and brought it back the next day.
21.5 Question: 21.5. Correct the sentence: After the taxi stopped at my address, I got off the taxi.
21.5 Answer: After the taxi stopped at my address, I got out the taxi.
21.6 Question: 21.6. Correct the sentence: I found my glasses and put back them in the case.
21.6 Answer: I found my glasses and put them back in the case.
21.7 Question: 21.7. 公交车停下来时，我下了车。
21.7 Answer: When the bus stopped, I got off.
21.8 Question: 21.8. 天黑了，所以我打开了台灯。后来我又把它关掉了。
21.8 Answer: It was dark, so I turned on the lamp.  Then I turned it off.
21.9 Question: 21.9. 我捡起地上的钥匙，把它们放回了桌上。
21.9 Answer: I picked up the keys on the floor and put them back on the table.
21.10 Question: 21.10. Complete both with one particle, then explain the difference between the vehicles. A. The taxi stopped outside my house, and I got _____. (I left the taxi.) / B. The tram stopped at my street, and I got _____. (I left the tram.)
21.10 Answer: out (I left the taxi.)
off (I left the tram.)
21.11 Question: 21.11. Complete both with turn off, then explain where a noun or a pronoun can go. A. After the room cooled down, I _____ the fan _____. (put the noun between the verb and particle) / B. Later, the fan was on again, so I _____ it _____. (use the pronoun)
21.11 Answer: turned the fan off (put the noun between the verb and particle)
turned it off  (use the pronoun)
21.12 Question: 21.12. Complete with go off and turn off in the past simple. Explain which action happened by itself and which action someone did. A. The kitchen timer _____ after ten minutes. (it began ringing) / B. I heard it and _____ it _____. (I stopped the ringing)
21.12 Answer: went off  (it began ringing)
turned off (I stopped the ringing)
21.13 Question: 21.13. Write a sentence about getting on or off a bus/train, or getting in or out of a car. Name the vehicle.
21.13 Answer: I got in the car.
21.14 Question: 21.14. Name an item you moved back to its place, then use it or them with put back in a sentence.
21.14 Answer: I put back my phone on the table.
21.15 Question: 21.15. Write two equivalent sentences about switching off a device: first use its name with switch off, then refer to it with a pronoun.
21.15 Answer: I switched off the light, but someone switched it on after ten minutes, so I switched it off again.
