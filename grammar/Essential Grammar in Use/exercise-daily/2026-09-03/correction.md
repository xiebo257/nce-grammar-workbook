## Key Takeaway

1. **say vs tell (Unit 50)**: **say** has no personal object — "He **said** (that) he was leaving." **tell** needs a person — "He **told me** (that) he was leaving." Common errors: "said me" ✗ → "told me"; "told that" ✗ → "said that" (or add a person: "told me that").
2. **Backshift tenses in reported speech (Unit 50)**: present → past ("am" → "was"), past → past perfect ("was" → "had been"), present perfect → past perfect ("have eaten" → "had eaten"). The reported clause moves one step back in time.
3. **Backshift modals (Unit 50)**: will → would, can → could, may → might, must → had to. "I will call" → "she said she would call"; "I can swim" → "he said he could swim."
4. **Reported commands use tell/ask + person + to + verb (Unit 50)**: "My mother told me to clean my room" / "She asked me to go home early." Not "told me that I had to" — that's a statement, not the command pattern.
5. **Singular countable nouns still need a/an inside reported speech**: "had **a** meal" (not "had meal"). Grammar rules don't pause inside reported clauses.
6. **"that" is optional after say/tell**: "She said (that) she was busy" — both forms are correct.
