Lesson 85: old/nice/interesting etc. (adjectives)

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 85. Your answers are saved in this browser.

85.1 Put the words in the right order

  1. (new / live in / house / they / a) They live in a new house.
  2. (like / jacket / I / that / green)
  3. (music / like / do / classical / you?)
  4. (had / wonderful / a / I / holiday)
  5. (went to / restaurant / a / Japanese / we)

85.2 Complete with an adjective and noun

Use: air, clouds, foreign, holiday, job, languages, sharp, black, dangerous, fresh, hot, knife, long, water.

  1. Do you speak any foreign languages?
  2. Look at those _____. It's going to rain.
  3. Sue works very hard, and she's very tired. She needs a _____.
  4. I would like to have a shower, but there's no _____.
  5. Can you open the window? We need some _____.
  6. I need a _____ to cut these onions.
  7. Fire-fighting is a _____.

85.3 Write sentences for the pictures

Use feel(s), look(s), sound(s), smell(s), taste(s) + happy, ill, nice, horrible, new, surprised.

  1. (voice: happy) You sound happy.
  2. (coat: new) It _____.
  3. (speaker: ill) I _____.
  4. (person: surprised) You _____.
  5. (flowers: nice) They _____.
  6. (food: horrible) It _____.

85.4 Complete B's sentences

A and B do not agree. Use feel/look/sound/taste.

  1. A: You look tired. B: Do I? I don't feel tired.
  2. A: This is a new coat. B: Is it? It doesn't _____. (look)
  3. A: I'm American. B: Are you? You _____. (sound)
  4. A: You look cold. B: Do I? I _____. (feel)
  5. A: These bags are heavy. B: Are they? They _____. (look)
  6. A: That soup looks good. B: Maybe, but it _____. (taste)
