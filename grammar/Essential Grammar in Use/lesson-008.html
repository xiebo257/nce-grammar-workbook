8.1 Answer the questions about the pictures

  1. A woman is a bus driver. She is playing the piano now.
    Is she driving a bus?Does she drive a bus?What is she doing?
  2. A man is a window cleaner. He is cleaning a window now.
    Does he clean windows?Is he cleaning a window?What is he doing?
  3. Two people are teachers. They are watching television now.
    Are they teaching?Do they teach?What do they do?

8.2 Complete with am/is/are or do/don't/does/doesn't

  1. Excuse me, _____ you speak English?
  2. 'Where's Kate?' 'I _____ know.'
  3. What's funny? Why _____ you laughing?
  4. 'What _____ your sister do?' 'She's a dentist.'
  5. It _____ raining. I want to go out in the rain.
  6. 'Where _____ you come from?' 'Canada.'
  7. How much _____ it cost to stay at this hotel? Is it expensive?
  8. Steve is a good tennis player, but he _____ play very often.
