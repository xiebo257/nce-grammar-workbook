Lesson 106: in/at/on (places 1)

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 106. Your answers are saved in this browser.

106.1 Answer the questions using in/at/on

  1. Where is he? (the kitchen) In the kitchen.
  2. Where are the shoes? (inside the box)
  3. Where is the pen? (on top of the box)
  4. Where is the clock? (the wall)
  5. Where is the bus? (the bus stop)
  6. Where are the horses? (the field)
  7. Where are they standing? (the balcony)
  8. Where is she swimming? (the pool)
  9. Where is he standing? (the window)
  10. Where is the spider? (the ceiling)
  11. Where is he sitting? (on top of the table)
  12. Where is she sitting? (at the table)

  6. 'Is the hospital near here?' 'Yes, turn left _____ the traffic lights.'
  7. It's difficult to park _____ the centre of town. It's better to take the bus.
  8. My sister lives _____ Brussels.
  9. There's a small park _____ the top of the hill.
  10. I think I heard the doorbell. There's somebody _____ the door.
  11. Munich is a large city _____ the south of Germany.
  12. There are a few shops _____ the end of the street.
