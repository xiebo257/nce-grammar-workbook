Lesson 82: both/either/neither

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 82. Your answers are saved in this browser.

82.1 Write both/either/neither

Use of where necessary.

  1. Last year I went to Paris and Rome. I liked both cities very much.
  2. There were two pictures on the wall. I didn't like either of them.
  3. It was a good football match. _____ teams played well.
  4. It wasn't a good football match. _____ team played well.
  5. 'Is your friend English or American?' '_____. She's Australian.'
  6. We went away for two days, but the weather wasn't good. It rained _____ days.
  7. A: I bought two newspapers. Which one do you want? B: _____. It doesn't matter which one.
  8. I invited Sam and Chris to the party, but _____ them came.
  9. 'Do you go to work by car or by bus?' '_____. I always walk.'
  10. 'Which jacket do you prefer, this one or that one?' 'I don't like _____ them.'
  11. 'Do you work or are you a student?' '_____. I work and I'm a student too.'
  12. My friend and I went to the cinema, but _____ us liked the film. It was really bad.
  13. Helen has two sisters and a brother. _____ sisters are married.
  14. Helen has two sisters and a brother. I've met her brother, but I haven't met _____ her sisters.

82.2 Complete the picture sentences

Use Both ... or Neither ....

  1. (cups empty) Both cups are empty.
  2. (windows open) _____ open.
  3. (men without hats) _____ wearing a hat.
  4. (men with beards) _____ beards.
  5. (buses to airport) _____ to the airport.
  6. (answers incorrect) _____ correct.

82.3 Write sentences about the two people

The man and woman gave the same answers.

  1. Are you married? Both: No. Neither of them is married.
  2. How old are you? Both: 21. Both of them are 21.
  3. Are you a student? Both: Yes.
  4. Do you have a car? Both: No.
  5. Where do you live? Both: London.
  6. Do you like cooking? Both: Yes.
  7. Can you play the piano? Both: No.
  8. Do you eat seafood? Both: Yes.
  9. Are you interested in sport? Both: No.
