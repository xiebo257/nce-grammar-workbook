96.1 Write sentences beginning He gave ...

Mark gave things he didn't want to different people.

  1. What did Mark do with the armchair? (his brother) He gave it to his brother.
  2. What did he do with the tennis racket? (Gary) He gave _____.
  3. What happened to the books? (Sarah) He _____.
  4. What about the lamp? (his sister)
  5. What did he do with the pictures? (Robert)
  6. And the ladder? (a neighbour)

  5. Rachel - a bunch of flowers:
  6. Kevin - a wallet:

96.3 Write questions beginning Can you ...?

  1. (you want the salt / pass) Can you pass me the salt?
  2. (you need an umbrella / lend) Can you ____?
  3. (you want my address / give) Can _____ your _____?
  4. (you need twenty pounds / lend)
  5. (you want more information / send)
  6. (you want to see the letter / show)

96.4 Which is right?

  1. I gave to Sarah the keys. / I gave Sarah the keys.
  2. I'll lend to you some money if you want. / I'll lend you some money if you want.
  3. Did you send the bill me? / Did you send the bill to me?
  4. I want to buy for you a present. / I want to buy you a present.
  5. Can you pass to me the sugar, please? / Can you pass me the sugar, please?
  6. This is Lisa's bag. Can you give it to her? / Can you give her it?
  7. I showed to the policeman my identity card. / I showed the policeman my identity card.
