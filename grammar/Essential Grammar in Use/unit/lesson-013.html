13.1 Where were these people at 3 o'clock yesterday afternoon? What were they doing?

Look at the picture in the book. Write two sentences for each.

  1. Rachel (at home / watch TV). Rachel was at home. She was watching TV.
  2. Jack and Kate (at the cinema / watch a film)
  3. Tom (in his car / drive)
  4. Tracey (at the station / wait for a train)
  5. Mr and Mrs Hall (in the park / walk)

13.3 Complete the questions

Use was/were -ing. Use what/where/why if necessary.

  1. (you/live) Where were you living in 2012? — In London.
  2. (you/do) _____ at 2 o'clock? — I was asleep.
  3. (it/rain) _____ when you got up? — No, it was sunny.
  4. (Sue/drive) _____ so fast? — Because she was late.
  5. (Tom/wear) _____ a suit yesterday? — No, a T-shirt and jeans.
