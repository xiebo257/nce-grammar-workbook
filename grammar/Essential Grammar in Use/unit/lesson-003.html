3.2 Complete the sentences

Use: build, cook, go, have, stand, stay, swim, work.

  1. Please be quiet. I'm working.
  2. He's in the kitchen. He .
  3. You on my foot.
  4. Somebody in the river.
  5. We at the Central Hotel.
  6. She a shower.
  7. They a new hotel at the moment.
  8. I now. Goodbye.

3.3 Write sentences about Jane

Use She's -ing or She isn't -ing.

  1. Have dinner: Jane isn't having dinner.
  2. Watch TV: She's watching TV.
  3. Sit on the floor: She
  4. Read a book: She
  5. Play the piano: She
  6. Laugh: She
  7. Wear a hat: She
  8. Drink coffee: She

3.4 What's happening now?

  1. I / wash / my hair: I'm not washing my hair.
  2. It / snow: It's snowing. or It isn't snowing.
  3. I / sit / on a chair
  4. I / eat
  5. It / rain
  6. I / learn / English
  7. I / listen / to music
  8. The sun / shine
  9. I / wear / shoes
  10. I / read / a newspaper
