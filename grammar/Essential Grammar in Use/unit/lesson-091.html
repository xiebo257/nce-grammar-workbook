91.1 Complete the sentences with enough + noun

Use: chairs, money, paint, wind.

  1. (a ticket costs £5; she has only £4) She hasn't got enough money.
  2. (a large group has only two chairs) There aren't _____.
  3. (only a little paint remains for a large door) She hasn't got _____.
  4. (the windsock hangs still) There isn't _____.

91.2 Complete the sentences with adjective + enough

Use: big, long, strong, tall.

  1. (a child cannot reach a high shelf) He isn't tall enough.
  2. (too many people are trying to fit in the car) The car _____.
  3. (the boy cannot reach the bicycle pedals) His legs aren't _____.
  4. (the man cannot lift the barbell) He _____.

91.3 Complete the sentences using enough

Use: big, eat, loud, milk, old, practise, space, time, tired.

  1. 'Is there enough milk in your coffee?' 'Yes, thank you.'
  2. Can you hear the radio? Is it loud enough for you?
  3. He can leave school if he wants - he's _____.
  4. When I visited New York last year, I didn't have _____ to see all the things I wanted to see.
  5. This house isn't _____ for a large family.
  6. Tina is very thin. She doesn't _____.
  7. My office is very small. There isn't _____.
  8. It's late, but I don't want to go to bed now. I'm not _____.
  9. Lisa isn't a very good tennis player because she doesn't _____.

91.4 Complete the sentences using enough

  1. We haven't got enough money to buy a new car. (money/buy)
  2. This knife isn't _____ tomatoes. (sharp/cut)
  3. The water wasn't _____ swimming. (warm/go)
  4. Have we got _____ sandwiches? (bread/make)
  5. We played well, but not _____ the game. (well/win)
  6. I don't have _____ newspapers. (time/read)
