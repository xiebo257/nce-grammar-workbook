Lesson 42: too/either; so am I / neither do I etc.

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 42. Your answers are saved in this browser.

42.1 Write too or either

  1. I'm happy. I'm happy too.
  2. I'm not hungry. I'm not hungry _____.
  3. I'm going out. I'm going out _____.
  4. It rained on Saturday. It rained on Sunday _____.
  5. Rachel can't drive a car. She can't ride a bike _____.
  6. I don't like shopping. I don't like shopping _____.
  7. Emma's mother is a teacher. Her father is a teacher _____.

42.2 Answer with So ... I or Neither ... I

  1. I went to bed late last night. So did I.
  2. I'm thirsty.
  3. I've just eaten.
  4. I need a holiday.
  5. I'll be late tomorrow.
  6. I was very tired this morning.
  7. I can't go to the party.
  8. I didn't phone Alex last night.
  9. I haven't got any money.
  10. I'm not going out tomorrow.
  11. I don't know what to do.
