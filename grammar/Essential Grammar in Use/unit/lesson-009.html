Lesson 9: I have and I've got

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 9. Your answers are saved in this browser.

9.1 Write with got

  1. They have two children. They've got two children.
  2. She doesn't have a key. She hasn't got a key.
  3. He has a new job.
  4. Do you have an umbrella?
  5. We have a lot of work to do.
  6. I don't have your phone number.
  7. Does your father have a car?
  8. How much money do we have?

9.2 Write with do/does/don't/doesn't

  1. Have you got any money? Do you have any money?
  2. I haven't got many clothes.
  3. Has Tom got a brother?
  4. How many children have they got?
  5. Have you got any questions?
  6. Sam hasn't got a job.

9.3 Write sentences about Mark

Use he's got or he hasn't got.

  1. car: No. He hasn't got a car.
  2. bike: Yes.
  3. dog: No.
  4. mobile phone: Yes.
  5. watch: No.
  6. brothers or sisters: Yes, two brothers and a sister.

9.4 Complete with have, has, don't have, or doesn't have

  1. Sarah _____ a car. She goes everywhere by bike.
  2. They like animals. They _____ three dogs and two cats.
  3. Charles isn't happy. He _____ a lot of problems.
  4. They are always busy. They _____ much free time.
  5. 'What's wrong?' 'I _____ something in my eye.'
  6. 'Where's my pen?' 'I don't know. I _____ it.'
  7. Amy wants to go to the concert, but she _____ a ticket.

9.5 Complete with have/has got or haven't/hasn't got

Use: six legs, a key, a headache, a lot of friends, a job, much time.

  2. Everybody likes Tom. He _____.
  3. She can't open the door. She _____.
  4. Quick! We _____.
  5. An insect _____.
  6. I'm unemployed. I _____.
