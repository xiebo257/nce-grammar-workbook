97.1 Write sentences using and/but/or

Match these ideas: I stayed at home; I bought a sandwich; I went to the window; I wanted to phone you; I jumped into the river; I usually drive to work; Do you want me to come with you? with I watched TV; I didn't eat it; I looked out; I didn't have your number; I swam to the other side; I went by bus this morning; Shall I wait here?

  1. I stayed at home and watched TV.
  2. I bought a sandwich, but I didn't eat it.
  3. I _____.

97.2 Complete using and/but/so/because

  1. (very hot; he opens the window) It was very hot, so he opened the window.
  2. (rain prevents their tennis game) They couldn't play tennis _____.
  3. (they arrive at the museum and find it closed) They went to the museum, _____.
  4. (Ben says he isn't hungry; 20 minutes later he has eaten nothing) Ben wasn't hungry, _____.
  5. (Helen arrives late and says sorry; the roads are busy) Helen was late _____.
  6. (Sue says goodbye, gets into her car and leaves) Sue said _____.
