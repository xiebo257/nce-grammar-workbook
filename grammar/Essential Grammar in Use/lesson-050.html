Lesson 50: She said that ... He told me that ...

50.1 Report what these people said

Write sentences with He/She/They said (that) ....

  1. He: 'I've lost my watch.' He said he had lost his watch.
  2. She: 'I'm very busy.'
  3. She: 'I can't go to the party.'
  4. He: 'I have to go out.'
  5. He: 'I'm learning Russian.'
  6. She: 'I don't feel very well.'
  7. They: 'We'll be home late.'
  8. She: 'I've just come back from holiday.'
  9. She: 'I'm going to buy a guitar.'
  10. They: 'We haven't got a key.'

50.2 Use the pictures to complete the sentences

  1. Clare: 'I'm enjoying my new job.' I met Clare last week. She said she was enjoying her new job.
  2. Emma: 'I'm not hungry.' Emma didn't want anything to eat. She said _____.
  3. Mike: 'I need it.' I wanted to borrow Mike's ladder, but he said _____.
  4. Hannah: 'I don't want to go.' Hannah was invited to the party, but she said _____.
  5. Susan: 'You can have it.' Susan told me she didn't want the picture. She said _____.
  6. Martin: 'I'll send you a postcard.' Martin has just gone away on holiday. He said _____.
  7. Nicola: 'He's gone home.' I was looking for Robert. Nicola said _____.
  8. David: 'I want to watch TV.' Why did David stay at home? He said _____.
  9. Mary: 'I'm going to the cinema.' Has Mary gone out? I think so. She said _____.

50.3 Write say/said or tell/told

  1. He said he was tired.
  2. What did she tell you?
  3. Anna _____ she didn't like Peter.
  4. Jack _____ me that you were ill.
  5. Please don't _____ Dan what happened.
  6. Did Lucy _____ she would be late?
  7. The woman _____ she was a reporter.
  8. The woman _____ us she was a reporter.
  9. They asked me a lot of questions, but I didn't _____ them anything.
  10. They asked me a lot of questions, but I didn't _____ anything.
