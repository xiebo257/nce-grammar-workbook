Lesson 102: the people we met; the hotel you stayed at (relative clauses 2)

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 102. Your answers are saved in this browser.

102.1 Make one sentence from two

  1. Helen took some pictures. Have you seen them? Have you seen the pictures Helen took?
  2. You gave me a pen. I've lost it. I've lost the _____.
  3. Sue is wearing a jacket. I like it. I like the _____.
  4. I gave you some flowers. Where are they? Where are the _____?
  5. He told us a story. I didn't believe it. I _____.
  6. You bought some oranges. How much were they? How _____?

102.2 Make one sentence from two

  1. I was carrying a bag. It was very heavy. The bag I was carrying was very heavy.
  2. You cooked a meal. It was excellent. The _____.
  3. I'm wearing shoes. They aren't very comfortable. The shoes _____.
  4. We invited some people to dinner. They didn't come. The _____.

102.3 Complete the questions

  1. Your friend stayed at a hotel. What's the name of the hotel you stayed at?
  2. Your friend was talking to some people. Who are the people _____?
  3. Your friend was looking for some keys. Did you find the _____?
  4. Your friend is going to a party. Where is the _____?
  5. Your friend was talking about a film. What's the name of _____?
  6. Your friend is listening to some music. What's that _____?
  7. Your friend applied for a job. Did you get _____?

102.4 Complete the questions using where

  1. John stayed at a hotel. Did you like the hotel where you stayed?
  2. Sue had dinner in a restaurant. What's the name of the restaurant _____?
  3. Sarah lives in a village. How big is the _____?
  4. Richard works in a factory. Where exactly is _____?
