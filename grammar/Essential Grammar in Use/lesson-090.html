90.1 Write sentences with comparatives and superlatives

  1. (big/small: A is biggest; then C, D, B) (A/D) A is bigger than D. (A) A is the biggest. (B) B is the smallest.
  2. (long/short: D is longest; then C, A, B) (C/A) C is _____ A.(D) D is _____.(B) B is _____.
  3. (young/old: A is 23, B is 19, C is 24, D is 21) (D/C) D _____.(B) _____.(C) _____.
  4. (expensive/cheap: A costs £8, B £10, C £15, D £12) (D/A) _____.(C) _____.(A) _____.
  5. (good/bad: restaurant A is excellent, B not bad, C good but not wonderful, D awful) (A/C) _____.(A) _____.(D) _____.

90.2 Complete the sentences using a superlative

  1. This building is very old. It's the oldest building in the town.
  2. It was a very happy day. It was _____ of my life.
  3. It's a very good film. It's _____ I've ever seen.
  4. She's a very popular singer. She's _____ in the country.
  5. It was a very bad mistake. It was _____ I've ever made.
  6. It's a very pretty village. It's _____ I've ever seen.
  7. It was a very cold day. It was _____ of the year.
  8. He's a very boring person. He's _____ I've ever met.
