Lesson 20 — I have done (present perfect) and I did (past)

#QuestionYour answerCorrectIssue
20.1 #4 "Has Sarah gone away? Yes, _____ on Friday." She went she went capitalization After "Yes, " the clause continues — lowercase "she".
20.3 #8 "I _____ (switch) off the light before going out this morning." have switched switched tense "this morning" + "before going out" = finished past context → past simple.

Rule: present perfect vs past simple
Finished time word (yesterday, last week, ago, this morning when it's over) → past simple. No finished time (ever, never, a few times) → present perfect.

Rule: passive formation
Passive = be + past participle. Present: is/are + done. Past: was/were + done. Plural subject → are/were. Use the correct past participle (wake → woken, not woke).

Lesson 22 — is being done / has been done (passive 2)

#QuestionYour answerCorrectIssue
22.2 #5 "(the car / damage)" — no starter text, full sentence needed has been damaged. The car has been damaged. missing subject Items 5–8 have no starter text — need a full sentence with "The car" as subject.
22.3 #6 "How old are these houses? When _____ (they/build)?" are they built were they built tense Houses already exist → building is completed past → past passive (were built), not present.
Rule: passive tense choice
Present continuous passive: is/are being + past participle (happening now). Present perfect passive: has/have been + past participle (completed, result now). Existing things → past passive (were built).

Lesson 23 — be/have/do in present and past tenses

#QuestionYour answerCorrectIssue
23.2 #5 "My parents are usually at home. They _____ go out very often." aren't don't auxiliary Bare verb (go) → do-family (don't), not be-family (aren't). "aren't go out" is grammatically broken.
Rule: choosing the correct auxiliary
Bare verb (go, walk, eat) → do/does/did. -ing form → am/is/are/was/were. Past participle → have/has (perfect) or is/are/was/were (passive).

Recurring Mistake Patterns

4 errors
Present perfect vs past simple — finished time
  • "this morning" + "before going out" → past simple — L20
  • "now" signals Mexico period is finished → past simple — L20
  • "before that" = past period → worked not has worked — L20
  • "a few times" = experience, no finished time → present perfect — L20
Fix: Check for finished time markers (yesterday, ago, this morning when over, "now" ending a period) → past simple. No finished time (ever, never, a few times) → present perfect.
2 errors
Wrong past participle in passive
  • wake → woke → woken (not "woke" in passive) — L21
  • meet → met → met (not "known") — L24
Fix: Memorize irregular verb forms. Passive (be + past participle) and perfect (have + past participle) both require the past participle, never the past simple form.
2 errors
Passive tense choice
  • General ongoing fact (no past marker) → present passive (are shown) — L21
  • Existing things → past passive (were built, not are built) — L22
Fix: General fact = is/are + done. Specific past event = was/were + done. Existing result from completed past = was/were done or has/have been done.
1 error
Subject-verb agreement in passive
  • films (plural) → are made (not is made) — L21
Fix: Check singular/plural of the subject before choosing is/are or was/were in passive.
2 errors
Wrong verb from box
  • "find" doesn't fit context → "give" (was given) — L21
  • "know" in sentence but box has "meet" → never met — L24
Fix: Match the verb to the meaning, not to a word already in the sentence. Read the context carefully and check which box verb fits.
1 error
Auxiliary family choice
  • bare verb (go) → don't (do-family), not aren't (be-family) — L23
Fix: Look at the verb form after the blank. Bare verb → do/does/did. -ing → be. Past participle → have/has or be (passive).
