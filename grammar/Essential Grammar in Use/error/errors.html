Unit 15 — Present Perfect 1 (Lesson 15)

UnitQuestion (context)What was wrong
15 "I'm looking for Paula. _____ you _____ her?" (verb: see) capitalization Answer used lowercase at the start of a question. A question must begin with a capital letter.
15 "I can't find my umbrella. Somebody _____ it." (verb box provided) wrong verb Answer used "steal" which is NOT in the given verb box. You must choose from the verbs provided in the exercise.
15 "What are you going to do? _____ you _____?" (verb: decide) capitalization Answer used lowercase at the start of a question. Questions must begin with a capital letter.
15 "I _____ with this magazine. Do you want it?" (verb: finish) wrong verb Answer used the wrong verb. "Finish with" means done using something; the context "Do you want it?" signals the speaker is done with the magazine.

Unit 17 — Present Perfect 3 (Lesson 17)

UnitQuestion (context)What was wrong

20 "I live in New York now, but I _____ (live) in Mexico for many years." tense Answer used present perfect. "Now" signals the Mexico period is finished, so past simple is needed.
20 "Before that she was a waitress. She _____ (work) there for two years" tense Answer used "has worked" for a past period. Waitressing is in the past (before current job), so past simple is needed.
20 "I _____ (see) her a few times" tense Answer used past simple. "A few times" = life experience with no finished time reference, so present perfect is needed.

Recurring Mistake Patterns

25+ errors
Wrong pronoun / subject agreement
  • Karen/Kate -> he (should be she) — U32, U41
  • I/we -> kept as I/we in questions (should be you) — U33, U48, U53
  • Plural subject with singular verb (isn't, was) — U25, U40, U41, U66, U74
  • it/him for things (should be it) — U59
  • Object pronoun where possessive needed — U61, U63
Fix: Match pronouns to names (Karen = she). Convert I/we to you in questions. Plural subjects need plural verbs. Things take "it."
15+ errors
Wrong tense for time marker
  • Present perfect for finished past (this morning, Mexico/now) — U20
  • Past simple for life experience (a few times) — U20
  • Present for past (when we arrived) — U38
  • had to for future / must for past — U31
  • Present passive for past completed (houses built) — U22
Fix: Check the time marker first — past markers (yesterday, when, ago) trigger past; "now" triggers present; no marker triggers present perfect for experience.
12+ errors
Modal + wrong verb form
  • have to + -ing -> bare infinitive — U33
  • might + -ing -> bare infinitive — U29
  • would like + bare infinitive -> to + infinitive — U51
  • 3rd person singular without -s after modals/auxiliaries — U49, U56
Fix: All modals (can, could, should, must, have to, might) take the bare infinitive. "Would like" takes to + infinitive. 3rd person -s applies in present simple, not after modals.
10+ errors
Countable vs uncountable / articles
  • traffic, soap, advice = uncountable -> singular/some — U37, U67, U68
  • scissors, trousers, police = always plural -> plural verb — U66
  • "a" before vowel sound (island, old) -> "an" — U67, U75
  • Missing "the" for instruments, galleries, canals, oceans — U36, U53, U73
Fix: Learn the uncountable noun list. Plural-only nouns (scissors, trousers) take plural verbs. Vowel sounds need "an." Instruments and place names often need "the."
20+ errors
Spelling & typos
  • Name spellings: tom, Pual, Brain, Sad/said — U16, U51, U64, U50
  • Letter transposition: Satruday, palying, lest's, diffrence — U25, U35, U77
  • Word confusion: batch/bath, but/buy, cat/car, coffe/coffee — U26, U28, U83, U34
  • Plural spellings: wemen, familes, childrent, charis — U66
Fix: Slow down on spelling. Double-check names and common words. Don't confuse similar words (bath/batch, buy/but, car/cat).
8+ errors
Capitalization & punctuation
  • Lowercase at sentence/question start — U15, U20, U35
  • Stray periods, double punctuation — U18, U50
  • Missing subjects in responses — U27, U68
Fix: Always capitalize the first letter of a sentence or question. Check for duplicate punctuation. Include full phrases when instructed.
6+ errors
it vs there confusion
  • "it" for rain amount -> "there" — U39
  • "it's" for new information -> "there's" — U37
  • "there" for people -> "they" — U43
Fix: "there" = existence/amount/new info. "it" = identity/quality/known thing. "they" = pronoun for people.

Auxiliary matching in response patterns
