Write sentences about Joe as a child

  1. Were you tall? No. He wasn't tall.
  2. Did you like school? Yes. He liked school.
  3. Were you good at sport? Yes.
  4. Did you play football? Yes.
  5. Did you work hard at school? No.
  6. Did you have a lot of friends? Yes.
  7. Did you have a bike? No.
  8. Were you a quiet child? No.
