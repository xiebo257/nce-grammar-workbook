Exercise 3

Use the words in brackets to write sentences

  1. A: Where are your parents?
    B: They're watching TV. (they / watch / TV)
  2. A: Paula is going out.
    B: Where's she going? (where / she / go?)
  3. A: Where's David?
    B: _____ (he / have / a shower)
  4. A: _____? (the children / play?)
    B: No, they're asleep.
  5. A: _____? (it / rain?)
    B: No, not at the moment.
  6. A: Where are Sue and Steve?
    B: _____ (they / come / now)
  7. A: _____? (why / you / stand / here?)
    B: _____ (I / wait / for somebody)
