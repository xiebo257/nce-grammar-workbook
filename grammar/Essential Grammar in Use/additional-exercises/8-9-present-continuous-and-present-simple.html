Exercise 8

Complete the sentences

  1. Please be quiet. I'm working. (I / work)
  2. _____ to the cinema? (you / often / go)
  3. What _____? (you / cook)
  4. Jack _____ the piano very well. (play)
  5. _____ now. Goodbye! (I / go)
  6. Can I take this umbrella? _____ (it / rain)
  7. _____ TV very much. (I / not / watch)
  8. Excuse me, _____ for the museum. (we / look)
  9. What's this word? How _____ it? (you / pronounce)

Exercise 9

  2. Sometimes _____ away at weekends.
  3. It's a nice day today. The sun _____.
  4. (You meet Kate in the street.) Hello, Kate. Where _____?
  5. How often _____ on holiday?
  6. Emily is a writer. _____ books for children.
  7. _____ newspapers.
  8. 'Where are Mark and Laura?' '_____ TV in the living room.'
  9. Helen is in her office. _____ to somebody.
  10. What time _____ dinner?
  11. Joe isn't at home at the moment. _____ some friends.
  12. 'Would you like some tea?' 'No, thank you. _____ tea.'
