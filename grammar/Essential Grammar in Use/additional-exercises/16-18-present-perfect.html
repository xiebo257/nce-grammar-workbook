Exercise 16

Complete the sentences

Use the present perfect. The picture context is given in parentheses.

  1. (Tom left five minutes ago.) 'Is Tom at home?' 'No, he's just gone out.'
  2. (A stranger walks past.) 'Who's that man?' 'I don't know. I've never seen him before.'
  3. (The film has reached "The End".) 'Are you still watching the film?' 'No, it _____ just _____.'
  4. (The glasses have been found.) 'I'm looking for my glasses.' 'Here they are! I _____ them!'
  5. (A customer examines a book.) 'Is this a good book?' 'I don't know. I _____ it.'
  6. 'I'm looking for Jessica. _____ her?' 'Yes, she was here a few minutes ago.'
  7. 'More coffee?' 'No, thanks. I _____ enough.'
  8. '_____ to Sweden?' 'Yes, I went there a few years ago.'
  9. (A couple return from the cinema.) 'Hi. We _____ to the cinema.'
  10. (Steve and Jane left for a party.) 'Where are Steve and Jane?' '_____ to a party.'
  11. (Paul was asleep, but is awake now.) 'Paul was asleep in the armchair. He _____ up.'
  12. 'How long _____ here?' 'Since 2010.'
  13. 'Do you know Alan?' 'Yes, we _____ each other for a long time.'
  14. (Rain continues outside.) 'The weather is horrible here. It _____ all day.'

Exercise 17

Complete the sentences

  1. Mark and Sarah are married. They have been married for five years.
  2. David has been watching TV since 5 o'clock.
  3. Joe is at work. He _____ at work since 8.30.
  4. 'Have you just arrived in London?' 'No, I've been here _____ five days.'
  5. I've known Helen _____ we were at school together.
  6. 'My brother lives in Los Angeles.' 'Really? How long _____ there?'
  7. George has had the same job _____ 20 years.
  8. Some friends of ours are staying with us at the moment. They _____ here since Monday.
