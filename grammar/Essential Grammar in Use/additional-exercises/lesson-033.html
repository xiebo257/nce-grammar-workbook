  4. (A classroom is pictured.) What do you do? - I'm _____.
  5. (A sick child thinks about the classroom.) I don't feel very well. I don't want to go to _____.
  6. (A cinema screen shows "The End".) What did you do last night? - I went to _____.
  7. (Thinking about a taxi.) Shall we walk home? - No, let's get _____.
  8. (A piano is pictured.) Can you play _____? - Yes, but not very well.
  9. (Looking at pictures of racing cars.) I'm interested in _____.
  10. (Two identical cars are pictured.) What's the difference between those cars? - Nothing, they're _____.
