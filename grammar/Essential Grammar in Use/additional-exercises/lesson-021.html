Complete the conversations

1. France

A: Have you been to France?
B: Yes, many times.
A: When _____ the last time?
B: Two years ago.

2. A new car

A: Is this your car?
B: Yes, it is.
A: How long _____ it?
B: It's new. I _____ it yesterday.

3. Where you live

A: Where do you live?
B: In Harold Street.
A: How long _____ there?
B: Five years. Before that _____ in Mill Road.
A: How long _____ in Mill Road?
B: About three years.

4. Work

A: What do you do?
B: I work in a shop.
A: How long _____ there?
B: Nearly two years.
A: What _____ before that?
B: I _____ a taxi driver.
