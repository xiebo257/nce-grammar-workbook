Exercise 29

Complete the conversations

1. Plans

  1. A: Did you go (you/go) out last night?
  2. B: _____ (I/stay) at home.
  3. A: What _____ (you/do)?
  4. B: _____ (I/watch) TV.
  5. A: _____ (you/go) out tomorrow night?
  6. B: Yes, _____ (I/go) to the cinema.
  7. A: Which film _____ (you/see)?
  8. B: _____ (I/not/know)._____ (I/not/decide) yet.

2. A holiday

  1. A: How long _____ (you/be) here?
  2. B: _____ (we/arrive) yesterday.
  3. A: And how long _____ (you/stay)?
  4. A: And _____ (you/like) it here?
  5. B: Yes, _____ (we/have) a wonderful time.

3. A restaurant

  1. A: _____ (I/go) out with Chris and Steve this evening._____ (you/want) to come with us?

  3. A: To the Italian restaurant in North Street. _____ (you/ever/eat) there?
  4. B: Yes, _____ (I/be) there two or three times.In fact I _____ (go) there last night.
