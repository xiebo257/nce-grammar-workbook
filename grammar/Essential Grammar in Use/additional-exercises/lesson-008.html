Complete the sentences

  1. Please be quiet. I'm working. (I / work)
  2. _____ to the cinema? (you / often / go)
  3. What _____? (you / cook)
  4. Jack _____ the piano very well. (play)
  5. _____ now. Goodbye! (I / go)
  6. Can I take this umbrella? _____ (it / rain)
  7. _____ TV very much. (I / not / watch)
  8. Excuse me, _____ for the museum. (we / look)
  9. What's this word? How _____ it? (you / pronounce)
