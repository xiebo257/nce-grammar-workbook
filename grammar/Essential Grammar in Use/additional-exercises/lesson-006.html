Complete the questions

Conversation 1

  1. What's your name? - Ben.
  2. _____ married? - Yes, I am.
  3. Where _____? - In Barton Road.
  4. _____ any children? - Yes, a daughter.
  5. How _____? - She's three.

Conversation 2

  1. _____? - I'm 29.
  2. _____? - I work in a supermarket.
  3. _____ your job? - No, I hate it.
  4. _____ a car? - Yes, I have.
  5. _____ to work by car? - No, I usually go by bus.

Conversation 3

  1. Who is this man? - That's my brother.
  2. _____? - Michael.
  3. _____? - He's a travel agent.
  4. _____ in London? - No, in Manchester.
