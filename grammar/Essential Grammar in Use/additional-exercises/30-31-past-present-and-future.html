Exercise 30

Rachel talks about her best friend, Carolyn

Put the verbs in the correct form.

  1. I remember very well the first time _____ (we/meet).
  2. It was our first day at secondary school, and _____ (we/sit) next to each other for the first lesson.
  3. _____ (we/not/know) any other students in our class,
  4. and so _____ (we/become) friends.
  5. We found that _____ (we/like) the same things, especially music and sport,
  6. and so _____ (we/spend) a lot of time together.
  7. _____ (we/leave) school five years ago,
  8. but _____ (we/meet) as often as we can.
  9. For the last six months Carolyn _____ (be) in Mexico.
  10. At the moment _____ (she/work) in a school as a teaching assistant.
  11. _____ (she/come) back to England next month,
  12. and when _____ (she/come) back,
  13. _____ (we/have) lots of things to talk about.
  14. _____ (it/be) really nice to see her again.

  3. The flight was twelve hours, but _____ (we/enjoy) it.

  5. and _____ (sleep) a few hours,

  11. Jon _____ (want) to drive up the coast to San Francisco,
