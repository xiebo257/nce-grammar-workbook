30.2 Complete these sentences

Use can or can't + one of these verbs: come find hear see speak.

  1. I'm sorry, but we can't come to your party next Saturday.
  2. I like this hotel room. You _____ the mountains from the window.
  3. You are speaking very quietly. I _____ you.
  4. Have you seen my bag? I _____ it.
  5. Catherine got the job because she _____ five languages.

30.3 Complete these sentences

Use can't or couldn't + one of these verbs: decide eat find go go sleep.

  1. I was tired, but I couldn't sleep.
  2. I wasn't hungry yesterday. I _____ my dinner.
  3. Kate doesn't know what to do. She _____.
  4. I wanted to speak to Mark yesterday, but I _____ him.
  5. James _____ to the concert next Saturday. He has to work.
  6. Paula _____ to the meeting last week. She was ill.
