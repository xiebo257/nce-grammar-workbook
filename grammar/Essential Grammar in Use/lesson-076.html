76.1 Write some or any

  1. I bought some cheese, but I didn't buy any bread.
  2. In the middle of the room there was a table and _____ chairs.
  3. There aren't _____ shops in this part of town.
  4. Gary and Alice don't have _____ children.
  5. Do you have _____ brothers or sisters?
  6. There are _____ beautiful flowers in the garden.
  7. Do you know _____ good hotels in London?
  8. 'Would you like _____ tea?' 'Yes, please.'
  9. When we were on holiday, we visited _____ interesting places.
  10. Don't buy _____ rice. We don't need _____.
  11. I went out to buy _____ bananas, but they didn't have _____.
  12. I'm thirsty. Can I have _____ water, please?

  8. It's hot in this office. I'm going out for _____ fresh _____.

  10. I can do this job alone. I don't need _____.

76.3 Complete with some or any

  1. Kate didn't take any pictures, but I took some. (I / take)
  2. 'Where's your luggage?' '_____.' (I / not / have)
  3. 'Do you need any money?' 'No, thank you. _____.' (I / have)
  4. 'Can you lend me some money?' 'I'm sorry, but _____.' (I / not / have)
  5. The tomatoes in the shop didn't look very good, so _____. (I / not / buy)
  6. There were some nice oranges in the shop, so _____. (I / buy)
  7. 'How much coffee did you drink yesterday?' '_____.' (I / not / drink)
