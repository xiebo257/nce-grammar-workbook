Lesson 48: How long does it take ...?

Essential Grammar in Use, Unit 48. Your answers are saved in this browser.

48.1 Write questions with How long ...?

Use the journeys shown in the pictures.

  1. Plane: London to Amsterdam. How long does it take by plane from London to Amsterdam?
  2. Car: Milan to Rome.
  3. Train: Paris to Geneva.
  4. Bus: the city centre to the airport.

48.2 How long does it take?

Write full sentences.

  1. Fly from your city/country to London. It takes two hours to fly from Madrid to London.
  2. Fly from your city/country to New York.
  3. Study to be a doctor in your country.
  4. Walk from your home to the nearest shop.
  5. Get from your home to the nearest airport.

48.3 Write questions with How long did it take ...?

  1. Jane found a job. How long did it take her to find a job?
  2. I walked to the station.
  3. Tom painted the bathroom.
  4. I learnt to ski.
  5. They repaired the car.

48.4 Write sentences with It took ...

  1. I read a book last week. I started on Monday and finished three days later. It took me three days to read the book.
  2. We walked home last night. We left at 10 o'clock and arrived at 10.20.
  3. I learnt to drive last year. My first lesson was in January and I passed my test six months later.
  4. Mark drove to London yesterday. He left at 7 o'clock and arrived at 10.
  5. Lisa began looking for a job a long time ago. She got a job last week.
  6. Write a sentence about yourself.
