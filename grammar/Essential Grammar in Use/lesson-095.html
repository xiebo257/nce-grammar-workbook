95.1 Ask Tina questions with still

You last saw Tina two years ago. At that time she said:

  1. I play the piano. Do you still play the piano?
  2. I live in Clare Street. Do you _____?
  3. I'm a student. Are _____?
  4. I've got a motorbike.
  5. I go to the cinema a lot.
  6. I want to be a teacher.

95.2 Write three sentences for each situation

  1. Before: they were at a bus stop. Now: they are there and the bus has not arrived. They were waiting for the bus. They are still waiting. The bus hasn't come yet.
  2. Before and now: he is looking through job advertisements; he has not found a job. (before)(still)(yet)
  3. At 7.00 and 7.30: she is asleep; she has not woken up. (before)(still)(yet)
  4. Before and now: they are eating dinner; they have not finished. (before)(still)(yet)

95.4 Complete the sentences using already

  1. 'What time is Joe coming?' 'He's already here.'
  2. 'Do they want to see the film?' 'No, they've already seen it.'
  3. 'I have to see Julia before she goes.' 'It's too late. She _____.'
  4. 'Do you need a pen?' 'No, thanks. I _____ one.'
  5. 'Shall I pay the bill?' 'No, it's OK. I _____.'
  6. 'Shall I tell Paul about the meeting?' 'No, he _____. I told him.'
