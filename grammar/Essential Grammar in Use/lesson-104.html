104.1 Complete the sentences

Alex lived in Canada from 2001 to 2009 and has lived in England since 2009. Karen lived in France before moving to Switzerland in 2011. Clare worked in a restaurant from 2010 to 2012 and has worked in a hotel since 2012. Adam was a teacher from 2002 to 2008 and has been a journalist since 2008.

Use from ... to, until or since:

  1. (Alex / Canada / 2001 → 2009) Alex lived in Canada from 2001 to 2009.
  2. (Alex / Canada / → 2009) Alex lived in Canada _____ 2009.
  3. (Alex / England / 2009 →) Alex has lived in England _____.
  4. (Karen / France / → 2011) Karen lived in _____.
  5. (Karen / Switzerland / 2011 →) Karen has lived in _____.
  6. (Clare / a restaurant / 2010 → 2012) Clare worked _____ 2010 _____.
  7. (Clare / a hotel / 2012 →) Clare has worked _____.
  8. (Adam / a teacher / 2002 → 2008) Adam was a _____.
  9. (Adam / a journalist / 2008 →) Adam has been _____.
  10. (Alex / Canada) Alex lived in Canada for eight years.
  11. (Alex / England) Alex has lived in England _____.
  12. (Karen / Switzerland) Karen has _____.
  13. (Clare / a restaurant) Clare worked _____.
  14. (Clare / a hotel) Clare _____.
  15. (Adam / a teacher) Adam _____.
  16. (Adam / a journalist) Adam _____.
