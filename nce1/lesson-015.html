NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 15: Your passports, please.

I. Write the plural form of each word

a girl, a friend, a case, a passport, an engineer, a ticket, a student, a tourist, a pencil, a tie.

II. Answer the questions according to the statements given and the examples

Example: The books are new. / Are the books new? -> Yes, they are. Example: The pencils are short. / Are the pencils long? -> No, they aren't. They are short.

  1. Our doors are white. / Are your doors red?
  2. The tourists are Swedish. / Are the tourists German?
  3. These cars are dirty. / Are these cars clean?
  4. These engineers are busy. / Are these engineers busy?
  5. Our friends are Danish. / Are your friends Norwegian?
  6. The carpets are red. / Are the carpets brown?
  7. The mechanics are dirty. / Are the mechanics dirty?
  8. Our passports are green. / Are your passports yellow?

III. Fill in the blanks with am, is, or are

  1. ___ your girl friend fat or thin?
  2. We ___ all very well, thank you.
  3. Our house ___ big and clean.
  4. Look! Here ___ my dress. It ___ very smart.
  5. ___ you Dutch tourists?
  6. We ___ not German. We ___ Norwegian.
  7. ___ you an engineer or a taxi driver?
  8. These ___ my books. Whose ___ these pens and pencils?
  9. My friend ___ a teacher. His two brothers ___ teachers, too.
  10. ___ these your pens? What make ___ they?
  11. Here ___ our passports.
  12. How ___ your parents? They ___ very well, thank you.
