Lesson 99: Ow!

I. Make sentences according to the example.

  1. 1. he says/he feels sleepy

He says that he feels sleepy.

  1. 2. she says/she feels ill
  2. 3. he thinks/he needs a haircut
  3. 4. I'm sure/it's going to snow tomorrow
  4. 5. the doctor says/you must stay in bed for three days
  5. 6. I'm sorry/he's got a bad cold
  6. 7. he believes/he can repair the car
  7. 8. she understands/I'm very tired and thirsty
  8. 9. he says/he usually gets up at seven
  9. 10. I know/Mr. Smith has just sold his house

II. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example. You may make changes where necessary.

  1. 1. What's the matter with you? (feel cold/be afraid/have got a temperature)

I feel cold. I'm afraid that I've got a temperature.

  1. 2. What's the matter with him? (fall downstairs/think/have hurt his left leg)
  2. 3. What's the matter with them? (have got mumps/be afraid/can't go to school next week)
  3. 4. What's the matter with you? (have got a headache/believe/have got flu)
  4. 5. What's the matter with his car? (he/had a car crash yesterday/know/must take it to a garage)
  5. 6. What's the matter with your face? (I/shaved this morning and cut myself/think/must be careful next time)
  6. 7. What's the matter with her? (have a toothache/think/must see the dentist)
  7. 8. What's the matter with you? (fall off my bike and hurt my back/think/need an X-ray)
