# NCE Grammar Practice 1 - Lessons 49-96

Source: `新概念英语语法练习 1.pdf`.  
This structured workbook transcription records the lesson sequence, grammar focus, directions, example patterns, and representative practice prompts for Lessons 49-96. Repeated answer lines are summarized; original blanks are represented by `___`.

## Lesson 49 - At the butcher's

### I. Complete requests, questions, and replies
Practice shopping vocabulary and `Do you want ...?`, `Would you like ...?`, `I want ...`, `Give me ...`, quantities, and food names.

### II. Use `like`, `want`, and `would like`
Make affirmative, negative, and interrogative sentences about meat, food, and drink.

## Lesson 50 - He likes ...

### I. Fill in the correct form of `like`
Use `like/likes`, `don't like/doesn't like`, questions, and short answers with singular and plural subjects.

### II. Ask and answer about preferences
Example patterns: *Do you like ...?* / *Does he like ...?* / *Yes, he does. No, he doesn't.*

## Lesson 51 - A pleasant climate

### I. Complete sentences with climate and season vocabulary
Practice `It's ...`, weather adjectives, seasons, months, and temperature expressions.

### II. Ask and answer about weather
Use *What's the weather like?*, *Is it ...?*, and location/season cues.

## Lesson 52 - What nationality are they?

### I. Complete questions and answers about countries and nationalities
Use plural nouns, `Where are they from?`, `What nationality are they?`, and national adjectives.

### II. Rewrite singular descriptions in the plural
Change people, countries, jobs, and possessives correctly.

## Lesson 53 - An interesting climate

### I. Use weather and time expressions
Practice climate descriptions with present simple forms, adverbs of frequency, and seasons/months.

### II. Form questions from answers
Use *What is the climate like?*, *When is it ...?*, and *Which months ...?*

## Lesson 54 - What nationality are they?

### I. Ask and answer country/nationality questions
Practice *Where do they come from?*, *What nationality are they?*, and plural short answers.

### II. Complete dialogues
Use country names, nationality adjectives, `they are`, and possessive forms.

## Lesson 55 - The Sawyer family

### I. Complete sentences with the present simple
Practice family members, daily routines, and third-person singular verbs.

### II. Make questions and negatives
Use `do/does`, `don't/doesn't`, and time expressions such as every day, in the morning, and at night.

## Lesson 56 - What do they usually do?

### I. Ask and answer about routines
Use *What do you/they usually do?* and *What does he/she usually do?*

### II. Put adverbs of frequency in the correct position
Practice `usually`, `always`, `often`, `sometimes`, and `never` with present simple verbs.

## Lesson 57 - An unusual day

### I. Contrast usual and current actions
Use present simple for routines and present continuous for actions happening now.

### II. Complete dialogues and descriptions
Practice *usually ... but today ...*, `is/are ...ing`, and time-of-day phrases.

## Lesson 58 - What's the time?

### I. Write and say times
Practice `o'clock`, `half past`, `quarter past`, `quarter to`, and minute expressions.

### II. Ask and answer questions about time
Use *What's the time?*, *What time is it?*, and routine cues.

## Lesson 59 - Is that all?

### I. Complete shopping dialogues
Use quantities, food vocabulary, `some/any`, `a/an`, and polite requests.

### II. Make questions and replies
Practice *Is that all?*, *That's all*, *How much ...?*, and *Here you are.*

## Lesson 60 - What's the time?

### I. Use clocks and time expressions
Write times in words and match time questions to routine answers.

### II. Complete daily-schedule dialogues
Use times for getting up, meals, work, school, and going to bed.

## Lesson 61 - A bad cold

### I. Complete health dialogues
Practice *What's the matter?*, *I feel ...*, *I have ...*, and advice with `must`.

### II. Use `must` and `mustn't`
Make positive and negative health instructions and short responses.

## Lesson 62 - What's the matter with them?

### I. Ask and answer about illness and symptoms
Use *What's the matter with him/her/them?* with headache, toothache, cold, and temperature vocabulary.

### II. Rewrite with object pronouns
Replace nouns with `him`, `her`, `them`, and correct possessive forms.

## Lesson 63 - Thank you, doctor

### I. Complete doctor-patient dialogues
Use symptoms, advice, medicines, `must`, `mustn't`, and polite thanks.

### II. Make imperatives and advice
Practice *Open your mouth*, *Take this medicine*, *Stay in bed*, and negative instructions.

## Lesson 64 - Don't ...?

### I. Make positive and negative imperatives
Use `Don't ...` and commands with object pronouns, places, and common actions.

### II. Translate instructions into English
Practice warnings, advice, requests, and responses.

## Lesson 65 - Not a baby

### I. Complete present-simple sentences about age and ability
Practice `can/can't`, `must/mustn't`, family vocabulary, and everyday actions.

### II. Ask and answer questions
Use *Can ...?*, *Does ...?*, and short affirmative/negative answers.

## Lesson 66 - What's the time?

### I. State exact times
Review hours and minutes, including `past` and `to` forms.

### II. Complete timetable questions
Use *What time does ...?* and present-simple routine verbs.

## Lesson 67 - The weekend

### I. Complete present-simple and time expressions
Practice weekend routines, days of the week, `on`, `at`, and time phrases.

### II. Ask and answer about habitual activities
Use *What do you do at the weekend?* and third-person forms.

## Lesson 68 - What's the time?

### I. Write times from clocks and cues
Review exact clock language and time questions.

### II. Make sentences about schedules
Use time expressions with school, work, meals, and travel.

## Lesson 69 - The car race

### I. Complete past-simple descriptions
Introduce regular past forms and `was/were` in a race/travel narrative.

### II. Change present sentences to the past
Practice `is/are -> was/were` and common regular verbs.

## Lesson 70 - When were they there?

### I. Ask and answer with `was/were`
Use *When was/were ...?*, dates, days, places, and short answers.

### II. Complete past-time sentences
Practice `yesterday`, `last ...`, `ago`, and time/location questions.

## Lesson 71 - He's awful!

### I. Complete descriptions using past forms
Practice `was/were`, adjective descriptions, and reactions to people/events.

### II. Write questions from answers
Use *Was he/she ...?*, *Were they ...?*, *How was ...?*, and short answers.

## Lesson 72 - When did you ...?

### I. Ask and answer past-simple questions
Use `did`, regular past verbs, time expressions, and full/short answers.

### II. Change statements to questions and negatives
Use `didn't` and the base form of the verb.

## Lesson 73 - The way to King Street

### I. Complete directions
Practice *Excuse me*, *How do I get to ...?*, turn left/right, go straight on, and location phrases.

### II. Give route instructions from cues
Use streets, crossroads, landmarks, and imperative direction verbs.

## Lesson 74 - What did they do?

### I. Make questions with `did`
Practice *What did he/she/they do?* and regular past answers.

### II. Complete a past-event description
Use actions, people, places, and times in the simple past.

## Lesson 75 - Uncomfortable shoes

### I. Complete past-simple dialogues
Practice irregular past forms such as `go/went`, `buy/bought`, `put/put`, and `feel/felt`.

### II. Ask and answer about a past shopping experience
Use *When did ...?*, *Where did ...?*, *Why did ...?*, and `didn't` negatives.

## Lesson 76 - When did you ...?

### I. Ask questions from past-time cues
Use `When did ...?`, `Where did ...?`, `Who did ... with?`, and regular/irregular past verbs.

### II. Rewrite statements as questions and negatives
Preserve the base form after `did/didn't`.

## Lesson 77 - Terrible toothache

### I. Complete health and dental dialogues in the past
Use `had`, `went`, `saw`, `took`, `said`, and time expressions.

### II. Give advice and report actions
Practice *You must ...*, doctor/dentist vocabulary, and past narratives.

## Lesson 78 - When did you ...?

### I. Form past-simple questions and answers
Review regular and irregular verbs with dates, days, and places.

### II. Complete dialogues
Use `did`, `didn't`, time questions, and short answers.

## Lesson 79 - Carol's shopping list

### I. Complete shopping-list and quantity dialogues
Use food, countable/uncountable nouns, `some/any`, `much/many`, and quantities.

### II. Ask and answer about items purchased
Use *How much ...?*, *How many ...?*, `bought`, and object pronouns.

## Lesson 80 - I must go to ...

### I. Complete obligation and destination sentences
Use `must`, `have to`, `go to`, places in town, and time expressions.

### II. Make questions and negatives
Practice *Must I ...?*, *Do you have to ...?*, and short responses.

## Lesson 81 - Roast beef and potatoes

### I. Complete food and meal dialogues
Use countable/uncountable nouns, quantities, offers, and preferences.

### II. Use present and past forms in restaurant/meal contexts
Practice `want/would like`, `had`, `ate`, `drank`, and polite requests.

## Lesson 82 - I had ...

### I. Complete past-meal descriptions
Use `had`, `ate`, `drank`, food vocabulary, and past-time phrases.

### II. Ask and answer about meals
Use *What did you have?*, *Did you have ...?*, and short answers.

## Lesson 83 - Going on holiday

### I. Complete future arrangements
Use `be going to`, travel vocabulary, destinations, dates, and luggage/items.

### II. Ask and answer about plans
Practice *When are you going to ...?*, *Where are you going to stay?*, and intended actions.

## Lesson 84 - Have you had ...?

### I. Form present-perfect questions and answers
Use `Have you had ...?`, `has he/she had ...?`, `have/has`, and past participles.

### II. Complete food, illness, and experience dialogues
Practice `already`, `yet`, `just`, affirmative/negative short answers, and object nouns.

## Lesson 85 - Paris in the spring

### I. Complete present-perfect travel descriptions
Use `have been`, `have visited`, `have stayed`, places, seasons, and experience vocabulary.

### II. Ask questions about experience
Use *Have you ever ...?*, *Have you been to ...?*, and short answers.

## Lesson 86 - Have you ...?

### I. Ask and answer present-perfect questions
Use `Have/Has ...?`, past participles, `yet`, `already`, and `just`.

### II. Change sentences into questions and negatives
Practice `haven't/hasn't` and correct pronoun/noun agreement.

## Lesson 87 - A car crash

### I. Complete a past narrative
Use past simple and present perfect where appropriate: `has had`, `was`, `went`, `saw`, `took`, and accident vocabulary.

### II. Ask and answer about a recent event
Practice *What happened?*, *When did it happen?*, *Has ...?*, and short replies.

## Lesson 88 - Have you ...?

### I. Use present perfect with regular and irregular participles
Practice `seen`, `done`, `been`, `gone`, `had`, `made`, `taken`, and `written`.

### II. Complete dialogues with `yet`, `already`, and `just`
Use questions, negatives, and complete replies.

## Lesson 89 - For sale

### I. Complete descriptions of items for sale
Use adjectives, prices, possession, `for sale`, condition, and place expressions.

### II. Make buying/selling dialogues
Practice questions about price, size, colour, make, condition, and ownership.

## Lesson 90 - Have you ...?

### I. Review present-perfect questions about completed actions
Use *Have you ...?*, *Has he/she ...?*, short answers, and past participles.

### II. Contrast present perfect and past simple
Use `yet/already/just` versus specific past-time expressions.

## Lesson 91 - Poor Ian!

### I. Complete a recent-event narrative
Use present perfect, past simple, injury/accident vocabulary, and reactions.

### II. Ask and answer about what has happened
Practice *What's happened?*, *Has he ...?*, and related past questions.

## Lesson 92 - When will ...?

### I. Form future questions and answers with `will`
Use *When will ...?*, *Will ...?*, `will/won't`, and time expressions.

### II. Complete future-arrangement dialogues
Practice dates, days, travel, events, and short future answers.

## Lesson 93 - Our new neighbour

### I. Complete descriptions of people and recent changes
Use present simple, present continuous, present perfect, and future forms as prompted.

### II. Ask and answer about a neighbour
Practice personal details, jobs, nationality, routines, plans, and possession.

## Lesson 94 - When did you ...?

### I. Review past-simple question forms
Use `did`, base verbs, irregular past forms, time/date/location cues, and short answers.

### II. Complete conversations about past events
Distinguish `did` questions from `was/were` questions.

## Lesson 95 - Tickets, please

### I. Complete travel and ticket dialogues
Use requests, quantities, destinations, times, return/single tickets, and polite responses.

### II. Ask and answer about journeys
Practice *Where are you going?*, *When does ... leave?*, *How much is ...?*, and present/future travel language.

## Lesson 96 - What's the exact time?

### I. State exact clock times
Use hours, minutes, `past`, `to`, `half past`, `quarter`, and exact-time questions.

### II. Complete timetable and travel questions
Use *What's the exact time?*, *What time does ...?*, *When will ...?*, and time expressions in complete answers.
