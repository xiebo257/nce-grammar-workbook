NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 48: Do you like ...? Do you want ...?

I. Give correct replies to the sentences according to the examples

  1. 1. There's a small apple on the plate. (big)

I don't want a small one. I want a big one.

  1. 2. There are some red apples in the basket. (green)

I don't want any red ones. I want some green ones.

  1. 3. This ice cream is for you. (that)
  2. 4. Here's a cup of coffee for you. (a cup of tea)
  3. 5. I'm going to give you an old handbag. (new)
  4. 6. There are some short pencils in the box. (long)
  5. 7. Here are two blue hats. (red)
  6. 8. There are some biscuits in the cupboard. (a cake)
  7. 9. Here's a cup. (a glass)
  8. 10. I'm going to buy you a bottle of beer. (wine)

II. Answer the questions according to the example

  1. 1. Do you like cheese and butter?

I like cheese, but I don't like butter.

  1. 2. Do you like green tea and black tea?
  2. 3. Do you like red wine and white wine?
  3. 4. Do you like honey and jam?
  4. 5. Do you like white clothes and black clothes?
  5. 6. Do you like oranges and bananas?
  6. 7. Do you like American cars and Korean cars?
  7. 8. Do you like English and German?
  8. 9. Do you like Mercedes and Ford?
  9. 10. Do you like cakes and biscuits?
