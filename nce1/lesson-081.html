NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 81: Roast beef and potatoes

I. Combine each pair of sentences according to the example

  1. 1. Mr. Scott usually drinks coffee for breakfast.
    Mr. Scott usually eats bread for breakfast.

Mr. Scott usually has coffee and bread for breakfast.

  1. 2. Jane usually eats eggs for breakfast.
    Jane usually drinks milk for breakfast.
  2. 3. Sam ate two sandwiches and an apple for lunch yesterday.
    Sam drank a glass of beer for lunch yesterday.
  3. 4. Ann is eating an apple for lunch.
    Ann is eating salad for lunch.
  4. 5. My friends and I ate roast beef and potatoes for dinner.
    My friends and I drank six glasses of beer for dinner.
  5. 6. I sometimes eat cheese after a meal.
    I sometimes eat biscuits after a meal.

II. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. When do you usually have a bath? (at six thirty in the morning)

I usually have a bath at six thirty in the morning.

  1. 2. When did Susan and Jack have a meal in the restaurant? (at six yesterday evening)
  2. 3. Where did you and your friends have a party two weeks ago? (in my house)
  3. 4. Who had a good time in the park last weekend? (all the boys and girls)
  4. 5. When do teachers and students have their summer holiday? (in July and August)
  5. 6. When did Tony have a swim in the sea? (last Sunday evening)
  6. 7. Where are the students having their English lesson? (in their classroom)
  7. 8. What does your child usually have for breakfast? (two slices of bread with cheese, an egg and milk)
