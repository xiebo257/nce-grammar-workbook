## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Irregular past tense | `fly`→`flew`, `go`→`went`, `see`→`saw`, `take`→`took`, `catch`→`caught` | Irregular verbs have unique past forms that must be memorized. No `-ed` ending. |
| Regular past tense | `drop`→`dropped`, `like`→`liked`, `enjoy`→`enjoyed`, `type`→`typed`, `climb`→`climbed`, `finish`→`finished`, `empty`→`emptied`, `close`→`closed` | Regular verbs: add `-ed`/`-d`/`-ied`. CVC doubling: `drop`→`dropped` (double `p`). |
| `Do you ever...?` → `Yes, [pronoun] do/does. [Pronoun] [past verb] [object] [time].` | `Yes, she does. She typed some letters the day before yesterday.` | Short answer (`do`/`does`) + past tense statement. `any`→`some` in affirmative. |
| `When did...?` → `[Pronoun] [past verb] [pronoun] [time/location].` | `They bought it at the dairy last Tuesday.` | Object replaced with pronoun (`it`/`them`/`her`). Location preserved from question. |
| `didn't` + base verb, `but` + past verb | `I didn't sit down, but Jack sat down.` | First clause: `didn't` + base form. Second clause: past tense (regular or irregular). |
| Phrasal verb pronoun placement | `turned them on` (not `turned on them`) | With pronoun objects, the pronoun goes **between** the verb and the particle. |
