Lesson 141: Sally's first train ride

I. Rewrite the sentences according to the example.

  1. Someone sweeps the classroom every day.

    The classroom is swept every day.

  2. Someone cleans the blackboard after every lesson.
  3. Someone turns on the lights in the evening.
  4. Someone washes the dishes every day.
  5. Some people paint the school regularly.
  6. Someone cuts the grass very often.
  7. Someone cooks supper at six o'clock.
  8. People sell meat at the market every day.
  9. People in Canada grow a lot of wheat.
  10. People in Brazil grow a lot of coffee.
  11. People in some parts of Canada speak French.
  12. People clean our office every day.

II. Rewrite the sentences according to the example.

  1. Someone washed the car last week.

    The car was washed last week.

  2. Someone cleaned the desks and chairs before the lesson.
  3. Someone invited him to a party.
  4. Someone met the children after school.
  5. Someone took the children to school by car.
  6. Someone repaired his car yesterday.
  7. Someone turned off the lights last night.
  8. Someone washed the dishes yesterday.
  9. Someone made the beds this morning.
  10. Tom was badly hurt in the crash. Someone took him to hospital.

III. Rewrite the sentences according to the example.

  1. People speak English all over the world.

    English is spoken all over the world.

  2. They build houses very quickly here.
  3. Someone broke that window last month.
  4. They painted the door red.
  5. Someone saw him in the park.
  6. Someone caught the thief in the shop.
  7. Someone dusts the furniture every day.
  8. Someone found the money and gave it to the policeman.
  9. Someone delivers letters to every house every morning.
  10. Someone put the vase on the top shelf.
