## Usage table

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `No, not yet, but + subject + will be + past participle + time` | not done yet, will be done | `No, not yet, but I will be served soon.` |
| `No, subject + has/have only just been + past participle` | done very recently | `No, they have only just been repaired.` |
| `you` → `I` | listener → reporter in answer | `Have you been served?` → `I will be served` |

## Key takeaway

- In Part I, the answer's subject should match the question's subject: "Have **you** been introduced?" → "**I** will be introduced." Item 5 failed because the learner changed the subject from `I` (you) to `they` (the new students).
- `only just been + past participle` is the correct pattern for "done very recently" in Part II.
- Contraction vs full form (`they've` vs `they have`) is a consistent non-scoring pattern.
