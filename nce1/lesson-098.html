NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 98: Whose is it?

I. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the examples.

1.

  1. a. Whose is this bag? Is it Mary's? (John's bag)

No, it isn't hers. It's his.

  1. b. Whose is this tie? Is it Frank's? (my tie)
  2. c. Whose is this skirt? Is it mine? (Ann's skirt)
  3. d. Whose is this flat? Is it Mr. and Mrs. Blake's? (our flat)
  4. e. Whose is this television? Is it ours? (Stephen's television)
  5. f. Whose is this shop? Is it Mr. Smith's? (Mr. and Mrs. Scott's shop)
  6. g. Whose is this hat? Is it Jane's? (Sam's hat)
  7. h. Whose is this beer? Is it mine? (Dave's beer)

2.

  1. a. Whose are these apples? Are they yours? (the woman's apples)

No, they aren't mine. They're hers.

  1. b. Whose are these keys? Are they ours? (his keys)
  2. c. Whose are these magazines? Are they Jane's? (the boys' magazines)
  3. d. Whose are these shoes? Are they yours? (my brother's shoes)
  4. e. Whose are these hammers? Are they ours? (the workers' hammers)
  5. f. Whose are these cigarettes? Are they Jane's? (Jack's cigarettes)
  6. g. Whose are these cases? Are they ours? (the tourists' cases)
  7. h. Whose are these trousers? Are they your son's? (my daughter's trousers)

II. Answer the questions according to the example.

  1. 1. Whose case is it? Is it Kathy's or yours?

It isn't hers. It's mine.

  1. 2. Whose clothes are these? Are they yours or theirs?
  2. 3. Whose car is this? Is it his or yours?
  3. 4. Whose tickets are these? Are they ours or yours?
  4. 5. Whose key is this? Is it Kathy's or yours?
  5. 6. Whose cup is this? Is it Bob's or mine?
  6. 7. Whose room is this? Is it theirs or ours?
  7. 8. Whose newspapers are these? Are they yours or Ken's?
