NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 104: Too, very, enough

I. Combine each pair of sentences according to the examples

1.

  1. a. He was very young. He couldn't go to school.

He was too young to go to school.

  1. b. The man was very old. He couldn't swim in the sea.
  2. c. She was very ill. She couldn't go to work yesterday.
  3. d. The little dog was very weak. He couldn't walk.
  4. e. The news was very good. It couldn't be true.
  5. f. The boys were very tired. They couldn't climb the wall.
  6. g. We were very late. We couldn't catch the train.
  7. h. I was very stupid. I couldn't understand the maths problem.

2.

  1. a. The box was too heavy. He couldn't lift it.

The box was too heavy for him to lift.

  1. b. The food was too hot. They couldn't eat it.
  2. c. The trousers were too short. He couldn't wear them.
  3. d. Her handwriting was too bad. We couldn't read it.
  4. e. The work was too hard. They couldn't do it.
  5. f. The shelf was too high. I couldn't reach it.
  6. g. The questions were too difficult. They couldn't answer them.
  7. h. The bag was too big. She couldn't carry it.

II. Combine each pair of sentences according to the examples

1.

  1. a. The shelf is very low. He can reach it.

The shelf is low enough for him to reach.

  1. b. The table is very small. They can move it.
  2. c. The boxes are very light. He can lift them.
  3. d. The questions are very easy. They can understand them.
  4. e. The stereo is very loud. They can hear it.
  5. f. The pear is very soft. The old woman can eat it.
  6. g. The juice is very sweet. You can drink it.
  7. h. The wall is very low. He can climb it.

2.

  1. a. The knife is very sharp. It can cut the beef.

The knife is sharp enough to cut the beef.

  1. b. I'm very tired. I can sleep for twenty-four hours.
  2. c. He's very thirsty. He can drink four glasses of water.
  3. d. I'm very hungry. I can eat five cakes.
  4. e. He is big. He can ride a bike.
  5. f. He's very rich. He can buy the house.
  6. g. She's very lucky. She can win the prize.
  7. h. He's very strong. He can move the bookcase.
