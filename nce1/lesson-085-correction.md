## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| `do` (base form) | after `going to`, after `did`, in present simple Q&A | `going to do`, `did you do`, `Do you...? / I do` |
| `did` (past tense) | simple past statements, past question auxiliary | `They did a lot of work`, `Did you have...?`, `I did it last night` |
| `done` (past participle) | after `have`/`has` in present perfect | `have done`, `has done` |
| `has been to` (present perfect) | `He's been to the newsagent's, but he's here now.` | `been to [place]` = went there and came back. **`to` is required.** |
| `Have you ever been to...?` | `Have you ever been to the United States?` | Present perfect + `ever` + `been to` + place. |
| `have never been there` | `No, I have never been there.` | Present perfect negative with `never`. |
| Mixed tenses in dialogue | `Have you ever been to X?` (present perfect) → `When did you go there?` (past simple) | Present perfect for experience; past simple for specific time. |
