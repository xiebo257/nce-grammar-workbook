## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Practical reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Joining clauses with `that` | `He says that he feels sleepy.` | Put `that` between the reporting clause and the information clause. |
| Third-person singular present | `She says`, `he thinks`, `the baby wants` | Add `-s` or `-es` with `he`, `she`, `it`, and singular nouns. |
| Past event after “what's the matter” | `He fell downstairs.` | Use the past form for a completed accident: `fell`, not `fall`. |
| `have got` | `I've got flu`, `he's got measles` | `I've/he's got` means `I have/he has got`; do not confuse it with past `had`. |
| Subject changes in cues | `What's the matter with her?` -> `She has ...` | Match the answer subject to the person named in the question. |
| Reported modal | `He believes that he can repair the car.` | Keep `can` and `must` unchanged after `that`. |
| Present perfect | `They've already finished their homework.` | Use `have/has + past participle`: `finished`, not `finish`. |
| Future in a that-clause | `I'm afraid that it will rain tomorrow.` | Use `will` for the future prediction. |
| Generic plural noun | `Students are studying hard.` | A plural noun used generally can appear without `the`. |
