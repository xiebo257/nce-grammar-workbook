III. Answer the questions according to the example

  1. 1. Did he paint the chairs?
    Did he wash the dishes?

Yes, he did. He painted them.
No, he didn't. He didn't wash them.

  3. 4. Did Martin dry the spoons?

  4. 5. Did Jane clean the floor?
    Did Tom dust the shelves?
  5. 6. Did he sharpen the knife?
    Did he empty the basket?
  6. 7. Did your mother cook the meal?
    Did she bake any bread?
  7. 8. Did you turn on the tap?

  8. 9. Did Mary telephone her boss?

  9. 10. Did you open the door?
