Lesson 113: Small change

I. Give correct replies to the sentences according to the examples.

    1. Ken can speak English.

      So can I.

    2. He can read English.
    3. She had a swim yesterday.
    4. Bob is tall.
    5. David will go.
    6. He is learning English.
    7. She has read that book.
    1. Sam can't speak Chinese.

      Neither can I.

    2. She can't write German.
    3. They don't like chocolate.
    4. Jack won't sing.
    5. Tony didn't go.
    6. John couldn't hear it.
    7. Mary hasn't read it.

II. Join the sentences according to the example.

  1. They all smiled. I smiled, too.

    They all smiled, and so did I.

  2. He is tall. You are tall, too.
  3. My shoes are dirty. My trousers are dirty, too.
  4. This pencil is blunt. That one is blunt, too.
  5. You must come to the party. Your husband must come, too.
  6. He went away. His father went away, too.
  7. I like tomatoes. My friends like tomatoes, too.
  8. She goes to school every day. Her sister goes to school every day, too.
  9. He sits in the front. His brother sits there, too.
  10. I will have a haircut. My father will have one, too.
  11. You have finished. We have finished, too.
  12. He is here. His friends are here, too.

III. Make sentences according to the example.

  1. She doesn't know. /I

    She doesn't know, and I don't know, either. She doesn't know, and neither do I.

  2. I don't want to go. /she
  3. They haven't done their homework. /he

  8. You aren't very old. /he
  9. Your town isn't very big. /mine
  10. She hasn't got any ink. /I
