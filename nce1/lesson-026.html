Lesson 26: Where is it?

I. Fill in the blanks with a, an, or the

  1. Is there ___ book on ___ table?

Yes, there is. There is ___ big red book. Give me ___ book, please. Here you are.

  1. There are some glasses here. ___ glasses on ___ shelf are clean. ___ glasses on ___ table are dirty.
  2. Look at John's new car. What make is it?

It's ___ Fiat.

  1. My new friend is ___ American student.
  2. Is this your pen? Is it ___ blue one?

Yes, it's ___ blue one. It's my pen. Thank you.

  1. There is ___ orange bottle in the refrigerator. ___ bottle is full.
  2. On ___ right there is ___ television and on ___ left there is ___ desk.
  3. There's ___ cigarette shop and it's open.
  4. There's ___ electric cooker in our kitchen. It's new and it's white.
  5. My father is ___ engineer. I am ___ son of ___ engineer.
