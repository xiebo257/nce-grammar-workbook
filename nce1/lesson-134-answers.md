# NCE Grammar Practice 1 - Lesson 134: He said (that) he ... He told me (that) he ...

Exported: 2026/9/23 18:39:11

## Answers

### Question 1

**Prompt:** "She has just won a prize. She is very lucky."What did he tell you?

**Your answer:**

He told me she had just won a prize and she was very lucky.

### Question 2

**Prompt:** "We are going to move into a new flat."What did she tell you?

**Your answer:**

She told me they were going to move into a new flat.

### Question 3

**Prompt:** "My son came back to see me from New York."What did she tell you?

**Your answer:**

She told me her son had come back to see her from New York.

### Question 4

He told me he was drinking coffee with his friends.
