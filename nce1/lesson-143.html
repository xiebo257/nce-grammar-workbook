NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 143: A walk through the woods

I. Give correct replies according to the example.

  1. Sweep the floor, please.

    I don't need to sweep the floor. It has already been swept.

  2. Clean the cooker, please.
  3. Clean the carpet, please.
  4. Wash the dishes, please.
  5. Dust the table, please.
  6. Water the flowers, please.
  7. Make some coffee, please.
  8. Type the letters, please.
  9. Put the newspapers on the shelf, please.
  10. Open the windows, please.

II. Make sentences according to the example.

  1. new shop/open/tomorrow

    The new shop will be opened tomorrow.

  2. the job/do/soon
  3. your shoes/repair/tomorrow
  4. the highest building in the city/build/next year
  5. children/send to school/next week
  6. wall/paint/green/next week
  7. refrigerator/repair/next Monday
  8. two new films/show/in the city/next month
  9. pictures/put up on the wall/soon
  10. party/hold/in two days' time

III. Write dialogues according to the example.

  1. repair your car/soon

    Has anyone repaired your car yet?
    No, it hasn't been repaired yet, but it will be repaired soon.

  2. catch the thief/this week
  3. empty the litter bins/tomorrow
  4. repair the telephone line/next week
  5. water the garden/this evening
  6. tell them the good news/this evening
