# NCE Grammar Practice 1 - Lesson 122: Who (whom), which and that

Exported: 2026/9/20 21:21:48

## Answers

### Question 1
**Prompt:** The books were very expensive. /I bought the books.
**Your answer:** The books which I bought were very expensive.

### Question 2
**Prompt:** The trees fell down. /The trees were big.
**Your answer:** The trees which were big fell down.

### Question 3
**Prompt:** The dresses were too expensive. /She wanted the dresses.
**Your answer:** The dresses which she wanted were too expensive.

### Question 4
**Prompt:** She wants a doll for her birthday. /The doll walks and talks.
**Your answer:** She wants a doll which walks and talks for her birthday.

### Question 5
**Prompt:** I've lost the umbrella. /I bought the umbrella last week.
**Your answer:** I've lost the umbrella which I bought last week.

### Question 6
**Prompt:** I passed the exams. /The exams were not very difficult.
**Your answer:** I passed the exams which were not very difficult.

### Question 7
**Prompt:** This is the car. /I've just bought the car.
**Your answer:** This is the car which I've just bought.

### Question 8
**Prompt:** The street is called Fifth Avenue. /We are walking along the street.
**Your answer:** The street which we are walking along is called Fifth Avenue.

### Question 9
**Prompt:** The store is across the street. /The store is the biggest in this city.
**Your answer:** The store which is the biggest in this city is across the street.

### Question 10
**Prompt:** The teacher was standing at the front. We handed the papers to the teacher.
**Your answer:** The teacher who we handed the papers to was standing at the front.

### Question 11
**Prompt:** I'm looking for someone. /He has a mobile phone to sell.
**Your answer:** I'm looking for someone who has a mobile phone to sell.

### Question 12
**Prompt:** The young man is a doctor. /We just saw the young man.
**Your answer:** The young man who we just saw is a doctor.

### Question 13
**Prompt:** The person helped me./The person was a stranger.
**Your answer:** The person who was a stranger helped me.

### Question 14
**Prompt:** The man had run away with a lot of money. /The police wanted the man.
**Your answer:** The man who the police wanted had run away with a lot of money.

### Question 15
**Prompt:** At last I met the man. /I had heard so much about the man.
**Your answer:** At last I met the man who I had heard so much about.

### Question 16
**Prompt:** The policemen are on traffic duty./The policemen look very tired.
**Your answer:** The policemen who look very tired are on traffic duty.

### Question 17
**Prompt:** This is the girl. /The girl found your money.
**Your answer:** This is the girl who found your money.

### Question 18
**Prompt:** They looked for the men. /The men were selling tickets.
**Your answer:** They looked for the men who were selling tickets.
