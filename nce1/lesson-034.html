NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 34: What are they doing?

I. Answer the questions using the cues

  1. What are you looking at? (we/the boats)

We're looking at the boats. Where are the boats? (on the river) They are on the river.

  1. What are Linda and her sister waiting for? (they/the bus)

Where are they waiting? (at the bus stop)

  1. What are the children doing? (they/their homework)

Where are they doing their homework? (in their room)

  1. What are the dogs eating? (they/bones)

Where are they eating the bones? (in the garden)

  1. What are the girls doing? (they/look at a dress)

What colour is the dress? (red)

  1. What are you doing? (we/wait for the next class)

Who is your teacher? (Miss Scott)

  1. What are Mr. and Mrs. Wilson doing? (they/watch television)

What are they watching? (the news)

  1. What are the babies doing? (they/sleep)

Where are they sleeping? (in their bedroom)
