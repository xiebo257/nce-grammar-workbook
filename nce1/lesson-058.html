Lesson 58: What's the time?

I. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the examples

1.

  1. a. What are you doing now? (read a newspaper)
    Do you read a newspaper every day? (often)

I'm reading a newspaper.
No, I don't read a newspaper every day, but I often read a newspaper.

  1. b. What are you doing now? (write a letter to my mother)
    Do you write home every week? (sometimes)
  2. c. What is she doing now? (clean her shoes)
    Does she clean her shoes every day? (every week)
  3. d. What is your sister doing now? (sweep the floor)
    Does she often sweep the floor? (sometimes)
  4. e. What are the students doing now? (watch television)
    Do they always watch television at night? (sometimes)

2.

  1. a. What is the postman doing now? (deliver letters)
    Does he often deliver letters at eight o'clock in the morning? (often at eight thirty)

He is delivering letters.
No, he doesn't. He often delivers letters at eight thirty in the morning.

  1. b. What is your husband doing now? (take our son to school)
    Does he take your son to school at this time every morning? (usually at seven)
  2. c. What are you doing now? (wait for a bus)
    Do you usually wait for a bus at eight o'clock? (usually at seven thirty)
  3. d. What are the children doing now? (do their homework)
    Do they always do their homework in the afternoon? (usually in the evening)
  4. e. What is the keyboard operator doing now? (type letters)
    Does he often type letters at noon? (usually at nine in the morning)
