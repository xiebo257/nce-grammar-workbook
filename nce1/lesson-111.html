NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 111: The most expensive model

I. Put the adjectives given in the comparative or superlative form.

  1. Ann is _____ (old) than Hans.
  2. Summer is _____ (warm) than winter.
  3. Summer is the _____ (warm) of the four seasons.
  4. These cars are the _____ (expensive) in China.
  5. An aeroplane is _____ (fast) than a train, and a train is _____ (fast) than a car, so an aeroplane is the _____ (fast) of the three.
  6. This is the _____ (bad) coffee I have ever tried.
  7. You are no _____ (bad) than I am at singing. I think I am the _____ (bad) singer in the world!
  8. Your work is much _____ (good) than it was last week.

II. Make sentences, using the information given according to the example.

  1. That book is interesting but this book is very interesting.

    This book is more interesting than that one.
    That book is less interesting than this one.

  2. This girl is beautiful but that girl is very beautiful.
  3. That armchair is comfortable but this armchair is very comfortable.
  4. The girl is careless but the boy is very careless.
  5. The first question is difficult but the second question is very difficult.
  6. My son is hard-working but my daughter is very hard-working.
  7. That dress is expensive but this dress is very expensive.
  8. This driver is careful but that driver is very careful.

III. Make sentences, using the information given according to the examples.

  1. the chair on the left/the chair in the middle (comfortable/of all)

    The chair on the left is the most comfortable of all the chairs, and the chair in the middle is the least comfortable.

  2. this picture/that picture (beautiful/I've ever seen)

    This picture is the most beautiful I've ever seen, and that picture is the least beautiful.

  3. your book/his book (interesting/I've ever read)
  4. this engineer/that engineer (hard-working/I've ever met)
  5. this hairdresser/that hairdresser (careful/of all)
  6. the red car/the black car (expensive/I've ever seen)
  7. the French paper/the Maths paper (difficult/of all)
  8. this man/that man (important/of all)

IV. Make sentences, using the cues given according to the example.

  1. our room/dirty/your room/not as clean

    Our room is dirtier than your room.
    Our room is not as clean as your room.

  2. this watch/expensive/that watch/not as cheap
  3. his handwriting/bad/her handwriting/not as good
  4. a boat/slow/a car/not as fast
  5. a cigar/big/a cigarette/not as small
  6. this book/thin/that book/not as thick
  7. this question/difficult/that question/not as easy
