NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 125: Tea for two

I. Answer the questions according to the example.

  1. Do you have to clean the blackboard today?

    Yes, I have to clean it today and I shall have to clean it tomorrow, too.

  2. Do you have to see the doctor today?
  3. Do I have to cook lunch today?
  4. Does he have to serve in the shop today?
  5. Do you have to see the dentist today?
  6. Does he have to wear that tie today?
  7. Does she have to help her mother today?
  8. Do you have to water the garden today?

II. Answer the questions according to the examples.

    1. Do you have to take an examination today?

      No, I don't have to take one today, but I had to take one yesterday.

    2. Does she have to type any letters today?

      No, she doesn't have to type any today, but she had to type some yesterday.

    3. Do I have to water the flowers today?
    4. Do you have to clean your bike today?
    5. Do we have to clean the windows today?
    6. Does he have to buy some butter today?
    1. Do you have to go to school today?

      No, I don't, and I won't have to go to school tomorrow, either.

    2. Do I have to go to bed early today?
    3. Do you have to do your homework today?
    4. Does she have to wash any clothes today?
    5. Do I have to do the shopping today?
    6. Do we have to hand in our papers today?

III. Answer the questions according to the example.

  1. Do I have to wear shoes and socks?

    You must wear shoes but you don't need to wear socks.

  2. Do I have to wash my hands and face?
  3. Do we have to close the door and the windows?
  4. Does he have to wear a shirt and a tie?
  5. Do we have to go to school today and tomorrow?
  6. Do we have to read the book and answer the questions?
  7. Do I have to clean my teeth and wash my hair?
  8. Do you have to cook vegetables and fish for supper?
  9. Does she have to look after the baby and the cat?
  10. Do I have to put on my coat and hat?
