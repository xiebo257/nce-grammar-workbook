Lesson 109: A good idea

  2. 2. many/much
  3. 3. dirty
  4. 4. clean
  5. 5. few
  6. 6. slow
  7. 7. bad

II. Complete the dialogues according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Would you like some butter?
    Would you like some milk?

Yes, please. I'd like some butter. I've got very little.

  2. c. Would you like some ink?

  3. d. Would you like some tea?

III. Fill in the blanks with little, a little, few or a few

  1. 1. Would you like some tickets? I have ... to spare.
  2. 2. He is very rich, but he is so mean that ... people know he is rich.
  3. 3. I've got very ... money. Could you lend me ...?
  4. 4. She is not popular; ... people like her.
  5. 5. He didn't like the job, so he left the company after ... months.
  6. 6. May I have ... more sugar in my tea, please?
  7. 7. We haven't finished yet; there is still ... more work to do.
  8. 8. I made very ... mistakes in the test.
  9. 9. ... flowers can make a room look brighter.
  10. 10. We have ... milk left. We need to buy some.
  11. 11. There are only ... cigarettes in the box.
  12. 12. There is only ... tea left in the teapot.
  13. 13. Your homework today is better than it was yesterday; you have made only ... mistakes.
  14. 14. Jane gave me ... help with my work.

IV. Make dialogues according to the example

  1. 1. I/got a good mark in the exam/John/Jane

I got a good mark in the exam.
You're right. But John got a better mark. And Jane got the best mark of all.
