# NCE Grammar Practice 1 - Lesson 120: It had already happened.

Exported: 2026/9/19 21:00:46

## Answers

### Question 1
**Prompt:** The class started. Bob reached school.
**Your answer:** When Bob reached school, the class had already started.

### Question 2
**Prompt:** They had dinner. I got back home.
**Your answer:** When I got back home, they had already had dinner.

### Question 3
**Prompt:** The rain stopped. She opened her umbrella.
**Your answer:** When she opened her umbrella, the rain had already stopped.
