## Answers

### Question 1

**Prompt:** The chair broke. He was sitting on it.

**Your answer:**

The chair he was sitting on broke.

### Question 2

**Prompt:** Show me the book. You got the book from the library.

**Your answer:**

Show me the book you got from the library.
