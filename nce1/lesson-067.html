NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 67: The weekend

I. Fill in the blanks with was or were

  1. 1. It __________ cold on Monday.
  2. 2. I __________ at school on Friday.
  3. 3. What __________ the weather like on Wednesday?
  4. 4. John and Paul __________ very busy last week.
  5. 5. The woman __________ at the hairdresser's on Wednesday.
  6. 6. My dog __________ not well on Tuesday.
  7. 7. __________ you at home on Saturday?
  8. 8. Susan __________ in the country for the weekend.
  9. 9. I __________ at my teacher's with my friend on Thursday.
  10. 10. Where __________ you at about two thirty on Monday?

II. Give short answers to the questions according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Was it May 16th on Saturday?

Yes, it was.

  1. b. Was it hot last week?
  2. c. Was your daughter ill on Monday?
  3. d. Were you in bed at eleven on Saturday?
  4. e. Was it a fine day on Sunday?
  5. f. Was he in class last week?
  6. g. Was she busy on Wednesday?
  7. h. Was John all right on Friday?

2.

  1. a. Were you at the greengrocer's on Thursday?

No, I wasn't.

  1. b. Were you at school on Friday?
  2. c. Was it cold at the weekend?
  3. d. Were you at home on June 30th?
  4. e. Was she at the dentist's on Thursday?
  5. f. Was Dave in the library on Wednesday?
  6. g. Was Jane tired on Monday?
  7. h. Was it July 25th on Tuesday?

III. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Were you at school last week?
    Was Mary at school last week, too? (at the office)

Yes, I was.
No, she wasn't. She was at the office.

  1. b. Was Jack absent from school on Tuesday?
    Was Jill absent from school on Tuesday, too? (at school)
  2. c. Was it fine on Thursday?
    Was it fine on Friday, too? (cloudy)
  3. d. Were you at your mother's on Monday?
    Was your sister at your mother's on Monday, too? (at the office)
  4. e. Was Ann at the grocer's at five thirty on Saturday?
    Was her husband at the grocer's at five thirty on Saturday, too? (at the hairdresser's)

2.

  1. a. Were you at the stationer's at twenty to ten?
    Was John there at that time?

No, I wasn't.
No; he wasn't there, either.

  1. b. Was Ann at school on Friday?
    Was Lisa at school on that day?
  2. c. Was it windy last week?
    Was it rainy last week?
  3. d. Were you at the doctor's at eight forty on Tuesday?
    Were you at the dentist's at that time on that day?
  4. e. Was John at home on Saturday?
    Was he at home on Sunday?
