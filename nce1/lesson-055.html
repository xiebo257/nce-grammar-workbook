Lesson 55: The Sawyer family

I. Fill in the blanks with the where necessary

  1. 1. Miss Tan goes to ... work by car every day. She arrives at ... office at eight in ... morning, and she comes home late in ... evening.
  2. 2. Mr. and Mrs. Smith usually stay at ... home in ... afternoon.
  3. 3. That new student is Austrian. He comes from ... Austria.
  4. 4. They often come home late at ... night.
  5. 5. What's ... climate like in your country?
    It's interesting. It's often cold in ... North and windy in ... East.
  6. 6. Do you often eat ... lamb?
    No, I don't. I don't like ... lamb. But I like ... beef.
  7. 7. To tell you ... truth, Mr. James doesn't shave every day.
  8. 8. Our cat drinks ... milk, but it doesn't drink ... water.
  9. 9. Can you give me a hammer, please?
    Which one? This small one?
    No, not ... small one. ... big one.
  10. 10. ... Sawyers usually watch ... television every evening.

II. Fill in each blank with the correct form of the verb given

  1. Tim's Day
    Tim ... (wake) up at eight o'clock in the morning, and ... (get) up at half past eight. He ... (wash) his face and hands and ... (clean) his teeth. Then he ... (go) downstairs for breakfast. He ... (eat) two eggs and some bread and butter, and he ... (drink) two or three cups of coffee.

    After breakfast he ... (walk) to Mr. Scott's house. He ... (be) often late. He ... (show) Mr. Scott his homework, and there ... (be) often mistakes in it.

    For lunch at noon, he usually ... (eat) three pieces of steak and tomatoes or beans. After lunch he always ... (drink) a cup of tea with sugar in it. He sometimes ... (sit) in an armchair and ... (read) a newspaper, and sometimes, but not often, he ... (do) some work for Mr. Scott.

    For supper in the evening, he ... (eat) some slices of bread, butter, honey and cakes. Then he ... (watch) television or ... (talk) to friends.

III. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. Do you usually go to school by bus or by bike? (by bike)

I usually go to school by bike.

  1. 2. Do your classmates often ask questions in class or after class? (after class)
  2. 3. Are your teachers always busy or only sometimes? (always)
  3. 4. Does your father usually go to work early or late? (early)
  4. 5. Does your friend watch television or do his homework in the evening? (do his homework)
  5. 6. Does your mother go to the shops every day or every week? (every day)
  6. 7. Do you often play basketball or football in the afternoon? (football)
  7. 8. Does she usually go to school in the morning or in the afternoon? (in the morning)
  8. 9. Does Jane go to bed early or late every evening? (early)
  9. 10. Do you always go out with friends or stay at home in the evening? (stay at home)
