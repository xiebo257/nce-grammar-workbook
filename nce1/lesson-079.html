NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 79: Carol's shopping list

I. Fill in the blanks with much, many, some, any or a lot of

  1. 1. John drinks __________ coffee every day.
  2. 2. I haven't got __________ time for study.
  3. 3. I have __________ black ink, but I don't have __________ blue ink at all.
  4. 4. We need __________ groceries this week.
  5. 5. Are there __________ flowers in your garden in autumn?
  6. 6. There is not __________ chalk in the box on the teacher's desk. We need some.
  7. 7. I bought __________ clothes in Beijing last year.
  8. 8. Jane doesn't know __________ people in London and she hasn't got __________ friends there.
  9. 9. He borrowed __________ books from the library yesterday for the long holiday.
  10. 10. The old woman was poor. She did not have __________ money and often did not have __________ food to eat, but she was happy. “I have __________ friends,” she said, “and I don't have __________ enemies. That is the secret of happiness. People who have __________ money often have __________ enemies, too!”

II. Answer the questions according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Have you got a pencil and a ruler?

I've got a pencil, but I haven't got a ruler.

  1. b. Has he got a shirt and a tie?
  2. c. Have they got a book and a map?
  3. d. Has she got a cup and a glass?
  4. e. Has Jane got a spoon and a fork?
  5. f. Have you got a desk and a chair?

2.

  1. a. Have you got any boxes and baskets?

I've got some boxes, but I haven't got any baskets.

  1. b. Have they got any apples and bananas?
  2. c. Has he got any stamps and envelopes?
  3. d. Have we got any peas and beans?
  4. e. Has she got any cakes and biscuits?
  5. f. Has Sam got any books and magazines?

3.

  1. a. Have you got any milk and water?

I've got some milk, but I haven't got any water.

  1. b. Has he got any lamb and mince?
  2. c. Has she got any honey and sugar?
  3. d. Have we got any cheese and jam?
  4. e. Have they got any paper and glue?
  5. f. Has Ann got any tea and juice?

4.

  1. a. Have you got many eggs and chickens?

I've got a lot of eggs, but I haven't got many chickens.

  1. b. Has he got many bottles of beer and bottles of wine?
  2. c. Have they got many newspapers and magazines?
  3. d. Has she got many pears and peaches?
  4. e. Has he got many pens and watches?
  5. f. Have you got many cabbages and lettuces?

5.

  1. a. Have you got much paper and money?

I've got a lot of paper, but I haven't got much money.

  1. b. Have you got much medicine and water?
  2. c. Has she got much butter and sugar?
  3. d. Have they got much lamb and beef?
  4. e. Has he got much tobacco and whisky?
  5. f. Has he got much beer and tea?
