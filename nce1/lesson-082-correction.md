## Key takeaway

- **Q7 — spelling `Germen` → `German`.** The adjective is "German" (G-e-r-m-a-n), not "Germen." "Germen" is not a word. The learner may have confused the ending with "men" (plural of "man"). **Rule: nationality adjectives don't change form: German, French, Italian, English — always the same spelling.**
- **Q11/Q14/Q16/Q17 — missing `and` in `did, too` pattern.** The pattern is: `[Subject1] [verb] ..., and [Subject2] did, too.` The conjunction `and` is required to join the two clauses. Without it, the sentence has a comma splice (two independent clauses joined by only a comma). **Rule: always include `and` before `did, too` / `must, too` / `do, too`.**
- **`have` patterns — fully mastered.** The past (`had`/`didn't have`), present (`has`/`doesn't`), continuous (`are having`/`aren't`), modal (`can have`/`can't`, `must have`), and `some`→`any` shift are all correct.
- **Parallel structures — fully mastered.** `did, too` (both affirmative), `doesn't`/`didn't`/`aren't`/`can't` (one affirmative, one negative) are correctly applied with matching tenses.
