## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Subject pronoun matching | `What is he going to do?` → `He's going to…` / `What are you going to do?` → `I'm going to…` | The answer's subject must match the question's subject: `you`→`I'm`, `he`→`He's`, `she`→`She's`, `they`→`They're`, `[name]`→`He's`/`She's`. |
| `going to` future | `I'm going to buy some sweets.` | Form: `subject + am/is/are going to + base verb`. |
| Double-object form 1: verb + indirect + direct | `give the old woman some money` | `Please give [indirect object] [direct object].` |
| Double-object form 2: verb + pronoun + direct | `give her some money` | `Please give [pronoun] [direct object].` Indirect object → pronoun. |
| Double-object form 3: verb + direct + to + indirect | `give some money to the old woman` | `Please give [direct object] to [indirect object].` |
| Double-object form 4: verb + direct + to + pronoun | `give some money to her` | `Please give [direct object] to [pronoun].` |
| Section III two-form answer | `I'm going to give tea to my guests.` / `I'm going to give them tea.` | Form 1: direct + `to` + indirect. Form 2: pronoun + direct. |
