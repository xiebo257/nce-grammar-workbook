Grammar practice · 语法练习

Patterns: Whose is this/that ...?; This/That is my/your/his/her ... .

I. Rewrite following the example

Example: Whose shirt is that? -> Whose is that shirt?

  1. Whose pencil is this?
  2. Whose tie is that?
  3. Whose coat is this?
  4. Whose umbrella is that?
  5. Whose blouse is that?
  6. Whose car is this?
  7. Whose watch is this?
  8. Whose handbag is that?
  9. Whose dress is this?

II. Answer using the cue

Example: Whose is that blue shirt? Is it Tim's? (Steven) -> No, it isn't Tim's. It's Steven's.

  1. Whose is that black umbrella? Is it Tony's? (my brother)
  2. Whose is this dirty coat? Is it Robert's? (Emma)
  3. Whose is that new Ford? Is it your father's? (that mechanic)
  4. Whose is this small handbag? Is it Ruth's? (Diana)
  5. Whose is that long blouse? Is it your sister's? (Helen)
  6. Whose is this white skirt? Is it your sister's? (my mother)
  7. Whose is this old house? Is it William's? (Maria)
  8. Whose is that fat dog? Is it Sophie's? (my daughter)
  9. Whose is that nice tie? Is it Paul's? (my brother)
