A. He’s, She’s or It’s

II. Answer using the cue

Example: Is that mechanic clean or dirty? (dirty) -> He isn't clean. He's dirty.

  1. Is that policewoman young or old? (young)
  2. Are you cold or hot? (cold)
  3. Is that keyboard operator tall or short? (tall)
  4. Is your handbag new or old? (old)
  5. Is a Volvo French or Swedish? (Swedish)
  6. Is Steven fat or thin? (fat)
  7. Is that boy good or bad at school? (good)
  8. Is that book thick or thin? (thick)
  9. Is your shirt big or small? (small)
