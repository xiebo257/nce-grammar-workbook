Lesson 110: How do they compare?

I. Make sentences, using the information given according to the example

  1. 1. Jane has three books; Mary has five books; Ann has eight books.

Mary has more books than Jane does, but she has fewer books than Ann does.
Ann has the most books of the three, and Jane has the fewest.

  1. 2. Jane has bought four skirts; Mary has bought six skirts; Ann has bought two skirts.
  2. 3. Jane has got two handbags; Mary has got one handbag; Ann has got three handbags.
  3. 4. Jane has sixty dollars; Mary has one hundred dollars; Ann has one hundred and fifty dollars.
  4. 5. Jane had seven bars of chocolate; Mary had four bars of chocolate; Ann had three bars of chocolate.
  5. 6. Jane ate two slices of bread; Mary ate one slice of bread; Ann ate four slices of bread.
  6. 7. Jane drank one bottle of beer; Mary drank four bottles of beer; Ann drank two bottles of beer.
  7. 8. Jane has written two letters; Mary has written three letters; Ann has written four letters.
  8. 9. Jane had one piece of steak; Mary had two pieces of steak; Ann had three pieces of steak.
  9. 10. Jane made two mistakes; Mary made five mistakes; Ann made three mistakes.
