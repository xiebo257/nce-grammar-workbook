NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 126: Have to and do not need to

I. Complete the sentences by adding "have to", "don’t need to" or "didn’t need to".

  1. I _____ buy both books so I bought only one.
  2. We _____ hurry. We are very late.
  3. We _____ hurry. There is plenty of time.
  4. He _____ do any homework last night because he had finished it at school.
  5. You _____ take a taxi. There is a bus coming.
  6. She _____ get up early yesterday because it was a holiday, so she stayed in bed till 10 o'clock.
  7. You _____ stay behind to help me with my work. I can do it by myself.
  8. I _____ take a taxi. I can't wait for a bus.
  9. You _____ write home because you are going home tomorrow.
  10. I _____ buy any new shoes because my mother bought two pairs for me yesterday.
  11. He _____ do any work yesterday because his brother had done it all for him.
  12. You _____ do all the work by yourself. I can help you if you want.

II. Make dialogues, using the cues given according to the example.

  1. clean the blackboard

    I must clean the blackboard now because I'm on duty today.

    No, you don't need to do it because I'll do it for you.
    Then I shall have to clean it tomorrow.

    No, you won't have to do it then, either, because I'll clean it for you.
