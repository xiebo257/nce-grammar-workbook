### Question 1

**Prompt:** The children __________ (leave) home last Saturday morning at nine o'clock. The bus __________ (go) very slowly, so they __________ (arrive) at the beach at about nine thirty. They __________ (cook) some food and __________ (eat) it quickly. They also __________ (drink) cold drinks. Then some of them __________ (play) on the sand, and others __________ (sleep).
