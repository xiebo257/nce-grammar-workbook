## Key takeaway

- **Q16 — spelling `out` → `our`.** One letter different: o-u-**t** → o-u-**r**. "Out" (adverb/preposition) vs "our" (possessive pronoun). A common confusion — proofread possessive pronouns carefully.
- **Q17 — singular/plural pronoun `them` → `it` for "their house".** "House" is singular → `it`, not `them`. The learner correctly used `them` for plural objects in Q12 ("some nice cars"), Q13 ("two bars of chocolate"), Q14 ("some letters") — but incorrectly used `them` for the singular "their house." **Rule: check the noun's number before choosing `it` or `them`. "House" = singular → `it`.** This is the reverse of the L71 Q30 error (where `it` was used for plural "windows" → should be `them`).
- **Q20 — missing `an` before "envelope".** "Envelope" starts with a vowel sound → takes `an`: "an envelope." The cue includes the article — preserve it in the answer.
- **Q26 — spelling `but` → `buy` + `refigerator` → `refrigerator`.** Two spelling errors: (1) `but` (conjunction) vs `buy` (verb) — missing `y`. (2) `refigerator` missing the first `r`: r-e-f-**r**-i-g-e-r-a-t-o-r. "Refrigerator" has two `r`s.
