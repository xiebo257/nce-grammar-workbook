NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 16: Are you ...?

I. Answer using the cues

Example: Are you two Russian? (Dutch) -> No, we aren't. We're Dutch.

  1. Are the tourists English? (German)
  2. Are you two Swedish? (Italian)
  3. Are the students Japanese? (Korean)
  4. Are you two American? (German)
  5. Are their handbags black? (white)
  6. Are your cars German? (Japanese)
  7. Are our passports red? (green)
  8. Are these cars new? (old)
  9. Are your hats big? (small)

II. Answer using the alternatives in brackets

Example: Are they American or English? (English) -> They aren't American. They're English.

  1. Are their ties orange or yellow? (orange)
  2. Are you two Danish or Dutch? (Danish)
  3. Are you two English or French? (French)
  4. Are your shirts dirty or clean? (clean)
  5. Are the dogs grey or white? (white)
  6. Are the cases black or brown? (brown)
  7. Are the blouses big or small? (big)
  8. Are they young or old? (old)
  9. Are you two boys cold or hot? (hot)

III. Rewrite the sentences in the plural according to the example

Example: I am Danish. -> We are Danish.

  1. It's an umbrella.
  2. Is he a hairdresser?
  3. Here it is.
  4. This is my son.
  5. Is your brother tall?
  6. Here is your ticket.
  7. Is she a keyboard operator?
  8. My friend is Norwegian.
  9. Is this your passport?
  10. What colour's your tie?
  11. Is this your blouse?
  12. His shirt's white.
  13. Whose is this book?
  14. How is your daughter?
  15. This pen is Paul's.
  16. Are you a Customs officer?
  17. I'm a nurse.
  18. What make is this car?
  19. What colour's your coat?
