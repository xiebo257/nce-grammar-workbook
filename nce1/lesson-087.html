IV. Complete the dialogues, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. your friend/buy a watch/last week

Has your friend bought a watch?
Yes, he has.
When did he buy it?
He bought it last week.

  1. 2. you/see the cat/a minute ago
    Yes, I have.
    When did you see it?
  2. 3. they/eat the chicken/yesterday evening
    Yes, they have.
    When did they eat it?
  3. 4. she/do the housework/at about four o'clock
    Yes, she has.
    When did she do it?
  4. 5. he/have a cigarette/two minutes ago
    Yes, he has.
    When did he have one?
  5. 6. they/find their books/half an hour ago
    Yes, they have.
    When did they find them?
  6. 7. they/have a party/last Friday evening
    Yes, they have.
    When did they have one?
  7. 8. she/go to the baker's/at nine this morning
    Yes, she has.
    When did she go there?
  8. 9. you/meet your new neighbours/yesterday morning
    Yes, I have.
    When did you meet them?
  9. 10. Sophie/drive a Toyota/a year ago
    Yes, she has.
    When did she drive one?
