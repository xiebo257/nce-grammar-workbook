Lesson 116: Every, no, any and some

I. Fill in the blanks with compounds of "every", "no", "any" or "some".

  1. There is _____ interesting in the newspaper today. You must read it!
  2. Can _____ read French? We've got _____ here in French, but we can't understand _____ in French.
  3. He went _____ yesterday. He stayed at home.
  4. I know _____ here. I'm a stranger myself.
  5. Is there _____ cheap in the shop? I haven't got much money.
  6. Are you going _____ now?
    Yes, I'm going _____ for lunch.
  7. Mrs. Jones is very lazy. _____ in her house is dirty and untidy.
  8. They are looking for you _____. They've got _____ important to tell you.
  9. He goes _____ by aeroplane. He thinks an aeroplane is the fastest.
  10. _____ helped me. I did it myself.
  11. These poor people have _____ to live. They have no home.
  12. I can remember _____ about it. I've forgotten all about it.
  13. There must be _____ in the room. I heard a noise.
  14. Can you see _____ in the sky?
    Yes, some stars.
  15. I'm very thirsty. I want to buy _____ to drink.
  16. Have you seen Mike?
    Yes, I saw him in front of the library just now. He must be _____ in the library now.
