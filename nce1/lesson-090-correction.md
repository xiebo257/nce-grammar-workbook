## Key takeaway

- **Q19 — `We're` vs `We've`.** `We're` = `We are` (present of `be`); `We've` = `We have` (present perfect auxiliary). "We **are** had" is ungrammatical — it would be passive voice ("we are had" = "someone has us"), not present perfect. The correct form is "We **have** had" = "We've had" (present perfect: `have` + `had` = possession over time). **Rule: `We've` = `We have` (auxiliary for present perfect); `We're` = `We are` (main verb `be` or continuous auxiliary). Never combine `We're` with a past participle for present perfect.**
