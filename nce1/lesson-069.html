II. Complete the dialogues, using the cues given according to the examples

  1. 1. Were you and your friends in the country last weekend? (yes)
    When were you there? (from ten in the morning to four in the afternoon)

Yes, we were.
We were there from ten in the morning to four in the afternoon.

  1. 2. Were Tom and Mary absent from school last Friday? (yes)
    Where were they? (home)

Yes, they were.
They were at home.

  1. 3. Were you and your wife at the car race on May 16th? (no)
    Where were you? (the park)
  2. 4. Were they at Jane's birthday party on Monday evening? (yes)
    When were they there? (from eight thirty to nine thirty)
  3. 5. Were they in Greece in August 1999? (no)
    Where were they? (Austria)
  4. 6. Were you in Turkey in 2000? (no)
    Where were you? (Japan)
  5. 7. Were you at the baker's on Tuesday afternoon? (yes)
    When were you there? (about five)
  6. 8. Were they at the office on Sunday morning? (no)
    Where were they? (church)

III. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. Were the boys out or at home last night? (out)

They were out.

  1. 2. Were Ann and Susan at home or at the game on Friday night? (at the game)
  2. 3. Were the girls at the party or at the basketball game on April 1st? (at the party)
  3. 4. Were the Sawyers at the car race or at the horse race on Saturday? (at the car race)
  4. 5. Were you at school or in the office on Tuesday? (in the office)
  5. 6. Were you in the city or in your country house for the weekend? (in our country house)
  6. 7. Were you and your friends at your mother's or at your house on Wednesday? (at my mother's)
  7. 8. Were they at the grocer's or at the butcher's on Thursday morning? (at the grocer's)
