## Usage table

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `may + base verb` | present/future possibility | `She may come with us.` |
| `may have + past participle` | past possibility | `He may have missed the bus.` |
| `may have been + -ing` | past continuous possibility | `He may have been reading.` |
| `may have been + noun/adjective` | past state possibility | `She may have been ill.` |
| `X may ... or X may not. I'm not sure.` | balanced uncertainty answer | `It may rain tomorrow or it may not. I'm not sure.` |
