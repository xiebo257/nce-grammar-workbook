## Usage table

| Pattern | Example from this lesson | Practical reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Positive and negative statements | `They're short. They aren't tall.` | Use the two supplied adjectives in the given order. |
| Demonstratives | `These policemen ...` / `Those doors ...` | Keep `these` and `those` exactly as given by the cue. |
| Plural `be` agreement | `These boxes are heavy.` | Use `are` with plural nouns and `they`. |
| Regular plural nouns | `shop -> shops`, `box -> boxes` | Add `-s` or `-es` according to the noun's spelling. |
| Irregular plural nouns | `child -> children`, `housewife -> housewives` | Learn the complete plural form. |
| Possessive plural | `His children's hands ...` | For an irregular plural, place the apostrophe after the plural `-ren`: `children's`. |
| Adjectives with plural nouns | `The students are hard-working.` | Adjectives do not take plural `-s`. |

## Key takeaway

- Copy every cue accurately: `skirts` is not `shirts`, and `these` is not `those`.
- Check spelling in familiar words such as `they`, `hard-working`, and `shop`.
- Use `are` with plural subjects.
- When a possessive noun is plural, pluralize the owner too: `child's` becomes `children's`.
