NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 47: A cup of coffee

I. Answer the questions according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Do you want any apples? (a pound)

Yes, I do. Give me a pound of apples, please.

  1. b. Do you want any pencils? (three)
  2. c. Do you want any eggs? (twelve)
  3. d. Do you want any glasses? (one)
  4. e. Do you want any boxes? (two)
  5. f. Do you want any chocolate? (a large bar)
  6. g. Do you want any ice creams? (one)

2.

  1. a. Do you want any coffee?

No, I don't. I don't like coffee.

  1. b. Do you want any whisky?
  2. c. Do you want any cheese?
  3. d. Do you want any bread?
  4. e. Do you want any milk?
  5. f. Do you want any honey?
  6. g. Do you want any beer?

II. Answer the questions according to the examples

  1. 1. Do you want a banana? (yes)

Yes, please. I like bananas.

  1. 2. Do you want any butter? (no/cheese)

No, thank you, but I want some cheese.

  1. 3. Do you want a cup of tea? (yes)
  2. 4. Do you want any wine? (no/beer)
  3. 5. Do you want any biscuits? (yes)
  4. 6. Do you want a cup of black coffee? (yes)
  5. 7. Do you want any sugar? (no/honey)
  6. 8. Do you want any water? (no/coffee)
  7. 9. Do you want any fruit? (no/jam)
  8. 10. Do you want a glass of wine? (yes)

III. Answer the questions according to the example

  1. 1. Do you like cheese on your bread? (butter)

No, I don't. But I like butter on my bread.

  1. 2. Do you like cream in your coffee? (milk)
  2. 3. Do you like ice in your whisky? (in beer)
  3. 4. Do you like egg on your bread? (jam)
  4. 5. Do you like honey in your milk? (sugar)
  5. 6. Do you like pure milk in an ice cream? (chocolate)
  6. 7. Do you like sugar in your tea? (lemon)
  7. 8. Do you like jam on your bread? (cheese)
  8. 9. Do you like chocolate in a cake? (fruit)
  9. 10. Do you like sugar in your soup? (salt)
