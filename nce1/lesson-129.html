NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 129: Seventy miles an hour

I. Rewrite the sentences, using "must have been/done" or "can’t have been/done" according to the example.

  1. In my opinion, you didn't see me. I wasn't there.

    You can't have seen me. I wasn't there.

  2. I'm sure you had a good time yesterday. You all looked excited.
  3. The men worked all day without a rest. I am sure they were very tired.
  4. I don't think you did all that by yourself. It was not easy.
  5. That peach tasted unpleasant. I'm sure it was bad.
  6. I'm sure he wasn't there because I didn't see him.
  7. I'm sure you didn't pass the exam. You didn't study for it.
  8. I'm sure that it didn't rain last night. The ground is not wet.
  9. You didn't go anywhere after dinner in the restaurant. I'm sure you left your case there.
  10. I'm sure you ate my biscuits. Why didn't you tell me?

II. Answer the questions, using "had better do" according to the example. You may make changes where necessary.

  1. Do I have to change my shirt?

    Yes, you'd better change your shirt.

  2. Do I have to have a bath?
  3. Does he have to have a haircut?
  4. Do we have to get something to eat?
  5. Do I have to do my homework?
  6. Does she have to go and see a doctor?
  7. Do I have to telephone the teacher?
  8. Does he have to take his son to school?
  9. Do I have to do some shopping?
  10. Does she have to water the flowers?

III. Give advice, using "had better (not) do" according to the examples.

  1. I feel sleepy. (get some sleep)

    You'd better get some sleep.

  2. I've got a bad cold. (not go swimming)

    You'd better not go swimming.

  3. He's got a headache. (take an aspirin)
  4. I'm very tired. (not stay up late)
  5. I'm not feeling well tonight. (not go out)
  6. The bus is crowded. (take a taxi)
  7. She feels hot. (open the windows)
  8. It's raining today. (stay in)
  9. The baby is sleeping. (not turn on the stereo)
  10. She's got a temperature. (not eat too much)
  11. I don't understand the text. (ask your teacher)
  12. He has got a stomachache. (not drink anything)
