NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 62: What's the matter with them?

I. Make sentences according to the example

  1. 1. you/tell the truth/lies

You must always tell the truth. You mustn't tell lies.

  1. 2. I/be early for school/late
  2. 3. we/drink clean water/dirty water
  3. 4. he/put on a clean shirt/dirty one
  4. 5. she/drink milk/beer
  5. 6. you/go out in the fresh air/stay at home
  6. 7. they/speak English/Chinese
  7. 8. we/remember the telephone number/forget it
  8. 9. you/study first/watch television after school
  9. 10. the children/play in the garden/on the banks of a river

II. Give short answers to the questions according to the examples

  1. 1. Must a woman have new clothes? (yes)

Yes, she must.

  1. 2. Must I finish this homework before the lesson? (yes)
  2. 3. Must he do all the work today? (yes)
  3. 4. Must you go now? It's only eight o'clock. (yes)
  4. 5. Must I type the letter now? (yes)
  1. 6. Must she stay in bed? (no)

No, she mustn't.

  1. 7. Must they go to bed late? (no)
  2. 8. Must we tell him the news? (no)
  3. 9. Must I walk across the street? (no)
  4. 10. Must he take an aspirin? (no)

III. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. What's the matter with Bob? He looks ill. (have a cold)

He has a cold.

  1. 2. What's the matter with you? (have a bad headache)
  2. 3. What's the matter with our teacher? (feel ill)
  3. 4. What's the matter with your son? (have a temperature)
  4. 5. What's the matter with Susan? (have a bad toothache)
  5. 6. What's the matter with them? (have mumps)
  6. 7. What's the matter with you? (have a stomachache)
  7. 8. What's the matter with the baby? (be thirsty)
  8. 9. What's the matter with John? (have measles)
  9. 10. What's the matter with your sister? (have an earache)
