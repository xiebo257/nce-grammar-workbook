NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 18: What are their jobs?

I. Fill in the blanks with possessive adjectives

Use my, your, his, her, our, your, or their.

  1. Excuse me. What's ___ name? My name's Sophie.
  2. Michael Baker and Jeremy Short are sales reps, and here are ___ offices.
  3. Where are my cases? ___ cases are here.
  4. She's a hard-working policewoman. What's ___ name?
  5. We are Danish, and ___ friends are Danish, too.
  6. Paul's son is a keyboard operator and ___ daughter is a teacher.
  7. This is Anna and this is Louise. What are ___ jobs? Anna's a hairdresser and Louise is a nurse.
  8. I'm a sales rep. What's ___ job? I'm an engineer.
  9. Whose is this pen? It isn't ___ pen! My pen is blue.
  10. My name is Paul Smith. This is ___ wife, Stella Smith. And these are ___ children, Dave and Anna.

II. Write the correct questions for the answers according to the examples

Examples: (these men) They are my friends. / They're Customs officers. -> Who are these men? What are their jobs? (this young man) He is John. / He's a keyboard operator. -> Who is this young man? What's his job?

Write the two questions for each cue:

  1. (that girl) She is my sister. / She's an air hostess.
  2. (these two men) They are Dave and Jack. / They're sales reps.
  3. (those people) They are my father and mother. / My father's a mechanic and my mother's a nurse.
  4. (this man and woman) They are Paul and Jane. / Paul's an office assistant and Jane's a hairdresser.
  5. (they) They are my son and daughter. / My son's an engineer and my daughter's a teacher.
  6. (these women) They are my friends. / They're policewomen.
  7. (this man) He is Dave. / He's a milkman.
  8. (they) They are Tom and Tony. / They're postmen.
