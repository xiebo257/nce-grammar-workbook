Grammar practice · 语法练习

I. Fill in the blanks with some, one, or ones

  1. Give me ___ knives and forks, please. Which ___? The ___ on the table.
  2. Give me a magazine, please. Which ___? The big ___ or the small ___? The small ___.
  3. Give us our umbrellas, please. Which ___? The ___ on the floor? Yes, the ___ on the floor.
  4. Give me ___ pens, please. Which ___? The blue ___ or the red ___? Oh, the red ___.
  5. Give me a tie, please. Which ___? A yellow ___ or a green ___? A green ___, please.

II. Complete the dialogues according to the example

Example: Give me some knives, please. Which ones? These or those? (blunt) -> These, please. Those are blunt.

  1. Give us some magazines, please. Which ones? These or those? (very small)
  2. Give me some glasses, please. Which ones? These or those? (dirty)
  3. Give us some cups, please. Which ones? These or those? (old)
  4. Give me some bottles, please. Which ones? These or those? (full)
  5. Give us some pens, please. Which ones? These or those? (old)
  6. Give me some spoons, please. Which ones? These or those? (large)
  7. Give me some plates, please. Which ones? These or those? (small)
  8. Give us some shirts, please. Which ones? These or those? (white)
