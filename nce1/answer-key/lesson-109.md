IV. 2. I made very few mistakes.
You're right. But John made fewer mistakes. And Jane
made the fewest mistakes of all.
3. That restaurant is very bad.
You're right. But the Small Café is worse. And the Sun
Restaurant is the worst of all.
4. She earns very little money.
You're right. But he earns less money. And his brother
earns the least of all.
5. I have a lot of friends.
You're right. But Jenny has more friends. And Robert
has the most of all.
6. Charlotte makes good coffee.
You're right. But my sister makes better coffee. And
my mother makes the best (coffee) of all.
