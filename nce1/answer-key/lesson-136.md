# Answer Key: Lesson 136

**Source:** `nce1/新概念英语语法练习1_按课程索引.pdf`, PDF page(s) 571, 572
**Extraction:** macOS Vision OCR; verify uncertain text against the source scan.

I. 1. He said she might get a better mark than him in the
Maths test.
2. He told her he would have dinner with her one day soon.
3. She said the watch he liked might be very expensive.
4. She told him she would write to him every day.
5. He said he wouldn't play football with them because he
couldn't play the game.
6. He said he was sorry he couldn't make up his mind.
7. He told her he would wait for her until ten o'clock.
8. She said she could get tickets for him and his parents.
Il. 1. He said it might be the most sensational news of the
year.
2. The woman said she would have to do all the housework
that day.
3. He told me he couldn't make a dress, but he could make
a cake.
4. She said she thought he would be there on time.
5. He said he was sure they could win the match.
6. He said he was afraid she might be angry.
7. The shop assistant told me I could buy the trousers for
half the price.
8. She told me she could teach me English.
