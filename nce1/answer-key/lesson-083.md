I. 2. When is he going to have a swim?
He has already had a swim.
When did he have a swim?
He had a swim two days ago.
3. When are you going to have a holiday in the country?
IWe have already had a holiday in the country.
When did you have a holiday in the country?
I/We had a holiday in the country last month.
4. When is she going to have an English lesson?
She has already had an English lesson.
When did she have an English lesson?
She had an English lesson this morning.
5. When are they going to have a meal in the restaurant?
They have already had a meal in the restaurant.
When did they have a meal in the restaurant?
They had a meal in the restaurant last week.
6. When are you going to have lunch?

When did you have lunch?
I/We had lunch at twelve o'clock.
7. When is he going to have a drink?
