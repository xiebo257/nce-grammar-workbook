2. Are the boys sweeping the floor?
Yes, they are.
Are the girls sweeping the floor, too?
No, they aren't. They're cleaning the desks and chairs.
3. Are you watching television?
Yes, we are.
Are your friends watching television, too?
No, they aren't. They're listening to the radio.
4. Are the dogs eating bones?
Yes, they are.
Are the cats eating bones, too?
No, they aren't. They're eating fish.
5. Are the children in this room writing?
Yes, they are.
Are the children in that room writing, too?
No, they aren't. They're crying.
