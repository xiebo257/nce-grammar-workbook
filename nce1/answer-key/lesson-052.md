II. 1. There are four seasons in a year, spring, summer,
autumn and winter, and there are three months in a
season.
2. There are twelve months in a year, and there are thirty
or thirty-one days in a month.
3. There are three hundred and sixty-five days in a year.
4. It's very warm here in spring, but it rains sometimes.
5. It's very hot in summer. The sun shines every day.
6. It's often windy in autumn, and it rains sometimes.
7. It's always cold in winter, and it snows in December and
January.
8. The climate in our city is very pleasant.
