I. 1. b. The man was too old to swim in the sea.
c. She was too ill to go to work yesterday.
d. The little dog was too weak to walk.
e. The news was too good to be true.
f. The boys were too tired to climb the wall.
g. We were too late to catch the train.
h. I was too stupid to understand the maths problem.
2. b. The food was too hot for them to eat.
c. The trousers were too short for him to wear.
d. Her handwriting was too bad for us to read.
e. The work was too hard for them to do.
f. The shelf was too high for me to reach.
g. The questions were too difficult for them to answer.
h. The bag was too big for her to carry.
II. 1. b. The table is small enough for them to move.
c. The boxes are light enough for him to lift.
d. The questions are easy enough for them to under-
stand.
e. The stereo is loud enough for them to hear.
f. The pear is soft enough for the old woman to eat.
g. The juice is sweet enough for you to drink.
h. The wall is low enough for him to climb.
2. b. I'm tired enough to sleep for twenty-four hours.
c. He's thirsty enough to drink four glasses of water.
d. I'm hungry enough to eat five cakes.
e. He's big enough to ride a bike.
f. He's rich enough to buy the house.
g. She's lucky enough to win the prize.
h. He's strong enough to move the bookcase.
