II. 2. He says he's hurt his leg, and (he) will have an X-ray
tomorrow.
3. They say they've got mumps, and they're afraid they
can't go to school next week.
4. She says she's got a headache, and she believes she's got
flu.
5. He says he's just had a car crash, and he knows he must
drive carefully.
6. He says I'd/we'd better go back to the station now, and
he's sure the train will leave soon.
7. She says she can't read her boss's letter, and she's sorry
she hasn't typed it yet.
8. They say they've already worked for two hours, and
they think they need some coffee.
9. He says he's late for the meeting, and he's sorry his
watch is five minutes slow.
10. She says she's never been to Paris, but she hopes she'll
go there next year.
