I. 2. Can Anna make tea?
Yes, she can.
Can she make bread?
No, she can't, but she can make coffee.
Who can make bread?
John can make bread.
3. Can Mike ride a bike?
Yes, he can.
Can he drive a car?
No, he can't, but he can ride a horse.
Who can drive a car?
My father can drive a car.
4. Can they speak Dutch?
Yes, they can.
Can they speak Japanese?
No, they can't; but they can speak Korean.
Who can speak Japanese?
Mary can speak Japanese.
5. Can you see any clouds in the sky?
Yes, I can.
Can you see a bird?
No, I can't, but I can see an aeroplane.
Who can see a bird?
He can see a bird.
6. Can John write his name?
Yes, he can.
Can he write his address?
No, he can't, but he can write his phone number.
Who can write his address?
His brother can write his address.
