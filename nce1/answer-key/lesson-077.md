II. 2. What did Jack do? He saw some nice cars.
Where did he see them? He saw them at the car race.
When did he see them? He saw them four days ago.
3. What did Marie do? She ate two bars of chocolate.
Where did she eat them? She ate them at home.
When did she eat them? She ate them yesterday.
4. What did she do? She typed some letters.
Where did she type them? She typed them in her office.
When did she type them? She typed them yesterday
morning.
5. What did your father do? He painted the bookcase.
Where did he paint it? He painted it in the garage.
When did he paint it? He painted it a week ago.
6. What did you do? We met our dentist.
Where did you meet him? We met him at the bus stop.
When did you meet him? We met him last Tuesday.
7. What did they do? They sold their house.
Where did they sell it? They sold it in London.
When did they sell it? They sold it in 1998.
8. What did he do? He telephoned his brother.
Where did he telephone him? He telephoned him in Bei-
jing.
When did he telephone him? He telephoned him last
month.
III. 2. I must buy some writing paper and an envelope. I want
to write a letter.
3. I must wash my hands. I want to eat my lunch.
4. I must sit down. I want to have a good rest.
5. I must boil some water. I want to have a cup of tea.
6. I must go to the greengrocer's. I want to cook a meal.
7. I must get up early. I want to catch the first bus.
8. I must talk to the salesman. I want to buy a refrigerator.
