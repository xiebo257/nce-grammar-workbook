I. 1. b. I'm writing a letter to my mother. /No, I don't write
home every week, but I sometimes write home.
c. She's cleaning her shoes. /No, she doesn't clean her
shoes every day, but she cleans her shoes every week.
d. She's sweeping the floor. /No, she doesn't often sweep
the floor, but she sometimes sweeps it.
e. They're watching television. No, they don't always
watch television at night, but they sometimes watch it
at night.
2. b. He is taking our son to school. /No, he doesn't. He
usually takes him to school at seven.
c. I am waiting for a bus. /No, I don't. I usually wait for
a bus at seven thirty.
d. They are doing their homework. No, they don't.
They usually do their homework in the evening.
e. He is typing letters. /No, he doesn't. He usually types
letters at nine in the morning.
