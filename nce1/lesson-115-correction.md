## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Practical reminder |

## Key takeaway

- `something/anything/nothing` refer to things; `someone/anyone/no one` refer to people.
- `Everything` and `everybody` are not interchangeable.
- In the watch dialogue, use `nowhere` for the final conclusion that the watch cannot be found in any place.
