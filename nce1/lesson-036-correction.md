## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| `some` (affirmative) | `There are some old men in the park.` | Use in affirmative statements for an unspecified quantity. |
| `any` (question / negation) | `Do you know any people?` / `There isn't any cheese.` | Use in questions and negative statements. |
| `some` as pronoun | `Some are standing under trees; some are sitting on chairs.` | `Some` can stand alone as a pronoun, replacing the noun. Capitalize when it starts a sentence. |
| `beside` (next to) | `sit down beside me` | Position immediately next to someone/something. |
| `between` (in the middle of two) | `between two big hills` / `between my father and my mother` | Used when something is in the middle of **two** things or people. |
| `on` (surface) | `on the grass` / `on your desk` / `on your bed` | Contact with a surface. Also: `on the left/right` (fixed phrase). |
| `along` (following the length) | `along the street` | Movement parallel to the length of something. |
| `out of` (exiting) | `coming out of the shop` | Movement from inside to outside. |
