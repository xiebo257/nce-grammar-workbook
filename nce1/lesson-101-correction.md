## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Practical reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Optional `that` | `He says (that) he feels hot.` | After common reporting verbs and expressions, `that` may often be omitted without changing the meaning. |
| Third-person singular | `she says`, `he believes`, `she hopes` | Add `-s` or `-es`, and check the complete spelling of the verb. |
| Present perfect | `he's hurt`, `they've worked` | Use `has/have + past participle`; `hurt` has the same base, past, and participle forms. |
| Modal plus base verb | `he'll have an X-ray` | After `will`, `can`, and `must`, use the base form: `have`, not `has`. |
| Singular pronoun shift | direct `I/my` -> reported `he/his` or `she/her` | Match pronouns and possessives to the named speaker. |
| Plural pronoun shift | direct `we` -> reported `they` | When `they` are the speakers, change all related first-person plural forms consistently. |
| Listener viewpoint | direct `you'd better` -> reported `I'd better` | If the reporter is the person addressed, quoted `you` becomes reported `I`. |
| Compound report | `She says ..., and she's afraid ...` | Preserve both original statements and join them with the appropriate conjunction. |
