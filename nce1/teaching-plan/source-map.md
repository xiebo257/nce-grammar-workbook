The map uses filenames inside that archive, not the unsorted archive-entry order. The main lesson images were checked through contact sheets and selected full-size views, with OCR used to aid identification and checking. OCR is imperfect on curved, rotated, or partially cropped pages; it is not used here as a verified verbatim transcription. The plan paraphrases objectives and adds original activities rather than claiming to reproduce teacher-book lesson notes.

The archive was inspected in temporary working storage; large source images have not been duplicated in the repository. Extract the named photograph from the supplied ZIP when preparing a particular lesson. The main page and accompanying page should be consulted together. The supplementary workbook photograph is separate from the existing HTML workbook; their exact wording need not match.

## Teaching-method references

[Preface](../preface.html), numbered sections/pages as displayed in that HTML:

- 1–5: performance, sentence patterns, four skills, graded presentation, drills, context, visual aids, and natural language.
- 7–8: paired teaching units, division between dialogue and extension work, recordings, and teacher-book support.
- 9–11: nine steps for presenting a text and the suggested presentation time.
- 11–12: answering questions, asking questions, activities, and reconstruction from pictures.
- 12–14: repetition, pattern drills, writing, prepared dictation from Teaching Unit 17, and home practice.

The three-stage recorded drill described in the preface is stimulus → learner response → correct response. Actual recordings are not included in this ZIP. No listening or pronunciation result can be inferred from these images.
