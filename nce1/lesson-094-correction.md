## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Practical reminder |
|---|---|---|
| First-person future answer | `What will you do tomorrow?` -> `I shall wash my clothes.` | The workbook models `shall` with `I`; `I will`/`I'll` is also a natural equivalent. |
| Third-person future answer | `What will Jane do?` -> `She'll move into a new flat.` | Replace a named person or group with the matching pronoun: Jane -> `she`, friends/students -> `they`. |
| Future contraction | `We'll travel`, `They'll fly`, `I'll watch` | `-'ll` is the contraction of `will`; keep the main verb in its base form. |
| Coordinated future actions | `I'll stay at home and have a rest.` | Include every action joined by `and`; do not drop the second cue. |
| `will` question formation | `You will buy me envelopes.` -> `Will you buy me envelopes?` | Move `will` before the subject and keep the main verb in base form. |
| `shall` question formation | `I shall meet you.` -> `Shall I meet you?` | With `I/we`, move `shall` before the subject: `Shall I ...?`, `Shall we ...?`. |
| Pronoun and object preservation | `They will go there.` -> `Will they go there?` | Keep objects and place phrases such as `me`, `her`, `them`, `there`, and `about it`. |
| Phrasal verbs in questions | `We shall climb up the hill.` -> `Shall we climb up the hill?` | Keep the particle with the verb: `climb up`, `move into`, `come and see`. |
