## Usage table

| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `is/are + past participle` | present passive | `The blackboard is cleaned after every lesson.` |
| `was/were + past participle` | past passive | `The desks and chairs were cleaned before the lesson.` |
| `subject + was/were + past participle` | single passive | `He was invited to a party.` |
| `subject + was/were + participle and participle` | reduced double passive | `The money was found and given to the policeman.` |

## Key takeaway

- Active → passive conversion: move the object to subject position, change the verb to `be + past participle`, and match the tense of the original active verb.
- **Determine tense from the active verb form**: `cleans` → present → `is cleaned`; `cleaned` → past → `was cleaned`. For irregular verbs like `put` (same form in present and past), check the third-person singular: if the original is `puts`, it's present; if it's `put` (without `-s`), it's past.
- Item 29: `Someone put` (no `-s`) = past tense → passive should be `was put`.
