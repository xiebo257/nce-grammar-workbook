Lesson 45: The boss's letter

I. Give short answers to the questions according to the examples

1.

  1. a. Can a dog swim?

Yes, it can.

  1. b. Can you bring me some books?
  2. c. Can we fly to Beijing?
  3. d. Can she write English?
  4. e. Can he cook?
  5. f. Can you see China on the map?
  6. g. Can Bob go to the United States?
  7. h. Can I turn off the television?
  8. i. Can Linda play football?
  9. j. Can you read English?

2.

  1. a. Can a cat swim?

No, it can't.

  1. b. Can we eat grass?
  2. c. Can birds swim under water?
  3. d. Can you buy a car now?
  4. e. Can your mother make cakes?
  5. f. Can your son cook?
  6. g. Can we make a cupboard?
  7. h. Can you answer the questions?
  8. i. Can he type?
  9. j. Can she dance?

3.

  1. a. Who can cut my hair? (the hairdresser)

The hairdresser can.

  1. b. Who can speak Korean? (I)
  2. c. Who can lift this stereo? (my brother)
  3. d. Who can read Japanese? (David)
  4. e. Who can speak German? (his friends)
  5. f. Who can translate this? (Xiao Li)
  6. g. Who can jump across the river? (he)
  7. h. Who can make biscuits? (her mother)
  8. i. Who can answer this question? (you)
  9. j. Who can paint the walls for us? (the men)

II. Write the sentences in a more polite way, using Can you ...? according to the example

  1. 1. Shut the windows!

Can you shut the windows, please?

  1. 2. Open the door!
  2. 3. Type these letters!
  3. 4. Turn off the light!
  4. 5. Make some cakes!
  5. 6. Give me the teapot!
  6. 7. Show me the letter!
  7. 8. Turn on the tap!
  8. 9. Wash the dishes!
  9. 10. Take off your shoes!
