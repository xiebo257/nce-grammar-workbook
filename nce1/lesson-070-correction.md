### Detailed Explanations

**1. Ordinal suffix — `1th` → `1st` (4 errors, Lesson 66)**
The ordinal of 1 is "first" → suffix `-st`, not `-th`. The suffixes are: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, then 4th–20th, 21st, 22nd, 23rd. The learner wrote `1th` consistently across four dates with day = 1 (October 1st, January 1st, May 1st, June 1st). **Rule: 1→1st, 2→2nd, 3→3rd, all others→-th. Only 1, 2, 3 (and 21, 22, 23) take non-`-th` suffixes.**

**2. Spelling — `fourty`→`forty` and `Novemver`→`November` (4 errors, Lesson 66)**
- `fourty` → `forty`: The number 40 drops the `u` from "four." The word is f-o-r-t-y, not f-o-u-r-t-y. This error appeared in 3 items (Q12, Q14, Q16).
- `Novemver` → `November`: The `b` was typed as `v`: N-o-v-e-m-**b**-e-r → N-o-v-e-m-**v**-e-r. One occurrence (Q9).
**Rule: "four" has a `u`, but "forty" does not. "November" has a `b`, not a `v`.**

**3. Time conversion — past vs to (1 error, Lesson 66)**
"a quarter **past** one" = 1:15 (15 minutes after 1:00). The learner answered 12:45, confusing "past" (after) with "to" (before). "A quarter **to** one" = 12:45. **Rule: past = after the hour (add minutes); to = before the next hour (subtract from 60).**

**4. Pronoun gender (2 errors, Lessons 67–68)**
- L67 Q28: Jill (female name) → `he` should be `she`.
- L68 Q4: Sam (male name in NCE) → `she` should be `he`.
Notably, the learner correctly identifies gender for most names — these are isolated slips. The learner even fixed a previous error: `the old woman` → `she` (L68 Q7), which was wrong in L54 Q19 (`he`).

**5. Preposition — `at` vs `on` for "Christmas Day" (1 error, Lesson 69)**
"Christmas Day" (December 25th) is a specific calendar date → takes `on`, not `at`. `at Christmas` (without "Day") = the holiday period; `on Christmas Day` = the specific date. **Rule: any expression with "Day" as a date takes `on`: on Christmas Day, on New Year's Day, on Children's Day.**
