# NCE Grammar Practice 1 - Lesson 136: He said (that) he ... He told me (that) he ...

Exported: 2026/9/23 20:34:59

## Answers

### Question 1

**Prompt:** "You may get a better mark than me in the Maths test," he said to her.

**Your answer:**

He said she might get a better mark than him in the Maths test.

### Question 2

**Prompt:** "I will have dinner with you one day soon," he told her.

**Your answer:**

He told her he would have dinner with her one day soon.

### Question 3

**Prompt:** "The watch you like may be very expensive," she said to him.

**Your answer:**

She said the watch he liked might be very expensive.
