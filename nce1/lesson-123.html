NCE Grammar Practice 1

Lesson 123: A trip to Australia

I. Combine each pair of sentences according to the example.

  1. These are the boxes. /We keep the papers in them.

    These are the boxes we keep the papers in.

  2. The chair broke. He was sitting on it.
  3. Show me the book. You got the book from the library.
  4. The books are on the top shelf. /You were looking for them.
  5. The fruit is very fresh./I bought the fruit in that greengrocer's.
  6. The knife slipped and cut his hand. /He was using the knife.
  7. The window was still open./The thief had climbed through the window.
  8. The furniture was very dusty. /She was cleaning it.
  9. The dress was green. /She put on the dress.
  10. The book was very interesting. He read the book last week.

II. Combine each pair of sentences according to the examples.

  1. I know the policeman./The policeman is speaking to John.

    I know the policeman who is speaking to John.

  2. The girl goes to my school. /I greeted the girl.

    The girl I greeted goes to my school.

  3. The tourists are from Japan./The tourists have already left.
  4. That is the lady. /I saw her on television last night.
  5. The man was very old. /I helped the man.
  6. The man is sitting in that car. /The man is my boss.
  7. The person knows my teacher. /I met the person in Beijing.
  8. The man is coming towards us. /You are looking for the man.
  9. The man came to thank you. /You helped the man.
  10. The man is going into that building. We want to see the man.
