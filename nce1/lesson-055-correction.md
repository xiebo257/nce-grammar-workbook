## Usage table

| Pattern | Example | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| Zero article: fixed phrases | `go to work`, `at home`, `at night`, `watch television` | Common fixed phrases with no article: `go to work/school/bed`, `at home/night`, `watch television`. |
| `the` + fixed time phrases | `in the morning/afternoon/evening`, `the North/East` | Parts of the day and compass directions take `the`. |
| Zero article: uncountable nouns (general) | `eat lamb`, `drink milk/water`, `like beef` | Uncountable nouns in general reference take no article. |
| `the` + specific reference | `the office`, `the climate`, `the small one` | Specific or previously mentioned items take `the`. |
| `the` + family name (plural) | `The Sawyers`, `The Smiths` | Family names in plural take `the`: "The Sawyers usually watch television." |
| `the` + fixed expressions | `tell the truth` | Some fixed expressions require `the`: "tell the truth." |
| Third-person singular `-s`/`-es` | `wakes`, `washes`, `goes`, `watches`, `does`, `is` | Present simple with `he`/`she`/`it`/[name]: add `-s` to most verbs, `-es` after `-ch`/`-sh`/`-o`. `be` → `is` (singular), `are` (plural). |
