Lesson 63: Thank you, doctor

II. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the examples

  1. 1. How long must he stay in bed? (a week)

He must stay in bed for a week.

  1. 2. How long must I take the medicine? (three days)
  2. 3. How long must you play the piano? (about an hour every day)
  3. 4. How long must she work? (eight hours every day)
  4. 5. How long must I stay in hospital? (about two weeks)
  1. 6. How long can I use your bike? (two weeks)

You can use my bike for two weeks.

  1. 7. How long can you live here? (about three months)
  2. 8. How long can he stay with his grandmother? (about a month)
  3. 9. How long can students keep books from the library? (two months)
  4. 10. How long can Mary play in the garden? (about two hours)

III. Answer the questions, using the cues given according to the example

  1. 1. Why mustn't he open the window? (have a bad cold)

Because he has a bad cold.

  1. 2. Why mustn't she go to school today? (have measles)
  2. 3. Why mustn't I swim in the river? (have an earache)
  3. 4. Why mustn't he eat an ice cream? (have a toothache)
  4. 5. Why mustn't I take off my coat? (have flu)
  5. 6. Why mustn't they go out? (have mumps)
  6. 7. Why mustn't he get up? (have a temperature)
  7. 8. Why mustn't she drink cold water? (have a stomachache)
  8. 9. Why mustn't he smoke? (have a bad cough)
  9. 10. Why mustn't she read a book? (have a headache)
