Lesson 44: Through the forest

穿过森林 · Practice & Progress

Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Join the sentences using so…that… or such…that….

用 so…that… 或 such…that… 连接句子。

Examples:
a) He was late for the school bus. The school bus left ten minutes ago without waiting for him.
He was so late for the school bus that it left ten minutes ago without waiting for him.
b) He is an experienced taxi driver. He knows every street in London.
He is such an experienced taxi driver that he knows every street in London.

  1. I. so…that / such…that — He is very lazy. He always gets poor marks for his homework.
  2. I. so…that / such…that — The bell around the cat's neck is very loud. It frightens the birds away when it moves.
  3. I. so…that / such…that — She is a very strong woman. People often mistake her for a man.
  4. I. so…that / such…that — He knocked at the door very noisily. He woke everyone in the building.
  5. I. so…that / such…that — They are very nice people. You never forget them.
  6. I. so…that / such…that — It is a very beautiful night. It reminds me of the wonderful time we had in Italy.
  7. I. so…that / such…that — He is very healthy. He always looks young and energetic.
  8. I. so…that / such…that — It is an expensive hobby. You'll have to spend a lot of money on it.
  9. I. so…that / such…that — He was given a hostile reception in that country. He swore never to go there again.
  10. I. so…that / such…that — His mother's death was a blow. The boy never spoke.

II. Fill in the blanks with when or while.

用 when 或 while 填空。

  1. II. when / while — he got into the car, he put the key into the ignition.
  2. II. when / while — the motor was warming up, he cleaned the car.
  3. II. when / while — everything was ready, he drove off.
  4. II. when / while — he turned the corner, he saw a traffic light ahead.
  5. II. when / while — he got to the traffic light, it turned red.
  6. II. when / while — he saw the red light, he stopped his car.
  7. II. when / while — he was waiting for the light to change, he took out his newspaper and started to read.
  8. II. when / while — he was reading, the light turned to green.
  9. II. when / while — he was about to move, the cars behind him started to blow their horns. He was confused, so he stepped on the brake.
  10. II. when / while — He got very angry he made the mistake.
  11. II. when / while — He was only a few minutes away from his office he got caught in a traffic jam.

III. Write sentences with the words, using the gerund.

用动名词形式完成下列句子。

Example: this strap, need, mend
This strap needs mending.

  1. III. Gerund — the floor, want, mop
  2. III. Gerund — the boy, need, encourage
  3. III. Gerund — I, try to avoid, meet him
  4. III. Gerund — he, couldn't help, laugh
  5. III. Gerund — I, enjoy, take a walk, after supper
  6. III. Gerund — he, go, swim, every weekend
  7. III. Gerund — they, don't remember, meet, each other, before
  8. III. Gerund — I, can't imagine, live, with such a man.
  9. III. Gerund — she, suggest, apologise, to the client.
  10. III. Gerund — the boy, resent, have to admit, his mistake
