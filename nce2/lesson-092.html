Lesson 92: Asking for trouble

自找麻烦

Grammar practice

Exercise the grammar pattern in the lesson by completing the items below. Answers are saved automatically in your browser and can be downloaded as a Markdown file.

I. Rewrite the second of each pair of sentences with So… or Nor/Neither…

用 So… 或 Nor/Neither… 改写下列句子的第二句。

  1. 1
    I. So / Nor / Neither — He is a responsible man. I am, too.
  2. 2
    I. So / Nor / Neither — Sally is a careful girl. Susan is a careful girl, too.
  3. 3
    I. So / Nor / Neither — Lily likes to go skiing. Jim likes to go skiing, too.
  4. 4
    I. So / Nor / Neither — Sally has never been abroad. Peter hasn't either.
  5. 5
    I. So / Nor / Neither — I don't like people smoking in public. My wife doesn't either.
  6. 6
    I. So / Nor / Neither — Tom will go to the south to enjoy the sunshine. Peter will, too.
  7. 7
    I. So / Nor / Neither — We shall never forget the shameful past. Our children will not either.
  8. 8
    I. So / Nor / Neither — He is doing his homework. His little sister is, too.
  • 9
    I. So / Nor / Neither — They have discovered the secret. I have, too.
  • 10
    I. So / Nor / Neither — I've never heard from him since. My husband hasn't, either.
