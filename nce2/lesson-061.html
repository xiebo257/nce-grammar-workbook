Lesson 61: Trouble with the Hubble

哈勃望远镜的困境

Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Join each pair of sentences with the word because.

用 because 连接下列句子。

Example: The pictures from the Hubble telescope were disappointing. Its main mirror was faulty.
The pictures from the Hubble telescope were disappointing because its main mirror was faulty.

  1. I. because — He failed the exam. He didn't work hard enough.
  2. I. because — He didn't hear the alarm. He overslept.
  3. I. because — He must be really sick. He hasn't come in to work.
  4. I. because — Peter looked unhappy. He had been scolded by his mother.
  5. I. because — I can't reach the book. It is too far away.
  6. I. because — No one noticed him. They were engrossed in the opera.
  7. I. because — She turned down the music. Her neighbour complained.
  8. I. because — We can't see what's happening over there. It's too far away.
  9. I. because — The space shuttle can only carry two astronauts. Space is limited.
  10. I. because — She failed the literature exam. She had studied the wrong books.

II. Join each pair of sentences beginning with By the time…

连接下列句子，以 By the time… 开头。

Example: The Hubble's eagle eye will send us thousands of pictures. You will read this.
By the time you read this, the Hubble's eagle eye will have sent us thousands of pictures.

  1. II. By the time… — I will leave for New York. You will read this note.
  2. II. By the time… — The children will grow up. You will see them again.
  3. II. By the time… — The meeting started. I entered the room.
  4. II. By the time… — The robbers escaped. The police arrived.
  5. II. By the time… — His mother will put his breakfast on the table. He will go downstairs.
  6. II. By the time… — The other guests will eat everything. You will get here.
  7. II. By the time… — He was in hospital for two days. He regained consciousness.
  8. II. By the time… — All the other runners will go home. He will finish the race.
  9. II. By the time… — The concert finished. She got to the theatre.
  10. II. By the time… — We will all learn something useful. The lesson will finish.
