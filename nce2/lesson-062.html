Lesson 62: After the fire

大火之后

Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Join each pair of sentences with the word or words in brackets.

用括号中的词连接句子。

Example: It caught fire. Great trees covered the countryside. (before)
Before it caught fire, great trees had covered the countryside.

  1. I. Join with given words — They bought the flowers for the singer. Her plane landed. (before)
  2. I. Join with given words — He did a lot of exercises. His mother got up. (before)
  3. I. Join with given words — He typed the report. He read it twice to check it. (after)
  4. I. Join with given words — Mary wrote the letter. She posted it on her way to the office. (after)
  5. I. Join with given words — The old man died. He wrote letters to all his relatives. (before)
  6. I. Join with given words — He got to the bookshop. It was closed. (by the time)
  7. I. Join with given words — He graduated from college. His brother worked on a local newspaper for two years. (when)
  8. I. Join with given words — The food was all eaten. They finally reached the table. (when)
  9. I. Join with given words — He didn't think of meeting her. He set out for the supermarket. (when)
  10. I. Join with given words — He managed to get out his camera. The sun set. (by the time)

II. Combine the sentences with not only…but also…

用 not only…but also… 合并句子。

  1. II. not only…but also… — Peter has a car. He also has a motorbike.
  2. II. not only…but also… — He stood still. He spoke clearly.
  3. II. not only…but also… — He felt tired. He also felt depressed.
  4. II. not only…but also… — They brought home a lot of food. They brought home a lot of daily necessities.
  5. II. not only…but also… — He resigned because he was tired of working. He resigned because he was too old to work.
  6. II. not only…but also… — He was destroyed. He was defeated.
  7. II. not only…but also… — Mary thought a lot about what to say. Mary thought a lot about what to do.
  8. II. not only…but also… — The heavy rain washed away the soil. It caused serious floods.
  9. II. not only…but also… — The family gave him a room. They helped him to get a job.
  10. II. not only…but also… — "You will be successful. You will be famous one day, too."

III. Join each pair of sentences using the past perfect continuous tense and the conjunction before.

用连词 before 和过去完成进行时连接下列句子。

Example: They fought the fire for weeks. Then they got it under control.
They had been fighting the fire for weeks before they got it under control.

  1. III. Past perfect continuous + before — He sold cars for ten years. Then he decided to go to college.
  2. III. Past perfect continuous + before — He worked in the factory for five years. Then he decided to quit.
  3. III. Past perfect continuous + before — My father worked in the garden for hours. Then he came in for supper.
  4. III. Past perfect continuous + before — She talked about leaving the village for a year or two. Then she finally applied for a job in the city.
  5. III. Past perfect continuous + before — I waited for the letter for days. Then it finally arrived.
  6. III. Past perfect continuous + before — She lived in the town for 30 years. Then she decided to move away.
  7. III. Past perfect continuous + before — They tried to put the fire out for 48 hours. Then they finally managed it.
  8. III. Past perfect continuous + before — The police looked for the criminal for months. Then they finally caught him.
  9. III. Past perfect continuous + before — She studied the language for a long time. Then she went to the country.
  10. III. Past perfect continuous + before — My brother played the piano for ages. Then he finally got a job with a band.
