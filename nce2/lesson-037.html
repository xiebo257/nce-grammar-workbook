Lesson 37: The Olympic Games

Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Make sentences with the future tense in the passive.

用将来时的被动语态造句。

Example: all new students/give/textbooks and folders
All new students will be given textbooks and folders.

  1. I. Future passive — the results of the entrance exam/announce/within ten days
  2. I. Future passive — the runners in the race tomorrow/offer/cold drinks every mile
  3. I. Future passive — breakfast/serve/between 8:00 and 10:00
  4. I. Future passive — a new stadium/build/by the end of next year
  5. I. Future passive — the meeting/hold/in the new community centre
  6. I. Future passive — the new motorway/complete/by next month
  7. I. Future passive — the concert/organise/by the music teacher
  8. I. Future passive — the broken window/replace/by the maintenance staff
  9. I. Future passive — you/pay/extra for working overtime every day this week
  10. I. Future passive — you/give/your new timetable next week

II. Make sentences using the future perfect tense.

用将来完成时造句。

Example: they/finish/work on the new stadium/by the end of next year
They will have finished work on the new stadium by the end of next year.

  1. II. Future perfect — he/write/the report/by the time he leaves work
  2. II. Future perfect — she/type/all the letters/before her boss returns later
  3. II. Future perfect — they/decorate/the outside of their house/long before winter arrives
  4. II. Future perfect — I/re-read/all the books/by the time I take the exam
  5. II. Future perfect — we/have/supper/and wash up/by eight o'clock

III. Rewrite the following sentences using a personal pronoun as the direct object.

用人称代词作直接宾语改写下列句子。

Example: I turned off the lights before going to bed.
I turned them off before going to bed.

  1. III. Pronoun object — He picked up the paper from the ground.
  2. III. Pronoun object — They put the jackets on before going out.
  3. III. Pronoun object — He made the story up.
  4. III. Pronoun object — She woke the children up early.
  5. III. Pronoun object — We picked out our presents.

IV. Answer the following questions using the words in brackets.

用括号中的词回答下列问题。

Example: What do many students hope they'll be doing during the Olympics? (work as interpreters)
They hope they'll be working as interpreters.

  1. IV. Answer questions — What does your son believe he'll be doing when he's 18 years old? (study at the best university of the world)
  2. IV. Answer questions — What does your husband think you'll be doing at six this evening? (cook a big supper)
  3. IV. Answer questions — What do you think she'll be doing if we visit her without warning? (read in her study or chat to her friend on the phone)
  4. IV. Answer questions — What do you think they'll be doing at this time tomorrow? (enjoy their holiday in Hawaii)
  5. IV. Answer questions — What do you think John will be doing on Saturday? (visit his parents as usual)
