Lesson 7: Too late

为时太晚 · Practice & Progress

Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Join the following sentences using the past continuous tense and the word in brackets.

仿照例句用过去进行时和括号中的词连接下列句子。

Example · 例句
They waited on the airfield. Then the plane arrived. (when)
They were waiting on the airfield when the plane arrived.

  1. I. Past continuous — We expected a letter from Mary. Mary came to visit us. (when)
  2. I. Past continuous — Two detectives kept guard at the door. Then two others opened the parcel. (when)
  3. I. Past continuous — I phoned for the doctor from a phone-box. My wife was helping the old man. (while)
  4. I. Past continuous — The secretary wrote a letter to a client. The boss called her. (when)
  5. I. Past continuous — The firemen hurried in the direction of the fire. Then we heard the alarm. (when)
  6. I. Past continuous — The girl practised the violin at home. We enjoyed ourselves outside. (while)
  7. I. Past continuous — The lazy boy lay in bed. His sister was doing morning exercises outside. (while)
  8. I. Past continuous — The beautiful young lady wore a blue hat. I saw her at the party. (when)
  9. I. Past continuous — He had a nap at one o'clock. We went back to work. (when)
  10. I. Past continuous — I took a walk with my friend. We met our old teacher. (when)

II. Put the verbs in brackets into the past simple or past continuous tense.

将括号中的动词改写成一般过去时或过去进行时。

  1. II. Past simple/continuous — I lit the fire at 6:00 and it (burn) brightly when my wife came back at 9:00.
  2. II. Past simple/continuous — He (have) a bath when the phone rang. Very unwillingly he (get) out of the bath and (go) to answer it.
  3. II. Past simple/continuous — My dog (walk) along quietly when the boy attacked him.
  4. II. Past simple/continuous — The burglar (open) the safe when he (hear) footsteps. He immediately (turn) off his torch and (crawl) under the bed.
  5. II. Past simple/continuous — You looked very busy when I saw you last night. What you (do)?
  6. II. Past simple/continuous — He (play) the guitar outside her house when someone opened the window and (throw) a bucket of water over him.
  7. II. Past simple/continuous — I looked over the wall and (see) my neighbour. He (plant) cabbages and (be) too busy to speak to me.
  8. II. Past simple/continuous — While the guests (dance) thieves (break) into the house and (steal) a lot of fur coats.
  9. II. Past simple/continuous — When I (hear) the knock I (go) to the door and (open) it, but I (not recognise) him at first because I (not wear) my glasses.
  10. II. Past simple/continuous — He (clean) his gun when it accidentally (go) off and (kill) him.

III. Rewrite the following sentences according to the example.

仿照例句改写下列句子。

Example · 例句
He gave back all the books he had borrowed.
He gave all the books back.
He gave them back.

  1. III. Rewrite — I can't make out the postmark on this letter.
  2. III. Rewrite — You must throw away all those old magazines.
  3. III. Rewrite — I am sure you made up the story so I don't believe it.
  4. III. Rewrite — They put up a statue of Florence Nightingale after her death.
  5. III. Rewrite — I asked him to put out the lights if he was going to be the last to leave.
  6. III. Rewrite — After his fourth attempt he gave up trying to pass the examination.
  7. III. Rewrite — I could always put up the boy if he can't find a room in a hotel.
  8. III. Rewrite — There was a man giving out leaflets outside the church.
  9. III. Rewrite — Put on the blue dress. Blue suits you.
