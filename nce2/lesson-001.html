I. Rewrite the following sentences with the words given in brackets, using the past tense.

用括号中所给的词将下列句子改写成一般过去时。

Example · 例句
I usually go to school at half past seven. (last week)
I went to school at half past seven last week.

  1. I. Past tense — I buy a newspaper every morning. (yesterday afternoon)
  2. I. Past tense — He gets to work at ten to eight every morning. (yesterday morning)
  3. I. Past tense — We talk to each other in English. (in yesterday's lesson)
  4. I. Past tense — They will go to the seaside this weekend. (last summer)
  5. I. Past tense — The boys in Class 2 usually win the football game. (last Sunday)
  6. I. Past tense — He misses the train and is late for the meeting. (the day before yesterday)
  7. I. Past tense — They usually enjoy the film. (yesterday evening)
  8. I. Past tense — She invites John and me for dinner. (last weekend)
  9. I. Past tense — He has to stay at the office to do some extra work. (last night)
  10. I. Past tense — He breaks the window and has to pay for it. (last month)
