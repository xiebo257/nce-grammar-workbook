Grammar exercises · 语法练习

I. Join the following sentences using the relative pronoun which.

用关系代词 which 连接下列句子。

Example: Workers began to unload a number of wooden boxes.
The wooden boxes contained clothing.
Workers began to unload a number of wooden boxes which contained clothing.

  1. I. which — He sent his son to a primary school. The school is not far from his house.
  2. I. which — The boy is very interested in the book. The book is full of wonderful stories.
  3. I. which — They missed the coach. The coach was to take them to the Lake District in Cumbria.
  4. I. which — I found that old photo album. It used to contain a lot of pictures of the family.
  5. I. which — The bus took them to the railway station. It broke down.
  6. I. which — The hotel room was supposed to have a sea view. It looked onto a building site.
  7. I. which — The plane was delayed five hours. It was due to take off at one o'clock.
  8. I. which — She looks more beautiful in the blue dress. It was bought by her boyfriend.
  9. I. which — The tickets were for a concert. They were given to the girl's parents.
  10. I. which — The smoke came from the top floor of that building. It made us cough.

II. Combine each pair of sentences, using a gerund.

用动名词合并句子。

Example: The man admitted it.
He hid in the box before the plane left London.
The man admitted hiding in the box before the plane left London.

  1. II. Gerund — She has finished it. She prepared lunch for the children.
  2. II. Gerund — We don't mind it. We work in hard conditions.
  3. II. Gerund — They are considering it. They might move away from the area.
  4. II. Gerund — The old lady remembered it. She took her umbrella with her.
  5. II. Gerund — The doctor suggested it. He gave the patient an injection.
  6. II. Gerund — They denied it. They didn't do anything wrong.
  7. II. Gerund — We couldn't stop it. We laughed at his funny jokes.
  8. II. Gerund — They tried to avoid it. They talked about the lost child.
  9. II. Gerund — He loves it. He plays football.
  10. II. Gerund — She cannot endure it any longer. She lives with such a fussy man.

III. Fill in the blanks with the gerund form of the verbs given in brackets according to the meaning.

根据意思用括号中动词的动名词形式填空。

Example: He was surprised at ___ (discover) by one of the workers.
